## Problem

A shopper choosing between two products online has only the shop's own words. Reviews are the thing every storefront is expected to have, and the platform has none: nothing to rate a product, nothing to say what other buyers thought, and no way for a business to answer. Building it carelessly is worse than not building it: fake reviews are illegal to publish in several places (in the European Union the Consumer Rights Directive and Unfair Commercial Practices Directive as amended by Directive (EU) 2019/2161 require a trader that shows reviews to say how it checks they come from people who used the product, and forbid posting false ones), a review is a shopper's personal data (it must be exportable and erasable), and a business must not be able to make its own average look better by deleting the bad ones without a trace, nor a rival's bots wreck it.

## Outcome

- A shopper who has **bought a product from that business and received it** can rate it from one to five and write a review, once per product; they can edit and delete their own.
- The business chooses, once, whether reviews are shown at all (**off until it switches them on**), and whether each review is checked **before** it appears or **after**. A moderator publishes, rejects (with a reason from a list) or hides; every decision is on the record with who and when.
- The business can **reply** publicly to a review; anyone (a shopper or staff) can **report** one, and reports reach a moderation queue.
- Each product and each list tile shows the **average and the count** of published reviews; the product page shows the reviews, newest first, with a line saying how they are checked.
- A review is never shown in another business's shop, in any list, count or search. There is no cross-business aggregate.
- The shopper's part of a review is in their **export** and is **erased** with them.
- Review bombing, a burst of one-star reviews, mass reporting, or one moderator removing many reviews, raises an alert to the owner.

## Already there

- Nothing about reviews or ratings exists in the repository (checked 2026-09-30: no table, event, route or screen). Every pre-condition does: the purchase facts as events, the public product reads, the storefront tenant from the host, the customer identity on an order, the erasure machinery and the alerts inbox (both designed, not built: see Constraints).
- `OrderFulfilled` already names `customerId` and `loginId` and each line's `variantId` (`Events.orderFulfilled`); a till sale names the customer only when one was attached, so an anonymous walk-in produces no eligibility, correctly.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **shopper** who bought (writes, edits, deletes, reports), any **shopper** (reads, reports), the **owner** (settings), the **catalogue or customer-care manager** (moderates, replies), a **complainant or regulator** (the record).
- **Channels:** ONLINE storefront (read, write, report); admin app (moderate, reply, settings). Not the till.
- **Scope:** per business and per **product** (a product's variants share one review). A review is not tied to a store; the shopper's store context is only where they browse.
- **Roles that can write:** the **shopper** on their own review. Settings, moderation and replies need a caller **held to no store** among OWNER and MANAGER (`403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`, the code `BusinessWide.require` answers elsewhere): reviews belong to the product and so to the whole business, not to one store. Reading the queue and history: the same. STOREKEEPER and CASHIER: none. The shopper's own routes need the shopper login (the shared admin filter's open-mutation entries for these POST/PUT/DELETE routes, one change, as [storefront-trust](storefront-trust.md) Decisions record).
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same; a sandbox's reviews never reach structured data (a sandbox is never indexable) and notifications are suppressed (notification-svc's rule).

## Scope

- **In**, in build order (each slice ships and is tested alone):
  1. **Who has bought what (product-svc, from order-svc's events).** product-svc consumes `OrderFulfilled` and the handover events into `purchase_facts` (idempotent on `eventId`, and on `(order, variant)`); it maps a variant to its product from its own tables and keeps only orders that name a customer. **A purchase counts when the shopper has the goods:** a till sale at `OrderFulfilled`; an online order at its handover (`OrderDispatched` for delivery, `OrderCollected` for pickup, the events that already exist), so nobody reviews a parcel still on the road. An order fulfilled and later fully cancelled or refunded does not lose eligibility (a returned product may still be judged; the rating is honest about it), but one that never reached FULFILLED never counts. `GET /catalog/products/{id}/review-eligibility` (shopper) answers `{canReview, reason?, existingReviewId?}` (`NOT_A_BUYER`, `ALREADY_REVIEWED`, `REVIEWS_OFF`). Events may arrive in any order; the fact is the union.
  2. **Write, edit, withdraw; settings; publication; the summary (product-svc).**
     - `review_settings` per business: `enabled` (default **false**), `moderation` (`BEFORE` default once enabled, or `AFTER`), `review_window_days` (nullable = unlimited: how long after purchase a review may be written; a business's own number, off until set), `auto_hide_reports` (nullable = off: the count of open reports at which a published review is hidden pending a decision; the business's own number). `GET/PUT /admin/reviews/settings` (business-wide OWNER/MANAGER; the same `Idempotency-Key`-free replace as other settings, with who and when on the row and each change appended to `review_events` with scope BUSINESS).
     - `POST /catalog/products/{id}/reviews` (shopper, `Idempotency-Key`): `rating` 1 to 5, optional `title` and `body`, `language` (BCP 47, default the shopper's), `displayName` (defaults to their first name only, never surname or address, and they may change it). One review per product per shopper for ever (a unique key on business, product, customer): after a delete the same row is reopened by writing again, never a second. Refused: `403 REVIEW_NOT_A_BUYER`, `409 REVIEW_ALREADY_EXISTS`, `409 REVIEW_WINDOW_CLOSED`, `403 REVIEWS_NOT_ENABLED`, `400 REVIEW_INVALID` (rating out of range, text beyond the platform's technical bounds, a language that is not BCP 47). The status is `PENDING` under `BEFORE`, `PUBLISHED` under `AFTER`.
     - `PUT /catalog/products/{id}/reviews/mine` edits the shopper's own; under `BEFORE` an edited published review goes back to `PENDING` and disappears (with its rating) until a moderator approves the edit, so nothing unchecked is on show; under `AFTER` it stays published and enters the moderation feed as edited. `DELETE …/mine` sets `WITHDRAWN` (nothing is physically removed; see the record).
     - The **summary** `product_rating_summary` (product, count, sum, and a count per star) is kept by the same transaction as every status change and counts `PUBLISHED` only; the average is computed at read (sum over count, not stored, nothing shown below one review).
     - `GET /catalog/products/{id}/reviews?after=&limit=&rating=&language=` (public, storefront tenant from the host, cursor on newest first, default 20 max 100) lists published reviews with the display name, rating, title, body, language, the date (UTC; the app formats), the business's reply, and `verifiedPurchase: true` on every one (there is no other kind). `GET …/rating` answers `{count, average, histogram}`. Both answer `{count: 0}` when reviews are off, and the app then shows nothing.
     - **How reviews are checked, said out loud.** The list response carries `howChecked`: `{verifiedBuyersOnly: true, moderation: BEFORE|AFTER}`, and the app shows one sentence from it ("Only people who bought this product can review it. Reviews are checked before/after they appear."). This is true by construction, is shown always, and is what the law that asks for it (as data: a `legal_obligations` row `CONSUMER_REVIEW_INFORMATION` for the European Union, Directive 2005/29/EC art. 7(6) as inserted by Directive (EU) 2019/2161, added at build with its citation) needs; showing it does not depend on the row.
  3. **Moderation, replies and the record (product-svc, notification-svc).**
     - `GET /admin/reviews?status=&productId=&reported=&after=&limit=` (queue, cursor on the last change); `POST /admin/reviews/{id}/publish`, `/reject`, `/hide`, `/restore` (`Idempotency-Key`, a `reason` from the list `SPAM`, `ABUSIVE`, `OFF_TOPIC`, `PERSONAL_INFORMATION`, `FAKE`, `OTHER` and an optional note; **publish needs none; reject and hide need one**: `400 REVIEW_REASON_REQUIRED`). A review cannot be rejected for its rating: the reasons are about the content, the reason list has no "negative", and the platform's record (below) makes a pattern visible. `409 REVIEW_STATE_INVALID` for a transition that does not exist (a withdrawn review cannot be published).
     - `PUT /admin/reviews/{id}/reply` and `DELETE …/reply`: one reply per review, by staff, shown under it with the business's name (never the person's); text within technical bounds; replying to a review that is not published is `409 REVIEW_STATE_INVALID`.
     - **The record: `review_events` (append-only)**: review, kind (`SUBMITTED`, `EDITED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `PUBLISHED`, `REJECTED`, `HIDDEN`, `RESTORED`, `REPLIED`, `REPLY_REMOVED`, `REPORTED`, `SETTINGS`), actor kind (`SHOPPER` or `STAFF`) and id, reason, note, and for `SUBMITTED`, `EDITED` and `REPLIED` the **text as it then was** (so what a moderator saw and what the shopper wrote can be shown later, even after edits). `GET /admin/reviews/{id}/history` (business-wide management). The review row carries the current state; nothing is ever updated in `review_events`.
     - notification-svc: a moderator notice (`REVIEW_PENDING`, in-app to the business-wide OWNER/MANAGER staff, one digest per period as the alerts inbox does, not one per review); a shopper notice when their review is **rejected or hidden** (`REVIEW_REMOVED`, with the reason in words: it affects them and is the fair-process line) and when the business **replies** (`REVIEW_REPLIED`, in the shopper's notice preferences: a group they may switch off). Events below.
  4. **Reports (product-svc).** `POST /catalog/reviews/{id}/reports` (any signed-in shopper, or staff through the admin route) with a reason from the list and an optional note (`Idempotency-Key`; **one report per person per review**, a repeat answers the first). Reports go to the queue (`reported=true`), never change the review by themselves unless the business set `auto_hide_reports`, in which case reaching that many **distinct** open reporters hides the review pending a decision (a mass-reporting brigade is what the alert in slice 7 sees). A moderator's decision closes the reports on it, with the decision kept. `review_reports` is append-only with a `closed_by_event` pointer set by a **new row** in `review_report_closures`, not an update. A reporter cannot report their own review (`409 REVIEW_OWN_REPORT`).
  5. **The average and count where shoppers look (product-svc, app).** `ProductResponse` and the list rows carry `rating: {count, average}` (absent when reviews are off or none published); `GET /catalog/products?minRating=` filters (a product with no published reviews is excluded only when the filter is given); sorting by rating is **out** (a rating sort under a cursor needs its own ordering key; see Out). The storefront tile shows stars and count; the product page shows the summary, the histogram, the list and the write control (or why it is not offered: "Only people who bought this can review it"). [storefront-trust](storefront-trust.md) slice 5's page for crawlers gets `aggregateRating` in its JSON-LD **only where reviews are on, the count is above zero and the reviews are on the same page** (the rule search engines apply); that is a one-line addition to that slice, recorded here and there.
  6. **Personal data: export and erasure (product-svc, customer-svc).** product-svc declares `SubjectDataSpec` for `product_reviews`, `product_review_events` (shopper-side rows), `review_reports` (reporter) and `purchase_facts`: **export** gives the shopper their reviews, their reports and the fact that purchases were recorded (no other shopper's data); **erasure** on `CustomerErased` removes the review's title, body, display name and customer link (status `ERASED`, dropped from the summary in the same transaction), erases the text stored in its `review_events` rows and anonymises their shopper actor id, anonymises the reporter of their reports, deletes their `purchase_facts`, and publishes `SubjectErasureCompleted` with counts (`storeql.product.subject-erasure-completed`). Staff decisions and the business's replies on an erased review stay as events without the reviewer's identity or words (RETAIN, reason: the business's own record of what it did). **This adds product-svc to the services that hold a customer id, which [WAVE2-BUILD](../target/flow-catalogue/_notes/WAVE2-BUILD.md) §3.3 item 9 expected to be none;** privacy-requests' list of acknowledging services gains product-svc, and the schema test that names undeclared personal columns covers the new tables. A whole business leaving is covered by `TenantDataSpec` as for every table.
  7. **Abuse and alerts (product-svc, exception-alerts).** product-svc evaluates and raises `ExceptionAlertRaised` through the shared `AlertRaiser` for four metrics (rows added to the catalogue on [exception-alerts](exception-alerts.md), owner product-svc, subject business-wide, no store): `reviews.submitted` (reviews written, per product and per shopper login), `reviews.one_star` (one-star reviews, per product: "one star" is the bottom of the scale, not a policy number), `reviews.reports` (distinct reporters on one review, and reports made by one person), `reviews.removal_share` (share of one moderator's decisions that reject or hide, needs `min_sample`; the manipulation case of a business removing its bad reviews). The owner sets the thresholds as any rule; nothing is on by default. The structural defence is the verified-buyer rule: a bot needs a real fulfilled order per review.
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Reviews by people who did not buy.** The row asks for a shopper who bought; unverified reviews are what the law about fake reviews is aimed at. A business that wants questions and answers from the public is a different feature.
  - **Photos, video, "helpful" votes and sorting by rating.** Each is another moderation surface (images need scanning and storage under the plan) or another abuse surface (vote farming); newest first with a star filter is enough and keeps the cursor simple. A rating sort is a later slice that adds its own `(rating, id)` cursor.
  - **Automatic profanity or sentiment filtering.** A word list is a language and a place assumed; moderation is a person's, with a reason.
  - **Asking for a review by email.** A reminder is a marketing message with its own consent purpose ([consent-evidence](consent-evidence.md)); the storefront's order page offers "Write a review" on received items and that is all.
  - **A review syndicated to other businesses or a marketplace, and any cross-business aggregate.** A review is the business's own; product ids are tenant-scoped and no query crosses them.
  - **Translating reviews.** The language is stored and shown; the reader translates.
  - **Verified review as a badge decided by the business.** Every published review is verified; there is nothing to switch.
  - **A second person to approve moderation.** Moderating moves no money, stock or access; the safeguard is the append-only record and the `reviews.removal_share` alert, not an approvals key (none added).
  - **Product Q&A, seller ratings, delivery ratings.** Different subjects.

## Data and flow

- **Owner: product-svc, and why.** A review is about a **product**, and every read of it is a product read: the average on the tile, the count on the page, the `minRating` filter on `GET /catalog/products`, the aggregate in the crawler page. Putting it in customer-svc would make the catalogue's hottest public read call another service per tile (or duplicate the summary by event) and put a catalogue rule (which products can be reviewed, which are live) in a service that has no products; putting it in order-svc would put content moderation in the transaction service. The two facts product-svc lacks come by event, the way inventory-svc learns orders: **who bought what** (`OrderFulfilled` and the handover events) and **who the customer is** (`customerId` in the shopper's token, the display name typed with the review). customer-svc keeps its role as the coordinator of export and erasure; it does not hold the data.
- **Owned by product-svc** (every table has `tenant_id UUID NOT NULL`, first in each composite index):
  - `purchase_facts`: tenant, customer_id, product_id, variant_id, order_id, channel, fulfilled_at, handed_over_at (nullable, filled by the handover event; the one place an update is allowed, and never on a review), unique `(tenant, order_id, variant_id)`; index `(tenant, customer_id, product_id)`.
  - `product_reviews`: id, tenant, product_id, customer_id (null once erased), rating SMALLINT (1 to 5, checked), title, body, language, display_name, status (`PENDING`, `PUBLISHED`, `REJECTED`, `HIDDEN`, `WITHDRAWN`, `ERASED`), verified_order_id (the order that made them eligible, kept for the record), reply_text, reply_by, reply_at, created_at, edited_at, published_at; unique `(tenant, product_id, customer_id)` where the customer is not null; index `(tenant, product_id, status, published_at DESC, id)`; `(tenant, status, updated_at)` for the queue.
  - `product_rating_summary`: tenant, product_id, review_count, rating_sum, and a count per star; maintained on the review's transaction.
  - `product_review_events` (append-only): as slice 3.
  - `review_reports` (append-only) and `review_report_closures` (append-only): as slice 4.
  - `review_settings`: one row per business.
- **Needs from other services:** order-svc's `OrderFulfilled`, `OrderDispatched`, `OrderCollected` (events, existing; `customerId` and each line's variant, verified at build, and `OrderDispatched`/`OrderCollected` are checked for `customerId`: where a handover event lacks it the fact is joined by `orderId` from `OrderFulfilled`, which always comes first because a handover is only allowed after FULFILLED); `CustomerErased` (existing); notification-svc for notices. Nothing is read by SQL from another service.
- **Events published** (each with `eventId` last, through the outbox on the review's transaction): `ProductReviewSubmitted` (`storeql.product.review-submitted`: reviewId, productId, rating, status; consumer notification-svc counts pending reviews for the moderator notice), `ProductReviewModerated` (`storeql.product.review-moderated`: reviewId, productId, customerId, decision, reason code; consumer notification-svc sends `REVIEW_REMOVED` on reject or hide), `ProductReviewReplied` (`storeql.product.review-replied`: reviewId, productId, customerId; consumer notification-svc sends `REVIEW_REPLIED`), `SubjectErasureCompleted` (privacy-requests' shape). Consumers dedupe on `eventId`; reporting-svc consumes none (no reporting is asked for).
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** create a review, publish, reject, hide, restore, report; `PUT` edit and reply are replaces, naturally idempotent. A retry answers the first result.
- **New error codes:** `403 REVIEWS_NOT_ENABLED`, `403 REVIEW_NOT_A_BUYER`, `409 REVIEW_ALREADY_EXISTS`, `409 REVIEW_WINDOW_CLOSED`, `400 REVIEW_INVALID`, `400 REVIEW_REASON_REQUIRED`, `409 REVIEW_STATE_INVALID`, `409 REVIEW_OWN_REPORT`, `404 REVIEW_NOT_FOUND` (also for another business's), `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`, `400 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED`.

## Constraints

- Golden rules 1 (no cross-service reads; purchases arrive as events), 3 (tenant from the JWT on admin and shopper routes, from the storefront host on the public read; `tenant_id` first; the shopper's `customerId` from the token, never the body), 6 and 7 (outbox; consumers dedupe on `eventId`), 8 (`product_review_events`, reports, closures are append-only: a "delete" is a status and an event), 9 and 10 (thin resources, DTOs), 15 (validate at the boundary), 12 (metrics and tracing as every route).
- **Never across businesses:** every query, count and summary is keyed by tenant first; the crawler feed and JSON-LD carry only the storefront's own; a test lists every read route and proves it takes the tenant from context.
- **Location-neutral:** a star scale is not a place; `language` is stored per review (BCP 47) and never assumed; no country's consumer-review rule is in code, the disclosure row is data with a citation and the disclosure line is true and shown everywhere.
- **Privacy:** the display name defaults to a first name only; the shopper's email, surname and address are never in a review response; a moderator sees the same, plus the customer id, and staff who read reviews are not shown more of a shopper than the shop already knows of them at the till.
- **Interlocks:** privacy-requests slice 4 and `SubjectDataSpec` (common-service) must exist before slice 6; the shopper routes need the shared admin filter's open-mutation entries and the gateway's shopper routing (one change, [storefront-trust](storefront-trust.md) Decisions); exception-alerts slice 2 (`AlertRaiser`) before slice 7. Slices 1 to 5 stand without them.
- Existing tenants: nothing appears or changes until the owner enables reviews; `ProductResponse.rating` is absent when off, so no client breaks.
- Flow guards: `flow-guard-comprehensive` (sign-in, orders) stays green: this page adds routes and consumers, changes no order behaviour.

## Acceptance

- [ ] **Eligibility.** A shopper with a fulfilled till sale naming them, or a dispatched or collected online order, for a product may review it; before the handover, an anonymous till sale, another shopper's order, or no purchase is `403 REVIEW_NOT_A_BUYER`; events in any order and a redelivered event give the same facts — `ReviewEligibilityIT.onlyBuyersWhoHaveTheGoods`, `.eventsInAnyOrder`, `PurchaseFactsHandlerTest.*`
- [ ] A refunded or fully returned purchase keeps eligibility; an order that never reached FULFILLED never counts — `ReviewEligibilityIT.aReturnDoesNotRemoveEligibility`
- [ ] **Writing.** One review per product per shopper (`409 REVIEW_ALREADY_EXISTS`); a delete then a write reopens the same row; a retry with the same key answers the first; invalid ratings, text or language `400 REVIEW_INVALID`; reviews off `403 REVIEWS_NOT_ENABLED`; the window, where set, `409 REVIEW_WINDOW_CLOSED` — `ProductReviewIT.oneReviewPerShopper`, `.aRetryAnswersTheFirst`, `.validation`, `.theWindowIsTheBusinessesOwn`
- [ ] The shopper's identity comes from the token: a body naming another customer is ignored or refused — `ProductReviewIT.theBuyerIsTheTokenNotTheBody`
- [ ] **Moderation modes.** Under BEFORE a new review is PENDING and absent from the list and the summary; under AFTER it is PUBLISHED at once; an edit under BEFORE hides it until approved and removes its rating from the summary — `ReviewModerationIT.beforeHidesUntilApproved`, `.afterPublishesAtOnce`, `.anEditIsCheckedAgainBefore`
- [ ] Publish, reject, hide, restore follow the state table; reject and hide need a reason from the list (`400 REVIEW_REASON_REQUIRED`); no reason names the rating; a bad transition is `409 REVIEW_STATE_INVALID` — `ReviewStatesTest.*` (pure), `ReviewModerationIT.decisionsNeedAReason`
- [ ] The summary counts published reviews only, moves with every status change in the same transaction, and never shows an average for none — `RatingSummaryTest.*`, `ReviewModerationIT.theSummaryFollowsTheStatus`
- [ ] **Reply.** One reply per review, shown with the business's name, not the person's; not on an unpublished review; removable — `ReviewReplyIT.*`
- [ ] **Reports.** One per person per review (a repeat answers the first); an own-review report `409 REVIEW_OWN_REPORT`; reports reach the queue and do not change the review, except that `auto_hide_reports`, where set, hides at that many distinct reporters; a decision closes them — `ReviewReportIT.*`
- [ ] **The record.** Every submit, edit, withdrawal, decision, reply, report and settings change is an event with actor and time and, for text, the text as it was; no route edits or deletes an event; the history is readable by business-wide management only — `ReviewRecordIT.theRecordIsAppendOnly`, `ReviewSchemaTest.appendOnly`
- [ ] **Public read.** The list and the rating are the storefront business's own published reviews, newest first with a cursor, `verifiedPurchase` on each and `howChecked` present; reviews off answers `count 0` — `ReviewCatalogIT.publishedOnlyAndNewestFirst`, `.howCheckedIsAlwaysSaid`, `.offMeansNothing`
- [ ] **Isolation.** Another business's staff of every role and shoppers, naming our review, product or customer ids, get 404 (or empty) and nothing moves; a review is never in another business's list, summary or filter; a shopper cannot read the queue or the history (403); a store-held manager is refused settings, moderation and replies `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`; a CASHIER or STOREKEEPER is 403 — `ReviewTenantIsolationIT.*`, `ReviewAuthorizationIT.*`
- [ ] **Tile and filter.** `rating` on the product and list rows only when reviews are on and published exist; `minRating` filters; the crawler JSON-LD has `aggregateRating` only when on, count above zero and reviews on the page — `ReviewCatalogIT.theTileCarriesTheAverage`, `StructuredDataIT.aggregateRatingOnlyWhenShown`
- [ ] **Notices.** The moderators get one digest for pending reviews; the shopper is told of a rejection or a hide with the reason in words, and of a reply where their preferences allow; a sandbox sends nothing; a redelivered event sends once — `ReviewNoticeHandlerTest.*`, `ReviewNoticeIT.*`
