## Problem

A shopper whose parcel arrived without the milk, or with a cracked jar, has one way to say so: phone or write to the shop. The person who answers has nowhere to put it. There is no record that it was raised, nobody who owns it, no note of what was said to the customer, and no way to see which complaints are still open. A manager cannot tell how long a complaint has waited or whether a refund went out because of it. The shopper cannot see what became of what they reported. Staff who deal with orders that are stuck (unpaid too long, waiting for a price, a substitution nobody answered, ready and never collected) have to hunt through the Orders screen with no list of what needs a person today.

## Outcome

- **Any member of staff can open a case in seconds** from a customer, an order or a return, and **the shopper can open one from My Orders or their account**: what went wrong (a fixed list of kinds), how urgent, and in their own words.
- **Every case has an owner, a state, a thread and a trail.** Staff notes are for staff; messages go to the customer, in the business's own words, by the channels the customer chose for what they are told about their account. The customer's view shows the messages and the state, never the notes.
- **A case says what it is about and what was done.** It links the order, return, payment or gift card it concerns, and links what resolved it: a return, a refund, a replacement order, store credit. **The case never moves money or stock itself.** The refund or return is made through the existing screens and rules (limits, approvals, step-up, `sales.refund`) and then linked. There is no second refund path.
- **Time targets are the business's**, off until set: a first reply and a resolution time per priority. A case past its target is flagged and can raise an alert.
- **A shopper sees their own cases, and only theirs.** Their export includes them; their erasure removes the words and keeps an anonymous shell.
- **One list shows the orders that need a person today**: held, stuck and unanswered orders, from the business's own limits, each with "Open a case".

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **shopper** (opens, replies, reads their own), **any staff** (opens a case from the till or the Orders screen), the **case handler** (a manager or anyone whose role holds `cases.handle`: works the queue), the **owner** (sets targets, canned replies, sees the whole business).
- **Channels:** back-office and POS (open; handle in back-office), ONLINE storefront (the shopper's Report a problem, My cases). No inbound email or telephony (see Out).
- **Scope:** per business; a case may name a store (the order's store, else where it was opened). A caller held to stores sees and handles cases at their stores and business-wide cases (no store); never another store's (`403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`, `404` for another business).
- **Roles that can write:** any staff opens a case (`POST /cases`) and sees the ones they opened; `cases.handle` (a new permission, default OWNER and MANAGER, assignable to custom roles through the existing role catalogue in tenant-svc) reads the queue, assigns, replies, resolves, links; OWNER sets targets, canned replies and reopen rules. The shopper (CUSTOMER) opens and replies on their own cases only. PLATFORM_ADMIN sees none of a business's cases unless acting on its behalf through the existing on-behalf mechanism.
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same; notification-svc suppresses the messages (logged `SUPPRESSED`).

## Already there

- Nothing for cases: no table, route or screen (checked 2026-09-30: no case or complaint code in any service; product-svc's "range review" and inventory-svc's food-safety checks are different things).
- The pieces a case *links* to are built: the till and back-office return (`createReturn`, `OrderReturned`), refunds (`POST /payments/refunds`, `sales.refund`), store credit and points by hand (`ManualGrantsIT`), gift cards, recall notices, the substitution and short-close notices. The order states PENDING, AWAITING_PRICE and their sweepers exist; the queue in slice 6 only reads them.
- The shopper's notices and their preferences are [shopper-notices](shopper-notices.md) and [notification-centre](notification-centre.md); a case message is one more notice type in them, not a new mechanism.

## Scope

- **Who may do it, and where (every slice):** as under "Who and where". A case at a store is handled by staff who may act at that store (`requireStoreAccess`); a case with no store needs a caller held to none or a business-wide handler. Settings (targets, canned replies) need OWNER or a business-wide MANAGER (`BusinessWide.require`-style, `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`).
- **In (slices in build order):**
  1. **The case (customer-svc, app).**
     - Open: `category` from a fixed list (MISSING_ITEM, WRONG_ITEM, DAMAGED_ITEM, LATE_OR_NOT_DELIVERED, QUALITY, PRODUCT_SAFETY, BILLING_OR_CHARGE, RETURN_OR_REFUND, SERVICE_COMPLAINT, QUESTION, OTHER), `priority` (LOW, NORMAL, HIGH, URGENT; NORMAL when not said), `subject`, `description`, an optional customer, an optional store, `channel` of origin (APP, PHONE, IN_PERSON, EMAIL, LETTER, OTHER: how it reached the business; staff say it, a shopper's own is APP). A staff-opened case for someone with no customer record carries `contactName` and one of `contactEmail`/`contactPhone` (kept on the case, erased with it, never created as a customer). A `PRODUCT_SAFETY` case shows a marker ("may need a recall check") and offers the existing recall screen; the case itself starts no recall.
     - Reference: a short `Ids.shortRef` the shopper and staff can quote (`CASE-…`).
     - States: OPEN, IN_PROGRESS, WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER, RESOLVED, CLOSED. Moves are explicit calls (start, wait, resolve, close, reopen), each a row in the append-only `case_events` with who, when and why. `resolve` needs a `resolution` (slice 4). CLOSED is final; RESOLVED can be **reopened** by staff, or by the shopper replying within the business's reopen window (`case_settings.reopen_days`, unset = the shopper's reply after RESOLVED opens a new linked case instead).
     - Owner: `assigned_to` (a staff login of the business, checked through `TenantProfiles`/tenant-svc staff list, and who may act at the case's store). `POST /cases/{id}/assign` (a handler) and `.../claim` (take an unassigned case). Assignment changes are events, never edits.
     - Notes: `case_notes` (append-only, staff only, never shown to the shopper), edited never, deleted never.
     - Queue: `GET /cases?status=&priority=&category=&assignedTo=&storeId=&overdue=&customerId=&q=&after=&limit=` (cursor; default 20, max 100), ordered by due time then priority; "mine" and "unassigned" filters; counts by status.
  2. **The shopper's side (customer-svc, app).** `POST /customers/me/cases` (from My Orders for an order of theirs, or from the account): kind, subject, description and an optional order id; the order must be the caller's (asked of order-svc through the reference check below; another shopper's order is `404 CASE_LINK_NOT_FOUND`, never a hint). `GET /customers/me/cases`, `GET /customers/me/cases/{id}` (the thread of messages and the state, no notes, no assignee name: "a member of staff"), `POST /customers/me/cases/{id}/messages` (reply), `POST /customers/me/cases/{id}/close` (the shopper may say "sorted, thanks"). A shopper opens at most as many cases as the business allows open at once (`case_settings.open_per_customer_max`, unset = unlimited) so a form cannot be used to flood a queue (`429 CASE_TOO_MANY_OPEN`). Routes added to the shared filter's opening table for a shopper; a CUSTOMER never reaches `/cases`.
  3. **Messages to the customer (customer-svc, notification-svc).**
     - Staff `POST /cases/{id}/messages {body, channelHint?}` and the shopper's replies land in `case_messages` (append-only, `direction` OUT or IN, author, at). An OUT message publishes `CaseMessageSent`; notification-svc sends it as `CASE_MESSAGE`, a catalogue message whose frame (greeting, case reference, "you can reply in the app", sign-off) the business may reword like any template, with the staff member's words inside it; by the shopper's `ACCOUNT` channel choice ([shopper-notices](shopper-notices.md): email always, push by choice, never SMS). To a customer with no login it is email to the contact held, with no reply link (see Out). This is service correspondence about their own case, not marketing: no `MARKETING` consent is needed, and it is refused to an anonymised customer.
     - **Canned replies:** `case_reply_templates` (per business, per language, a name and a body in the same Mustache subset the template engine uses: customer first name, case reference, order reference, business name; no HTML), written by OWNER or a business-wide handler; a handler picks one, edits it, sends. What is sent is the resulting text, kept on the message.
     - Status notices to the shopper (`CASE_RECEIVED` when they open one, `CASE_RESOLVED` with the resolution in words, `CASE_WAITING_ON_YOU` when it moves to waiting) are catalogue messages in the same group; the business may switch each off (`case_settings.notices`, default: RECEIVED and RESOLVED on, WAITING on).
  4. **What was done (customer-svc, order-svc, payment-svc).**
     - `case_links` (append-only): `kind` (ORDER, RETURN, RETURN_REQUEST, PAYMENT, REFUND, GIFT_CARD, STORE_CREDIT_GRANT, POINTS_GRANT, RECALL, ORDER_REPLACEMENT), `ref_id`, `role` (CONCERNS: what it is about; RESOLUTION: what fixed it), `linked_by`, `at`. `POST /cases/{id}/links {kind, refId, role}` (a handler, `Idempotency-Key`). customer-svc asks the owner through **`GET /internal/references/{kind}/{id}`** (service-to-service; never routed by the gateway; the owner answers only for the caller's business): `{exists, customerId?, orderId?, storeId?, amount?, currency?, at}`; ids and amounts, nothing personal. order-svc answers ORDER, RETURN, RETURN_REQUEST, GIFT_CARD, ORDER_REPLACEMENT; payment-svc PAYMENT and REFUND; customer-svc itself the two grants. Refusals: `404 CASE_LINK_NOT_FOUND` (unknown, or another business's), `409 CASE_LINK_NOT_THE_CUSTOMERS` (the order or return belongs to a different customer than the case's; a case with no customer takes an order and adopts its customer only when a handler confirms).
     - **Doing the fix, without a second path.** The Cases screen offers buttons that **open the existing dialogs pre-filled from the case** (a return on the linked order, a refund, store credit, a replacement order) and, when that dialog completes, link its result to the case with role RESOLUTION. Each dialog keeps its own rules: the return policy and `ORDER_RETURN_NEEDS_MANAGER`, the refund's ceiling and approval (`sales.refund`), store credit by hand needing management, a reason and its `Idempotency-Key` (`customer.store-credit-issue`), gift card issue without a sale (`sales.gift-card-issue`). The case adds one optional field to the two customer-svc hand grants and to the return and refund calls: `caseId`, kept on the grant or return's own record so the trail reads both ways (additive, ignored when absent, verified to be an open case of the business).
     - **Resolving:** `POST /cases/{id}/resolve {resolution, note}` with `resolution` one of REFUNDED, RETURN_ARRANGED, REPLACED, STORE_CREDIT_OR_POINTS_GIVEN, EXPLAINED, NOT_UPHELD, DUPLICATE, WITHDRAWN. The outcomes that say money or goods moved (REFUNDED, RETURN_ARRANGED, REPLACED, STORE_CREDIT_OR_POINTS_GIVEN) need at least one RESOLUTION link, else `409 CASE_RESOLUTION_NEEDS_LINK`, so a case cannot claim a refund that was never made. `NOT_UPHELD` needs a note. `resolve` sends `CASE_RESOLVED` to the shopper unless switched off.
  5. **Time targets (customer-svc, notification-svc via the alert inbox).** `case_settings.first_response_minutes` and `resolution_minutes` **per priority**, each null until the business sets it; nothing is assumed and a priority with no target is simply not timed. A case's `first_response_due` and `resolution_due` are computed when it opens (clock minutes; the clock stops while WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER and restarts on the reply; a business-hours calendar is out, below). The first staff message (not a note) satisfies the first response. `service_cases.overdue` and the `overdue` flag come from a sweep; a case's `overdue_since` is set once per target. The counted alert metrics are in the Authority and abuse paragraph below. A daily digest of overdue cases to handlers is the alert inbox's, not a new notice.
  6. **Orders needing attention (order-svc, app).** `GET /admin/orders/attention?kind=&storeId=&after=&limit=` (any staff at the store; management sees all): a read of order-svc's own tables, no new table, decided by pure `OrderAttention` from **the business's own limits** (a condition with no limit set is not listed): `UNPAID_NEAR_EXPIRY` (a PENDING order within its `pending_limit_hours` window of lapsing; [fulfilment-overrides](fulfilment-overrides.md)), `AWAITING_PRICE_STALE` (older than `price_wait_flag_minutes`), `SUBSTITUTION_UNANSWERED` (a proposal open past half its window; [shopper-notices](shopper-notices.md)), `PICK_SHORT_PART_FULFILLED` (a delivery held part-picked), `READY_NOT_HANDED_OVER` (FULFILLED online with no `order_handovers` row after the business's `handover_flag_hours`, a new nullable field on `order_settings`, unset = not listed), and `PAID_NOT_CONFIRMED` (a captured payment older than the technical bound whose order is still PENDING: an error the platform should never leave; the row says so and names the order). Each row has `Open a case` (`POST /cases` with the order linked, category chosen by kind) and a link to the existing screen that fixes it. **Payments held for review** are payment-svc's queue ([card-payments](card-payments.md) slice 7); the tab shows their count (asked of payment-svc, fail-open: no count if unreadable) and links to that screen rather than copying the rows. No state is changed by looking. The cases and this queue are one screen with two tabs.
  7. **Export, erasure, retention, screens.** Case data joins customer-svc's `SubjectDataSpec` (below), the customer's export, the retention purge, and the Flutter screens (below).
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Inbound email and telephone (a shared inbox, call logging, screen pop).** Each needs mail parsing, spam and threading rules, or a telephony provider and consent to record. A case records that it came by phone or email (`channel`) and staff type what was said; the shopper replies in the app. If a mailbox integration is wanted later it is a driver behind an interface with a `SIMULATED` twin, opening cases through the same `POST /cases`.
  - **A second refund, credit or return engine inside the case.** Everything that moves value goes through the owner's route with the owner's rules; the case links the result. This is the rule that keeps "the case says refunded" true.
  - **Business-hours calendars and holidays in time targets.** They need a per-store calendar and country holidays; a business sets generous clock-minute targets (the clock stops while waiting on the customer). Revisit if a business asks; tenant-svc owns store hours.
  - **Automatic assignment, routing rules, skills, escalation chains.** A person assigns. Escalation is "raise the priority", which is an event; `service_cases.overdue` tells a manager.
  - **Customer satisfaction scores and surveys.** Their own feature and a marketing-consent question.
  - **A case for a guest shopper's own opening.** A guest has no login to reply to or see cases with; staff may open one for them by phone (contact kept on the case).
  - **Attachments and photos.** Storage, scanning and plan limits are a feature of their own (photos of damage). A shopper describes it in words; staff record what they saw. Named as the next slice, gated by a `case.attachments.mb.max` plan key when built.
  - **Assigning a case to a customer's own staff record to hide it.** Not a rule; see the conflict guard in Data and flow.

## Data and flow

**Why customer-svc owns it.** A case is about a person and their history: it sits beside the customer record, the loyalty and store-credit ledgers, the consent and privacy requests, the export and the erasure, all customer-svc's. The shopper's "my cases" is a `/customers/me/**` view. Order-svc owns *orders*, which a case only refers to by id; making order-svc the owner would put a person's complaints, notes and privacy handling inside the service whose erasure and export it would then have to duplicate, and would leave a complaint about a payment or a delivery with no natural home. notification-svc owns delivery, not the conversation. A new service for one table set was rejected: the platform's rule is to add a service when a thing has its own scaling or ownership, and cases have neither. The cost is one more `References` lookup to the owners of the things a case names, which is exactly what database-per-service asks for.

- **Owned by customer-svc:**
  - `cases`: id, `tenant_id`, `ref` (short reference, unique per business), `customer_id` (nullable), `contact_name`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone_e164` (only when there is no customer), `store_id` (nullable), `category`, `priority`, `status`, `subject`, `description`, `channel`, `opened_by` (staff login, or the shopper's login), `opened_by_kind` (STAFF, SHOPPER), `assigned_to`, `first_response_due`, `resolution_due`, `first_responded_at`, `overdue_since`, `resolution`, `resolution_note`, `resolved_at`, `resolved_by`, `closed_at`, `reopen_count`, `idempotency_key` (unique per tenant), created and updated in UTC. The row's status moves; it is not a ledger. Index `(tenant_id, status, resolution_due)`, `(tenant_id, customer_id)`, `(tenant_id, store_id, status)`, `(tenant_id, assigned_to, status)`.
  - `case_events` (append-only): id, `tenant_id`, `case_id`, `kind` (OPENED, ASSIGNED, PRIORITY_CHANGED, STATE_CHANGED, MESSAGE_SENT, MESSAGE_RECEIVED, NOTE_ADDED, LINKED, RESOLVED, REOPENED, TARGET_MISSED, CLOSED), `actor_id`, `actor_kind`, `detail` (small text/JSON of ids and words; **never a customer's personal value**), `at`. The append-only trail the audit reads.
  - `case_messages` (append-only): id, `tenant_id`, `case_id`, `direction`, `author_id`, `body`, `channel_used` (filled from notification-svc's acknowledgement event, null until), `at`. `case_notes` (append-only): id, `tenant_id`, `case_id`, `author_id`, `body`, `at`.
  - `case_links` (append-only) as above; unique on (tenant, case, kind, ref, role).
  - `case_settings` (one per business): `first_response_minutes` and `resolution_minutes` (a small set per priority), `reopen_days`, `open_per_customer_max`, `notices` (which of the three status notices are on), `auto_close_resolved_days` (a RESOLVED case closes itself after that many days with no reply; null = never), changed by and at.
  - `case_reply_templates`: id, `tenant_id`, `language`, `name`, `body`, active, by and at.
  - **Conflict guard:** a staff member cannot be the handler of, or resolve, a case whose customer's login is their own login or whose customer record shares their staff email (`409 CASE_CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST`, decided from customer-svc's own `customers` row and the caller's login link, never a guess; another handler takes it).
- **SubjectDataSpec (privacy-requests slice 4):** `cases.customer_id`, `contact_*`, `subject`, `description`: ANONYMISE (the shell stays: category, priority, timestamps, resolution kind, links' ids and amounts) with the reason "the business's record of how a complaint was handled"; `case_messages.body`, `case_notes.body`: ERASE the text (the row stays as a count with author kind); `case_events.detail`: RETAIN (holds ids only). The schema test that fails on an undeclared personal column covers all of it. `GET /internal/subject-data/{customerId}` returns the customer's cases with their messages and the shopper-visible notes; **internal notes are included as personal data about the customer**, with staff replaced by "a member of staff" (third-party data), and the export says which is which.
- **Retention:** cases and their children follow the business's retention schedule (`RetentionPurgeService`, tenant-svc's schedule); the period is the business's own, never assumed here.
- **Needs from other services:** order-svc and payment-svc, `References` (above); tenant-svc through `TenantProfiles` for stores and staff; notification-svc by event.
- **Events published (through the outbox, each with an `eventId`):** `CaseOpened`, `CaseResolved`, `CaseTargetMissed` (`storeql.customer.case-opened`, `…case-resolved`, `…case-target-missed`: `eventId`, `tenantId`, `caseId`, `ref`, `customerId`, `category`, `priority`, `storeId`, `occurredAt`; ids and words only), `CaseMessageSent` (`storeql.customer.case-message-sent`: as above plus `messageId`, `language`; the body is fetched by notification-svc through `GET /internal/cases/{id}/messages/{messageId}` so message text never rides a topic). Consumers: notification-svc (the notices and, for `CaseTargetMissed` and `CaseOpened` with a HIGH or URGENT priority, an in-app note to the store's handlers through the alert inbox), reporting-svc (counts by category and time to resolve, read only). All three also go on the webhook catalogue for a business's own systems, with no body.
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** open (staff and shopper), assign, resolve, link, a message (so a double click sends once). A retry answers with the first.
- **New error codes:** `404 CASE_NOT_FOUND` (also another business's, another shopper's), `409 CASE_CLOSED`, `409 CASE_STATE_INVALID` (a move the state does not allow), `409 CASE_RESOLUTION_NEEDS_LINK`, `409 CASE_LINK_NOT_THE_CUSTOMERS`, `404 CASE_LINK_NOT_FOUND`, `409 CASE_CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST`, `409 CASE_ASSIGNEE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (not staff of the business, or may not act at the store), `429 CASE_TOO_MANY_OPEN`, `400 CASE_SETTINGS_INVALID` (a non-positive target), `400 CASE_CATEGORY_UNKNOWN`, `400 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED`, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`, `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`.

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** the case holds none. A linked refund or grant shows its own amount and currency from its owner. Goodwill value for the alert metric is in the business's home currency; a foreign figure is translated through `FxRates` and, with no rate, not counted (and said so on the rules screen), as the alerts page says.
- **Ledger postings:** none by the case. The refund, credit or gift card it links post as they do today (`PaymentRefunded`, store-credit and gift-card liabilities in purchase-svc).
- **Dates:** every instant UTC; the clock for a target is elapsed minutes; the app shows local time in the store's zone (`TenantProfiles.Stores.zoneOf`).
- **Plan limits:** none new. (Attachments, when built, get their own key.)

## Constraints

- Golden rules 1 (references by owner call, no joins), 3 (tenant and customer from the JWT; a shopper's own login decides "mine"), 6 and 7 (outbox; consumers dedupe on `eventId`), 8 (`case_events`, `case_messages`, `case_notes`, `case_links` only appended to), 9, 10, 11, 15.
- **Privacy:** messages and notes are personal data: covered by export, anonymised on erasure, purged on the retention schedule, never in an event body or a log line, never in an alert's evidence (ids only).
- **Location-neutral:** categories, priorities and states are platform vocabulary shown in words (`status_labels.dart`), in the shopper's language; phones read in the business's countries; times in the store's zone; no country's consumer-complaint rule is assumed. Where a law names a complaint-handling deadline it is a business's own target or, later, a `Jurisdictions` row with a citation; none is seeded here.
- **Nothing here widens access:** a handler sees only what their store access allows; a shopper sees only their login's cases; the case never grants the right to a refund the caller could not make in the owner's screen.
- Existing behaviour unchanged: no new mandatory step in any order, return or refund flow; `caseId` is optional everywhere.

## Authority and abuse

- **Approvals:** none new. Money and access in a case pass through the existing keys at the owner's route: `sales.refund` (refund amount), `customer.store-credit-issue`, `customer.points-adjust`, `sales.gift-card-issue`, `sales.return` and its step-up. This page names them and designs no mechanism.
- **Alert metrics (rows to add to the catalogue on [exception-alerts](exception-alerts.md), owner customer-svc):** `service_cases.overdue` (COUNT of open cases past a target, subject store or business), `service_cases.goodwill_value` (SUM of the value of store credit, points-as-money and gift cards issued under cases, subject staff, home currency; from `manual_grants` with `caseId`), `service_cases.closed_without_action` (SHARE of one handler's resolutions that are EXPLAINED, NOT_UPHELD or WITHDRAWN, with `min_sample`, subject staff: a person quietly closing complaints).
- **Permission to add:** `cases.handle` in tenant-svc's permission catalogue.

## Decisions

- (2026-09-30 evening, reconciliation of the second set of pages) **Orders needing attention has one owner: [background-work-and-stuck-items](background-work-and-stuck-items.md) slice 7** (the shared `AttentionItem` shape, `GET /admin/orders/attention` and the pure `OrderAttention`, the merged Needs attention screen). Slice 6 here is that page's endpoint with these six kinds registered in its `AttentionKinds` and an *Open a case* action on each row; it builds no second queue. The word "case" here is a *service case* (`service_cases.*` metrics); see [investigation-cases](investigation-cases.md) for the other meaning. House-account and refund wording follows [till-tenders-foreign-cash-and-accounts](till-tenders-foreign-cash-and-accounts.md).

- **customer-svc owns cases** (2026-09-30, industry standard): the case is about the person and lives with their record, export and erasure; owners of the things it names answer by reference.
- **The case links; it never does** (2026-09-30): a resolution that says money or goods moved must link the record that moved them, made through the owner's own screen and rules.
- **Internal notes are personal data** (2026-09-30): included in the subject's export with staff names removed; erased with the customer; the anonymous shell stays for the business's own record.
