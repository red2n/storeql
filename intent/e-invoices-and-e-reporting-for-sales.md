## Problem

A sale to a VAT-registered business is invoiced automatically, as an EN 16931 document, and sent over the network the business chose; the transports are proven against stubs and a sale on this platform reaches another business's inbox. E-reporting of what no invoice covers is built for France. What keeps the row partial, beside a provider contract, is what happens **after** the first document, and a few places where the numbers can be wrong:

- **A credit note exists only for a return.** The database says so (`chk_sales_invoice_credit`). An invoiced online order that is cancelled, a line that is closed short or substituted, or a price agreed down afterwards leaves the invoice standing at a figure that is no longer true, with no way to correct it in the form the law wants (a credit note naming the invoice).
- **The buyer's answer is not kept.** The network's status is polled and the latest is stored; the history (received, approved, disputed, refused) is not, and a **refusal by the buyer's platform** is a final state with no next step.
- **E-reporting is complete in kind and wrong at the edges.** It leaves out no-receipt returns (they have no order, and the report joins through the order); it cuts each day at UTC midnight instead of the store's own; it counts a line that was closed short at full value; it never retries a submission the network could not be reached for, nor asks again about one it took; the payment-data stream reports every sale's day as a payment day though the duty covers services only; and nothing sends a report when its period is due unless somebody presses the button.
- **Some things a business can do could double-report.** An invoice requested for a sale after its period was reported would be counted as a report line and an invoice.
- **The date is UTC.** An invoice's issue date is the UTC day; a sale made after midnight local time is invoiced on the wrong day.
- **What was sent is not proved kept.** The documents, the transmissions and the submissions are kept, but nothing guards them, ties them to the law's retention period, or exports them for an inspector.
- **Anyone at a store can file.** Preview and submit ask only for OWNER or MANAGER: a manager held to one store can file the whole business's report.

## Outcome

- **Every correction the law knows is a credit note that names the invoice:** a return (as now), an order cancelled after invoicing, a line that will never arrive or was substituted (cancel and reissue), and a price agreed down afterwards. Numbers stay gapless; a refusal gives its number back.
- **The buyer's network answers are kept as a history,** and a refusal by the buyer opens a clear next step for a person, never an automatic reissue.
- **E-reporting is whole and on time:** every sale and return that no invoice carries is in, no-receipt returns included, in the store's own days, at what was actually supplied; a submission is retried and its status followed until settled; where the business allows it, a period that has ended is prepared and sent by itself, and the filing is recorded on the calendar without a person typing a digest.
- **A sale cannot be reported twice.** An invoice asked for after its period was reported is possible, and it raises a correction of that report instead of a silent duplicate.
- **Where the network needs a receiver's address, the platform finds it** through the provider's directory and keeps what it found and when.
- **What was sent is provably kept** for the period the country sets, and exported with a manifest.
- **What only a provider contract can finish is named** (below).

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **owner** who chooses the network and files; the **store manager** who corrects an invoice at their store; the **finance user**; the **buyer** (a business shopper, only affected); the **inspector** (an export, no login).
- **Channels:** back-office; the network; the POS and storefront are unchanged.
- **Scope:** an invoice and a correction belong to the order's store; a transport, a report and the archive are business-wide.
- **Roles that can write:**
  - **Cancel-and-reissue and price adjustment:** OWNER and MANAGER at the order's store (`requireStoreAccess`: a store-held manager acts only at theirs, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`); the price adjustment is also an approvals action (below).
  - **Transport settings, e-reporting preview, submit, correction, auto-transmit setting, archive:** OWNER and business-wide MANAGER only; a manager held to stores is refused `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`. **This fixes today's rule**, which admits any manager.
  - Reads of invoices and transmissions stay management and the order's store staff as built.
- **Sandbox tenant:** SIMULATED only; nothing reaches a real network; e-reporting is rehearsed against the simulated platform.

## Scope

- **In** (slices, in build order; each is built and tested alone):
  1. **The truth of what is reported (order-svc).** Four fixes to `EReportingRepository`, each a defect not a feature: (a) **the store's own day.** Days and period edges are judged in the zone of the store the sale or return was made at, through `TenantProfiles` (one `(store, zone)` list passed to the query); **UTC only where a zone cannot be read**, as the expiry rule does; the invoice's issue date and numbering year follow the same rule (the tax point is the store's day, not UTC's). (b) **No-receipt returns.** They join no order, so they are read from `returns` with `no_receipt` and their `return_items` (`unit_price`, `tax_amount`) at the store's day, as a negative operation; `return_items` gains `vat_code` and `vat_rate` snapshots for a no-receipt item, set from pricing-svc at the moment the price is taken, so the report never divides a rate out of a rounded tax. (c) **What was supplied.** A sold line counts for `qty − short_qty` of its net and VAT (pro rata, the way the charge is), so a line closed short is not reported at full value; a substitute line is its own line already. (d) **Counted once.** The join from a return line to its order line is by the line, not the variant, so two lines of one variant cannot double a refund. The rule that a sale an e-invoice carries is out of scope is unchanged. A test proves each against the old figure.
  2. **The right payment data (product-svc, order-svc).** `EREPORTING_PAY_FR` covers **services**. product-svc gains a per-variant `supply_kind` GOODS or SERVICES (default GOODS, the fact for a stock business; nothing else assumed and a business that sells a service sets it), read by order-svc's invoice and report (cached, fail open to GOODS so a lookup failure never blocks a sale). The payment stream reports, per day, only the sales whose lines include a service, **on the day the money arrived** taken from order-svc's own payment accumulation (`PaymentCaptured` instants per order; a till sale is paid as it is made), and reports nothing where the business sells no service, an honest *néant*. Delivery and slot charges follow the goods they accompany and are not a separate supply. A business with no service reports an empty payment stream as before.
  3. **Send and settle (order-svc).** E-reporting gains the same worker the invoices have: a submission the network could not be reached for is retried with back-off and one it took (`PENDING`) is asked after until it settles ACCEPTED or REJECTED, `attempts` counted, `next_attempt_at` kept; the pending state and its reason stay visible. **The transport seam gets `statusOfReport(providerRef)`** (France's platform answers on the route it deposits on). **Idempotency:** a submit takes an `Idempotency-Key` (`400 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_INVALID` when not a UUIDv7), and a retry answers with the first; a period that stands is still `409 EREPORTING_ALREADY_SUBMITTED`.
  4. **Sent on its own, and recorded on the calendar (order-svc, tenant-svc).** `ereporting_settings` (per business, **default off**): `auto_transmit`. When on, a sweep reads tenant-svc's calendar for periods that have ended (`due` list, REST, existing 07.14) and prepares and sends each once, per return, period and currency; when off the calendar's due list stays the reminder and notification-svc raises `EREPORT_DUE` to the owner once. On ACCEPTED order-svc announces `EReportSubmitted` (`eventId`, `tenantId`, `returnCode`, `periodStart`, `periodEnd`, `currency`, `payloadDigest`, `status`, `occurredAt`), and **tenant-svc records the filing on the calendar** with that digest, once per event id, `filed_by` SYSTEM: the bytes and the calendar are tied together without a person typing the digest and without either service holding the other's data (the existing tie by digest stands for a person's manual filing). A **correction** (a submission that supersedes another) is never automatic: it is a person's act under the approvals action `finance.ereport-correct` (the difference in tax, home currency).
  5. **Not twice, and corrected (order-svc).** Issuing an invoice for a sale whose supply date falls inside a **standing** e-reporting submission (the business asked for an invoice after the period was reported) is allowed, and the sale is then reported twice unless corrected: the invoice is issued, the affected period is flagged `NEEDS_CORRECTION` on the submissions list with the sale named, and a **proposed correction** (the period rebuilt without that sale) is offered for a person to send under `finance.ereport-correct`; nothing is corrected silently. `GET /admin/ereporting/proposals`.
  6. **Corrections that are credit notes (order-svc).** `sales_invoices` gains `correction_kind` (RETURN, CANCELLATION, CANCEL_AND_REISSUE, PRICE_ADJUSTMENT) and the check becomes "a credit note has a return **or** a correction kind"; a credit note still names its invoice (`preceding_invoice_id`, BT-25) and takes the next `CRN` number gaplessly. (a) **Cancellation:** an order cancelled or voided after it was invoiced is credited in full, once per order (a unique guard), on the cancellation's own transaction. (b) **Cancel and reissue:** an `OrderLineShortClosed` or `OrderLineSubstituted` on an invoiced order credits the invoice in full and issues a new invoice for what the order now stands at (the practice the network reforms call *annule et remplace*), each once per adjustment (`order_line_adjustments` id is the guard); the shopper's refund already happens in payment-svc from those events and is not repeated. (c) **Price adjustment:** `POST /admin/orders/{id}/price-adjustments {lines:[{variantId, amountOff}], reason}` records an append-only `order_line_adjustments` row of kind PRICE, issues a partial credit note for exactly that, announces `OrderPriceAdjusted` (`refundAmount`), and payment-svc refunds it once per event with `PaymentRefunded.kind = ORDER_ADJUSTMENT` as it does for a short line (every refund goes through payment-svc). It needs the approvals action `sales.invoice-adjust` (the adjustment, home currency; a manager's own reason is recorded, a second person where the business's rule says). It is refused on an order with no invoice: an adjustment on a till sale to the public is a refund, and returns already cover it. (d) **Refused by the buyer:** see slice 7. A correction's document is sent by the same transport, as any other.
  7. **The buyer's answers as a history (order-svc).** Append-only `sales_invoice_status_events` (the invoice, the network's code and words, the mapped state RECEIVED, APPROVED, DISPUTED, REFUSED, PAID, OTHER, the instant, the transmission it followed): every distinct status a poll returns is a row, never overwritten; the invoice shows the trail. A **REFUSED** by the buyer's platform marks the invoice `buyer_refused`, notifies the manager of the order's store (notification-svc `EINVOICE_BUYER_REFUSED`), and offers `POST /admin/sales-invoices/{id}/cancel-and-reissue {reason}` (OWNER/MANAGER at the store, `Idempotency-Key`): a credit note in full and a new invoice, for a person to send after correcting whatever the buyer objected to. It never reissues by itself. Where the network requires the **seller** to say that money was received (France, for services and mixed supplies, when `E_INVOICE_PAYMENT_STATUS` is in force for the business's country), a driver method `sendStatus(providerRef, PAID)` is called once when the order's payment is recorded, for invoices with a service line; a network with no such message answers `NOT_SUPPORTED`, recorded, not a failure.
  8. **Finding the receiver (order-svc, driver).** The transport seam gains `resolveReceiver(vatId, country)` (the provider's directory: a Peppol participant lookup through the access point, France's directory through its platform), stub-tested for its exact request. A buyer with no electronic address on file is looked up when the invoice is sent and the result kept in `receiver_lookups` (tenant, VAT id, address, provider, the instant, found or not); a found address is used and shown, an unfound one keeps `EINVOICE_RECEIVER_ADDRESS_MISSING` as now. The customer's own record is never written by order-svc (customer-svc's data), and the result ages out by the business's own setting `einvoicing.lookup.max-age-days` (unset = looked up again on every send).
  9. **The archive (order-svc).** (a) A **guard**: a trigger forbids updating an issued document's bytes, number, date or amounts, a submission's payload or digest, and deleting any of these rows. (b) **The floor:** kept at least the country's floor (class `TRANSACTIONS`, or an `E_INVOICE_ARCHIVE` figure) from the issue date, never shortened by the business's schedule; `RetentionPurgeService` is proven never to touch them, and `SubjectDataSpec` declares them `RETAIN` with the reason. (c) **Export and verify:** `GET /admin/einvoicing/archive?from&to` streams a package (each document as issued in UBL and any CII or Factur-X form, each transmission and status trail, each submission with its payload and digest) with a sha256 manifest built by the shared `ArchiveManifest` (below); `POST /admin/einvoicing/archive/verify` recomputes and reports mismatches (`EINVOICE_ARCHIVE_TAMPERED`). `@SensitiveRead`, management with the statutory-return permission.
  10. **A second country's format (seam only).** `EReporting.payload` is French. The calendar's `statutory_returns` row names its `format`, and order-svc resolves a `ReportingFormat` by it, so a second country's real-time or periodic reporting (a different payload and a different network) is a new writer and a calendar row, not a fork of the service. Only the French writer is built; nothing about France is assumed by a business elsewhere.
  11. **The screens** and the alert metrics (below).
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Finishing the last mile to a certified provider.** See the go-live list.
  - **Formats and networks for countries not yet built** beyond the seam of slice 10 (the platform names the four networks it has drivers for).
  - **Reporting a sale to a shopper as an invoice, or issuing one to a shopper who gave no business identity.** The invoice is for a business buyer; a shopper is reported.
  - **Refunding without payment-svc.** A price adjustment and a short line refund through payment-svc; a credit note never moves money by itself.
  - **Automatically reissuing after a buyer's refusal.** A person corrects the cause and presses the button.
  - **Automatically correcting a submission.** A person under `finance.ereport-correct`.
  - **Separate treatment of delivery and slot charges.** They follow the goods; a business whose adviser says otherwise is a later row.
  - **Payments on account and credit sales.** The platform sells for payment at the moment of sale; a payment date later than supply is used only where a capture instant says so.
  - **Changing an existing invoice.** An issued document is never edited; a correction is a new one.

## Data and flow

- **Owned by** order-svc: `sales_invoices` gain `correction_kind`, `buyer_refused`, `service_lines` (boolean, from the supply kinds at issue) and their check replaces `chk_sales_invoice_credit`; new `sales_invoice_status_events` (append-only), `receiver_lookups`, `ereporting_settings`, `ereporting_submissions` gain `next_attempt_at`, `needs_correction` and a status `FAILED` is **not** added (an unreachable network stays `PENDING` with the reason and attempts, as today); `return_items` gain `vat_code`, `vat_rate`; `order_line_adjustments` gain kind `PRICE`; a guard trigger on the archive tables. Every table has `tenant_id` first in its index and v7 id checks.
- **Owned by** product-svc: `supply_kind` on the variant (default GOODS). **Owned by** tenant-svc: new obligation and retention rows (data, each with a citation and date), and a consumer that records the filing on the calendar.
- **Shared piece:** common-service `ArchiveManifest` (pure: a list of named byte arrays in, a manifest with sha256 per file and one over the whole out; and the verifier), used here and by purchase-svc's archive ([e-invoices-received](e-invoices-received.md) slice 5) so there is one definition.
- **Needs from other services:** stores' zones and identity (`TenantProfiles`); obligations and floors (`Jurisdictions`, fail **closed** for "is this duty due", as `requireDuty` does today); the calendar's due periods (tenant-svc REST); a variant's supply kind (product-svc REST, cached, fail open); the provider's directory and status calls (drivers); payment instants from its own accumulated payments.
- **Events published:** `EReportSubmitted` (`storeql.order.ereport-submitted`, fields above; consumer tenant-svc, idempotent on `eventId`); `OrderPriceAdjusted` (`storeql.order.price-adjusted`: `eventId`, `tenantId`, `orderId`, `adjustmentId`, `refundAmount`, `currency`, `reason`, `occurredAt`; consumers payment-svc (refund once), purchase-svc (posts the sale's reduction as it does a refund, through `PaymentRefunded`), reporting-svc, notification-svc); `SalesInvoiceCredited` (`storeql.order.sales-invoice-credited`: `eventId`, `tenantId`, `invoiceId`, `creditNoteId`, `kind`, `occurredAt`) for reporting-svc and notification-svc. Existing `OrderCancelled`, `OrderLineShortClosed`, `OrderLineSubstituted` are consumed inside order-svc's own transaction, not re-consumed.
- **Endpoints** (order-svc): `POST /admin/sales-invoices/{id}/cancel-and-reissue`; `POST /admin/orders/{id}/price-adjustments`; `GET /admin/sales-invoices/{id}/status-events`; `GET /admin/ereporting/proposals`, `POST /admin/ereporting/submissions/{id}/correct`; `GET|PUT /admin/ereporting/settings`; `GET /admin/einvoicing/archive`, `POST /admin/einvoicing/archive/verify`. Cursor pagination on lists.
- **Retryable writes** (Idempotency-Key): every POST above including the e-reporting submit; a retry answers with the first.
- **New error codes:** `ORDER_INVOICE_NOT_INVOICED` 409 (an adjustment or reissue on a sale never invoiced); `ORDER_INVOICE_ALREADY_CREDITED` 409; `ORDER_INVOICE_NOT_REFUSED` 409 (reissue after a refusal that did not happen, unless the business's role says otherwise: cancel-and-reissue is also allowed after an order change); `ORDER_PRICE_ADJUSTMENT_INVALID` 400 (not positive, above the line); `ORDER_PRICE_ADJUSTMENT_NEEDS_APPROVAL` 403 (`sales.invoice-adjust`, the shared authority `details`); `EREPORTING_CORRECTION_NEEDS_APPROVAL` 403 (`finance.ereport-correct`); `EREPORTING_NOT_STANDING` 409 (existing); `EREPORTING_ALREADY_SUBMITTED` 409 (existing); `EREPORTING_PERIOD_NOT_DUE` 409 (auto-transmit asked for a period not ended); `EREPORTING_SETTINGS_INVALID` 400; `EINVOICE_ARCHIVE_PERIOD_INVALID` 400; `EINVOICE_ARCHIVE_TAMPERED` 409; `EINVOICE_ARCHIVE_IMMUTABLE` 409; `BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY` 403; `STORE_ACCESS_DENIED` 403; `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_INVALID` 400.

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** an invoice and its corrections are in the order's currency; an e-report is per currency (existing: one submission per period, return and currency); no currency assumed.
- **Ledger postings:** none of its own. A credit note reduces what was sold; the money leaves through payment-svc's refund and purchase-svc posts that from `PaymentRefunded`, as every refund is posted today. A cancel-and-reissue nets to what the order now stands at.
- **Dates:** an invoice's issue date and numbering year are the store's own day; a report's days and edges are the store's own days; a sale's day is its first FULFILLED in the append-only history (unchanged); payment instants are UTC. **No zone, year start or country is assumed** (UTC only where a zone cannot be read).
- **Settings** (owner: order-svc, unset = off): `auto_transmit`; `einvoicing.lookup.max-age-days`. The business's approvals rules for `sales.invoice-adjust` and `finance.ereport-correct` live on the approvals page. The platform names no number.
- **Plan limits:** none.

## Constraints

- **Existing tenants:** a business that sends no invoices and reports nothing reads as today; a period already reported keeps its submission (immutable) and a re-run of the old figures is not silently changed: the corrected rules apply to new submissions and to a **preview** that says how it now differs from the standing one (`EReportingCompatibilityIT`).
- **Golden rules 6, 7, 8:** every correction and submission is one transaction with its event; consumers are idempotent by event id and adjustment id; invoices, credit notes, status events, submissions and adjustments are append-only; only a submission's status and attempt counters move, recorded.
- **Golden rule 1:** payment instants, calendar periods, obligations and supply kinds come by REST or event, never a join.
- **Never invents law:** every obligation is a data row with a citation and an effective date, verified by the compliance owner before it is live; a business elsewhere is asked nothing.
- **A document is evidence:** never edited; the credit note is the correction.

## What only a real provider contract can finish (go-live items)

Supplied by the user or the deployment owner; not code; the readiness check names them until they exist:

1. A **contract with a certified provider** per network: France's approved platform (which also carries the e-reporting deposit and the directory), a Peppol access point, and for Poland a KSeF authorisation the business issues; India's IRP credentials for an Indian business.
2. The provider's **credentials**, sealed under the deployment key and entered by the owner (`storeql.einvoice.*` base URLs and keys in `.env`).
3. The **mapping of each facade** (the routes the drivers are stub-tested against, now including `statusOfReport`, `sendStatus` and `resolveReceiver`) to the provider's real API, and the provider's **conformance test** passed with a real document and a real report.
4. Each country's **citations verified** by the compliance owner before its data rows go live, the business's **size band** where dates are staged, and its **SIREN / identity** on the provider's account.
