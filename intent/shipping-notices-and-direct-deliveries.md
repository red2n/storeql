## Problem

A supplier tells the shop what is on the lorry, and the platform has nowhere to put it: the buyer receives a purchase order blind, counting every pallet at the door against nothing but the order. A delivery that has no order at all (bread, milk, newspapers, a van of flowers) cannot be received in purchase-svc, because a receipt names an order; the shop either keys it as a stock receipt in inventory-svc, which records the stock and owes the supplier nothing, so the invoice arrives with nothing to match, or refuses the driver. A lorry that brings 105 cases against an order of 100 is refused outright, because the platform never chose a tolerance and the code will not invent one; a supplier who always over-delivers by a case has no rule. And a receipt cannot be corrected: a miscounted door is fixed by a stock adjustment that has lost its link to the delivery, so the supplier's liability stays wrong.

## Outcome

- **A supplier's notice of what is coming can be recorded, or arrives as a document.** It says which containers (pallets, cases, totes) hold which products, lots and dates, for which order if any, and when.
- **The dock receives against the notice, container by container.** One press for "everything as advised", counts where it differs, and a container that did not arrive or arrived unannounced is a recorded difference, not a guess.
- **A delivery with no order is accepted by a person who may accept it, costs what the person says it costs, and reaches the ledger and the supplier's invoice like any other delivery.**
- **Each supplier has its own over-receipt rule, set by the business, and off until set.** Within it the excess is accepted and flagged; beyond it the excess is refused at the door as a refused quantity.
- **A receipt can be corrected afterwards,** once, on the record, by a second person above the business's ceiling, and the supplier's liability, the stock and the books all follow.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the storekeeper at the dock (receives), the buyer (records notices, sets supplier rules, sees differences), the store manager (accepts a delivery above the ceiling, corrects a receipt), the owner, the finance clerk (invoice matching), the supplier (only affected).
- **Channels:** back-office (Admin > Procurement, and the store's Receiving view).
- **Scope:** per store for a delivery and a receipt; per supplier for the over-receipt rule and notices; per business for settings.
- **Roles that can write:**
  - **Notices:** OWNER, MANAGER, STOREKEEPER (a buyer is one of these), at the notice's store (`requireStoreAccess`); a business integration posts documents with an API key of at least the STOREKEEPER tier ([api-key-controls](api-key-controls.md)).
  - **Receiving against a notice:** any staff at the store, as receiving is today.
  - **A direct delivery:** any staff at the store to record; within the business's ceiling it books at once, above it waits for a second person (approvals key `stock.receipt-unordered`, below).
  - **The over-receipt rule and the supplier's "may deliver without an order" flag:** OWNER, MANAGER (as `PUT /suppliers/{id}` today: `SupplierResource.java:67`); business-wide caller for a supplier used at every store.
  - **A receipt correction:** OWNER, MANAGER at the receipt's store; a manager held to other stores is refused `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`.
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same; a `SIMULATED` document driver is the only one it may use.

## Scope

- **In**, in build order (each slice testable alone):
  1. **Over-receipt rule per supplier** (purchase-svc). `suppliers` gain `over_receipt_pct` and `over_receipt_qty` (both null = no tolerance = today's refusal, `PURCHASE_OVER_RECEIPT` unchanged). A receipt line whose total received exceeds the order line by no more than the rule (whichever the business set; both set means the larger allowance) is accepted: the whole received quantity is booked at the order's unit price, the order line shows `overReceived`, the receipt carries `overReceipt: true`, and the buyer is told. Beyond the rule the request is refused with the same code, and the message names the allowed quantity; the storekeeper's way through is the refused quantity of [goods-receipt-detail](goods-receipt-detail.md) (the excess is marked refused, never booked). The order never goes past what the rule allows, however many receipts arrive (the check runs under the order's row lock, as it does now). Consignment, dropship and bonded orders keep no tolerance (their own rules). No default number is named by the platform.
  2. **A delivery with no order** (purchase-svc, inventory-svc). A supplier flagged `direct_delivery_allowed` (off until a manager sets it) may deliver with no order. `POST /direct-deliveries` names the store, the supplier, the delivery note reference, and lines of variant, quantity and **unit cost typed by the person** (required, the business's home currency or the supplier's own currency as the supplier record says; a foreign amount is stored with its currency and translated through `FxRates` for the ledger, failing closed without a rate). The last cost paid to that supplier for the variant is *suggested* on the screen, never filled in silently. In one transaction purchase-svc creates a purchase order with `source = DIRECT_DELIVERY`, status `RECEIVED`, `submitted_at` null (no promise, so the scorecard's late days stay unknown, as they do for an order with none), one goods receipt against it and the `GoodsReceived` outbox row; the receipt and stock follow the ordinary path (the ledger posts Dr Stock / Cr GR/IR at the typed cost; the supplier's invoice then matches order, receipt and invoice through the existing three-way match with no special case). It skips `purchasing.order`, because nothing was ordered; the second-person control is the one below. A direct delivery is **owned, duty-paid stock only**: a bonded or consignment arrival needs an order (`409 PURCHASE_DIRECT_DELIVERY_NOT_SIMPLE` if the variant or supplier says otherwise where purchase-svc knows it). The same supplier and delivery note twice warns `DELIVERY_NOTE_SEEN` as goods-receipt-detail slice 3 does and is never refused. The good/damaged/refused split and lots of goods-receipt-detail apply to these lines unchanged.
  3. **The ceiling for a delivery with no order** (purchase-svc; inventory-svc's key extended). Above the business's rule for `stock.receipt-unordered` (value at the typed cost and quantity, home currency) the delivery is recorded `AWAITING_APPROVAL` and books nothing until a second person, other than the recorder, approves it through [approvals](approvals.md); refused or expired it books nothing and the goods are the store's to send back. Within the ceiling, or where the business has set no rule for the key (no rule is no gate, as for every key but `sales.price-override`), it books at once; where the rule exists and cannot be read the key's own fail mode (closed) applies and the delivery waits. The key's enforcement points become two: inventory-svc's direct stock receipt (existing) and this delivery. One key, one rule, because both measure "a receipt with no purchase order".
  4. **Shipping notices, keyed** (purchase-svc). `POST /shipping-notices` records a supplier's notice: supplier, store, the supplier's reference, the order if there is one (`poId`, null for an unordered delivery), `shipDate`, `expectedAt`, carrier, and containers, each with a label (the supplier's container id, such as a pallet code; free text, no vocabulary assumed), a kind (free text) and lines: an item (matched to a variant through the order line, else through `supplier_item_codes` on the code sent; a line that matches nothing is kept `UNMATCHED` and shown, never guessed), a quantity, and optionally lot number and expiry date. `shipping_notices` and their containers and lines are immutable once written; the header status moves `EXPECTED`, `PART_RECEIVED`, `RECEIVED`, `CANCELLED`, `SUPERSEDED` and each move is a row in `shipping_notice_events` (append-only). A supplier's amended notice for the same supplier reference is a new version that supersedes the earlier one, only while nothing was received against it (`409 PURCHASE_NOTICE_ALREADY_RECEIVING`). A notice for an order does not change the order and never raises the received quantity: only a receipt does.
  5. **Receiving against a notice, by container** (purchase-svc). `POST /shipping-notices/{id}/receive` (Idempotency-Key) takes, per container, `AS_ADVISED` (one press: every line good, quantities as advised), or `COUNTED` with per-line good, damaged and refused quantities and lot/expiry overrides, or `NOT_ARRIVED`; plus `unadvised` containers the driver brought that the notice did not name, with their lines. It creates one goods receipt (against the order, or as a direct delivery from slice 2 when the notice names none) through the same transaction as slices 1 to 3, with the container reference kept on each receipt line (`goods_receipt_lines.container_ref`), so a recall or a query can find the pallet. Every difference is a row in `shipping_notice_variances` (append-only): `SHORT`, `OVER`, `MISSING_CONTAINER`, `UNADVISED_CONTAINER`, `LOT_DIFFERS`, `EXPIRY_DIFFERS`, with quantities and who counted. The scorecard shows *notice accuracy* (containers and lines as advised over those received against a notice) beside its parts, **not weighted into the grade**: a weight would be a policy number the business has not chosen. A notice with a partly received container stays `PART_RECEIVED`; later receipts against it continue. A notice whose `expectedAt` day has passed in the store's zone with nothing received raises `ShippingNoticeOverdue` once (a nightly sweeper, no number: the grace is the end of the expected day in the store's own zone).
  6. **Notices arriving as documents** (purchase-svc, `shared/einvoice`). The pattern is the e-invoice inbox's. A `DespatchAdviceReader` beside the invoice readers reads the open UBL 2.1 Despatch Advice (XML, with the same XML hardening the invoice readers use: no external entities, size cap) and the platform's own JSON shape (the keyed request), and yields the same neutral notice. `POST /shipping-notices/documents` accepts a document from a business's own integration (API key at the STOREKEEPER tier or above, the supplier named or found by the sender's code in `supplier_item_codes`-style supplier identifiers that the supplier record already holds); a fetch-driver seam `ShippingNoticeInbox` (interface, `SIMULATED` driver that hands over a seeded notice so a demo and the k6 flow run, catalogue row in `ShippingNotices.CATALOGUE`, a `ck_notice_provider` check) is the place a real network's driver goes, as `docs/ACCOUNTING-CONNECTORS.md` describes: **no EDI network is assumed and none is built here**; adding one is a driver, a stub-backed test of its exact request shape and a catalogue row. A document that fails to parse or names no known supplier is kept in `shipping_notice_documents` with the reason and shown to the buyer, never dropped; the same file (checksum) twice is one document; bytes count toward `documents.mb.max` (the purchasing-documents pool of goods-receipt-detail).
  7. **A receipt corrected afterwards** (purchase-svc, inventory-svc). `POST /goods-receipts/{id}/adjustments` (Idempotency-Key, management at the receipt's store): per line a signed quantity correction and a required reason and note. Never edits the receipt; a `goods_receipt_adjustments` row (append-only) records it. **Down** (fewer arrived than was booked): purchase-svc posts Dr GR/IR / Cr Stock at the order's price and inventory-svc draws the quantity from the batch the receipt made, only while that batch still holds it (`409 PURCHASE_RECEIPT_ADJUST_STOCK_GONE` when it was sold or moved: the way through is a vendor return or a stock adjustment, which are their own acts). **Up** (more arrived than booked): the over-receipt rule of slice 1 applies against the order, and the extra books as a further receipt line. The order's received quantity and the supplier scorecard's figures are recomputed from the receipts and adjustments together. Above the business's rule for the new key `stock.receipt-correct` (net value of the correction, home currency) a second person other than the one who received and the one who corrects must approve it. A cost correction is **not** an adjustment: a price difference is the invoice variance of the three-way match, and re-costing a batch that may already have been sold is out. A closed accounting period refuses it (`PERIOD_CLOSED`, as receipts do). The `StockAdjusted` it publishes carries `origin: RECEIPT_CORRECTION` so purchase-svc posts its own reversal from `GoodsReceiptAdjusted` and never a second shrinkage from the adjustment ([stock-counts](stock-counts.md) slice 6's one-cause-one-posting rule).
  8. **The screens** (Flutter). See Screens.
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **An EDI network, VAN or AS2 link, and the US convenience-trade DEX/NEX handheld protocols.** Oracle grades DSD "with DEX/NEX"; a driver on slice 6's seam is where a business that needs one puts it. A driver's handheld delivery arrives as a notice raised the same day, and the dock receives against it as slice 5 says.
  - **A notice raising or changing an order, or counting as stock.** It is information: the on-order and inbound figures already come from the order.
  - **Weighting notice accuracy into the scorecard grade.** Shown, unweighted.
  - **Cost correction of a received batch, and re-costing history.** The invoice variance is the tool.
  - **Approving a delivery by a supplier's portal, and a supplier login.** The buyer records; no supplier account exists.
  - **Advanced appointment scheduling (dock doors, time slots).** Not asked, and it is a warehouse-management feature.
  - **Direct delivery of consignment, dropship or bonded stock.** They have their own rules and need an order.
  - **Deriving a per-supplier tolerance from history.** The platform proposes no number.

## Data and flow

- **Owned by purchase-svc:**
  - `suppliers` gain `over_receipt_pct NUMERIC(6,2)`, `over_receipt_qty NUMERIC(18,3)` (both nullable, non-negative), `direct_delivery_allowed BOOLEAN DEFAULT false`.
  - `purchase_orders.source` gains `DIRECT_DELIVERY` (its CHECK); `goods_receipts` gain `over_receipt`, `notice_id`, `delivery_note_ref` (shared with goods-receipt-detail); `goods_receipt_lines` gain `container_ref`, `over_qty`.
  - `shipping_notices` (id, tenant_id, supplier_id, store_id, po_id null, supplier_ref, version, superseded_by, ship_date, expected_at, carrier, status, source KEYED | DOCUMENT | SIMULATED, created_by, created_at); `shipping_notice_containers`; `shipping_notice_lines` (variant_id null when UNMATCHED, item_code, qty, uom, lot_no, expiry_date); `shipping_notice_events` (append-only); `shipping_notice_variances` (append-only); `shipping_notice_documents` (raw bytes' checksum, parse result, reason). Every one with `tenant_id` first in its index.
  - `direct_deliveries` (the record of the person's request: recorder, note ref, state `AWAITING_APPROVAL` | `BOOKED` | `REFUSED` | `EXPIRED`, the order and receipt it produced).
  - `goods_receipt_adjustments` (append-only): receipt_id, line_id, qty_delta, reason, note, approval reference, made_by, made_at.
- **Owned by inventory-svc:** nothing new; it consumes `GoodsReceived` and `GoodsReceiptAdjusted`.
- **Needs from other services:** the store's zone (`TenantProfiles.Stores.zoneOf`) for the overdue day; the home currency and `FxRates`; a variant's tracking (goods-receipt-detail, cached, fail open); on-hand of the batch a correction would draw (inventory-svc REST, or the event's own answer: the handler refuses in a way the correction is told of; the design uses the synchronous read before writing, fail closed for a *down* correction because it moves stock). No joins.
- **Events published (outbox, `eventId` last):** `GoodsReceived` (existing; additive `noticeId`, `overReceipt`, `containerRef` per line, `source: DIRECT_DELIVERY` where so); `GoodsReceiptAdjusted` (`storeql.purchase.goods-receipt-adjusted`: tenantId, receiptId, storeId, lines of variantId/qtyDelta/batch reference/value/currency, reason, madeBy) consumed by inventory-svc (draws or receives the delta once, keyed by `eventId`) and reporting-svc; `ShippingNoticeVarianceRecorded` and `ShippingNoticeOverdue` (`storeql.purchase.shipping-notice-variance-recorded`, `…-overdue`) consumed by notification-svc (`SHIPPING_NOTICE_VARIANCE`, `SHIPPING_NOTICE_OVERDUE`, to the buyer and the store's managers, in-app and email by preference); `ApprovalRequested/Decided/Expired` (the shared block).
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** direct delivery, receive against a notice, a notice keyed or by document (also by checksum), a correction, the supplier rule change.
- **New error codes:**
  - `422 PURCHASE_OVER_RECEIPT` (existing, now names the allowed quantity).
  - `409 PURCHASE_DIRECT_DELIVERY_NOT_ALLOWED` (the supplier is not flagged), `409 PURCHASE_DIRECT_DELIVERY_NOT_SIMPLE`, `400 PURCHASE_DIRECT_DELIVERY_COST_REQUIRED`, `409 PURCHASE_DIRECT_DELIVERY_NOT_APPROVED`.
  - `404 PURCHASE_NOTICE_NOT_FOUND`, `409 PURCHASE_NOTICE_ALREADY_RECEIVING`, `409 PURCHASE_NOTICE_NOT_RECEIVABLE` (cancelled or superseded), `422 PURCHASE_NOTICE_UNMATCHED_LINE` (receiving an `UNMATCHED` line without naming a variant), `400 PURCHASE_NOTICE_DOCUMENT_INVALID`, `415 PURCHASE_NOTICE_DOCUMENT_TYPE`.
  - `404 PURCHASE_RECEIPT_NOT_FOUND`, `409 PURCHASE_RECEIPT_ADJUST_STOCK_GONE`, `403 PURCHASE_RECEIPT_ADJUST_NOT_APPROVED`, `400 PURCHASE_RECEIPT_ADJUST_REASON_REQUIRED`, `409 PERIOD_CLOSED` (existing).
  - `400 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED`; the plan-limit refusal `Entitlements` already raises for documents.

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** the order's own; a direct delivery in a foreign currency is translated at the business's rate and fails closed without one; nothing is charged in another currency.
- **Ledger postings:** direct delivery: Dr Stock / Cr GR/IR at the typed cost when booked (dated the receipt), as any receipt. Correction down: Dr GR/IR / Cr Stock at the order's price; up: the reverse, dated the correction (redated to the first open day under [accounting-periods](accounting-periods.md), `document_date` kept). Over-receipt: the excess books at the order's price like the rest.
- **Dates:** UTC instants; expiry and expected day judged in the store's own zone; the overdue sweep counts the end of the expected day there.
- **Plan limits:** notice and receipt documents spend the existing `documents.mb.max` pool through `Entitlements.requireBytesWithin`; nothing new in `Plans.CATALOGUE`.

## Constraints

- Golden rule 1: inventory-svc's batch is never joined; a down correction asks it (REST) or is refused by its handler, never assumed.
- Append-only: notice versions, notice events and variances, receipt adjustments, receipts, movements. A mistake is a further row.
- Existing tenants: no supplier has a tolerance or the direct flag until set, so receiving behaves as today; a receipt with only `qtyReceived` reads as before (`ReceiptCompatibilityIT`); inventory-svc's direct stock receipt keeps working and stays a **stock-only** fact ("arrived, owes nothing"): a business that wants supplier deliveries accountable turns on the setting `receiving.supplier-deliveries-via-purchasing` (off until set), after which a receipt naming a supplier at inventory-svc is refused `409 INVENTORY_RECEIPT_SUPPLIER_USE_DELIVERY` and the Receive dialog sends the person to *Delivery with no order*. Found, gift and sample sources (receiving-controls) are unaffected.
- Tenant from the JWT; DTOs both ways; a notice, a document or a receipt is never served to another business.
- Never invent policy: tolerance, the ceiling and the direct flag are the business's; the platform names no number.

## Already there

## Acceptance

Who, authority, retry, isolation, record, abuse, refusals, location-neutral and the catalogue entry are each a line below, per the wave-2 rule.

- [ ] With no rule set an over-receipt is refused as before; with `over_receipt_pct` or `_qty` set the excess within it books at the order's price, the receipt is flagged and the buyer told; beyond it `422 PURCHASE_OVER_RECEIPT` names the allowed quantity — `OverReceiptIT.aToleranceIsTheSuppliersOwnAndOffUntilSet`, `OverReceiptTest` (pure)
- [ ] Two lorries at once cannot together exceed the tolerance — `OverReceiptIT.twoReceiptsTogetherCannotPassTheRule`

## Flow Tests entry

- **Catalogue area and file:** `target/flow-catalogue/procurement/shipping-notices-and-direct-deliveries.json` (the procurement domain, beside `goods-receipt`); the receiving cases of `inventory/inv-receiving-stock.json` point to it for the no-order path. The feature is not BUILT until this entry exists and its cases are automated.
- **Cases:**
  - Happy: a notice arrives and is received by container as advised (`ShippingNoticeIT.receivingByContainer`, k6 `shipping-notice-flow`); a delivery with no order books and matches its invoice (`DirectDeliveryIT.aDeliveryWithNoOrderReachesTheLedgerAndMatchesTheInvoice`); an over-receipt within the supplier's rule is accepted (`OverReceiptIT`); a receipt is corrected down (`ReceiptCorrectionIT`).
  - Negative: over the rule (`422 PURCHASE_OVER_RECEIPT`); unflagged supplier (`409`); no cost (`400`); a document that will not parse is kept and shown; receiving a cancelled or superseded notice (`409`); a correction after the stock is gone (`409`); a closed period (`409`).
  - Override: refused excess marked refused instead; a manager corrects; a second person approves a delivery above the ceiling and a large correction (`DirectDeliveryIT.aCeilingHoldsItForASecondPerson`, `ReceiptCorrectionIT.aLargeCorrectionNeedsASecondPerson`).
  - Isolation: other business's staff of every role and a shopper on each object; a manager held to another store; the supplier rule stays inside its business (the three `TenantIsolationIT` classes, `StoreScopeIT`).
  - Edge: an amended notice before and after receiving; an unadvised container; a lot that differs; two receipts against one notice; a foreign-currency supplier; a store in another zone at the end of the expected day; a redelivered `GoodsReceiptAdjusted`; the same delivery note twice.
  - Audit: notice versions, events, variances, deliveries and corrections append-only and readable (`ShippingNoticeIT.theRecordIsAppendOnly`); alerts raised by repeated deliveries or corrections (`DirectDeliveryAlertIT`).
