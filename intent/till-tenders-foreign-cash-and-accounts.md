## Problem

Two things a grocer is asked in the first week. **A customer offers foreign cash**: a shop near a border, a port or an airport takes euros or dollars at the counter, works out the change by hand at whatever rate the cashier remembers, and the till records a home-currency sale it cannot reconcile, so the drawer is "over" in one currency and "short" in another and nobody can tell rate error from theft. **A business customer wants to buy on account**: a café, a caterer or a school buys every week and pays monthly; the shop keeps a notebook. The platform has neither: a tender is a method and an amount in the sale's currency, and a customer's only stored balance is store credit, which the shop owes *them*. Oracle's Xstore treats both as ordinary tenders (foreign currency with a rate table and change in home currency; house account with a limit, statement and payment).

## Outcome

- **Foreign cash is a tender converted at the till at the business's own rate.** The sale, its price, tax and total stay in the home currency; the customer hands over dollars; the till applies them at the business's rate and gives **change in the home currency**. The receipt says exactly that. The cashier never types a rate.
- **The drawer is counted in each currency**, so a foreign shortfall and a home shortfall are told apart and a rate error cannot hide a theft.
- **A business customer can hold an account with a credit limit.** A sale is charged to it as a tender, refused over the limit unless a manager steps in, refunded back to it, paid down by receipts at a till or in the back office, and shown on a statement.
- **The books follow:** foreign cash is cash in tills at its home value (and any difference at the bank is an exchange difference); an account sale is debtors, a payment reduces them, a write-off is a bad debt.
- **Both are off until a business switches them on**, per store for foreign cash and per customer for accounts.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **cashier** (takes foreign cash, charges to an account, takes an account payment in cash or card), the **store manager** (steps in over a limit, holds an account, records other payments), the **owner** (opens accounts, sets limits, adjusts, sets the foreign currencies and the rates), the **finance user** (statements, write-offs), the **account customer** (never signed in here).
- **Channels:** POS · back-office (Customers → Accounts) · Finance. Online: neither (online tenders are cashless card, UPI or wallet: `PAYMENT_ONLINE_CASHLESS` stays, and a sale on account is refused online, `400 PAYMENT_ON_ACCOUNT_AT_TILL_ONLY`).
- **Scope:** foreign cash is per store (which currencies it accepts); an account is per business (a customer record), charged at any store the customer may buy at. A store-held caller acts only at their stores (`requireStoreAccess`, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`); opening, limits and settings are business-wide (`BusinessWide.require`, `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`) for the caller.
- **Roles that can write:** tenders: CASHIER, MANAGER, OWNER at the store (as any tender). Opening an account, changing its limit, holding, releasing and closing it: OWNER, or a business-wide MANAGER holding the new permission `accounts.manage` (default for a manager; a custom role can narrow it). An adjustment or write-off: the same, within the approvals ceiling. An account payment: CASHIER, MANAGER, OWNER for cash or card at the caller's own till session, MANAGER or OWNER for a bank transfer or other. A statement: management. The currencies a store accepts: the store's manager or a business-wide manager; the rates: as today (the business's).
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same; a statement is not emailed from a sandbox.

## Scope

- **In:** slices in build order. Slices 1 and 2 are foreign cash, 3 to 6 house accounts.
  1. **The foreign-cash tender (tenant-svc, common-service, payment-svc, order-svc, app).** A store lists the foreign currencies it accepts as cash (`stores.accepted_foreign_cash`, empty until set, in the admin change log; read by payment-svc through `TenantProfiles.Stores`, and where the list cannot be read foreign cash is **refused**, since it is a new capability). A CASH tender may name `tenderedCurrency` and `tenderedAmount` (the notes handed over) and the `rate` the till quoted the customer. The server checks the currency is accepted (`409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_CURRENCY_NOT_ACCEPTED`), takes the business's rate from `FxRates` **as at the sale's date** (a new `FxRates.rateOn(tenant, currency, date)` and the same on `Fx`), and refuses a quoted rate that is not that one (`409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_RATE_CHANGED`, the answer carrying the right rate so the till can re-quote; an offline replay is judged at the sale's own date, so a rate changed since does not refuse a sale already made). No rate at all is `409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_RATE_MISSING` and refuses only this tender. The home value is `Fx.toHome(tenderedAmount, rate, home)`, rounded to the home currency's minor units; the tender **applies** at most what is still due (`amount`, the customer may pay part in foreign cash and the rest another way), and the **change is the home value less what was applied, given in the home currency**. The tender row keeps `tendered_currency`, `tendered_amount`, `rate`, `rate_effective_from` and `change_given` beside the applied `amount`; `PaymentCaptured` carries the same (appended, `eventId` unchanged); order-svc prints the receipt line *Paid USD 50.00 at 0.7900 = 39.50, change 0.50* from it. Foreign cash is **cash for every other rule**: the store's method switch, the legal cash limit (counted at the home value handed over, so a split into notes of another currency cannot walk round it), and the approvals step-up `sales.large-cash-tender` (measured in home value). It is only ever CASH: a foreign card, wallet or cheque is not this tender (`400 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_CASH_ONLY`). **A price is never charged in another currency:** the sale's prices, tax, total and receipt due stay in the home currency; the foreign notes are a way of paying it, converted at the till. A **refund** of a sale paid this way is paid in the home currency; the foreign notes are never given back.
  2. **The drawer counted in each currency (payment-svc, app).** This extends the pure `CashExpectation` of [till-sessions-and-registers](till-sessions-and-registers.md) by currency: the **home drawer** expects float + home cash tenders − change given on foreign tenders − cash refunds + pay-ins − pay-outs − drops (a foreign tender's applied amount is *not* in the home drawer: the notes are); each **foreign drawer** expects the notes tendered in that currency (less foreign drops and transfers, [store-cash-and-banking](store-cash-and-banking.md)). `CloseTillRequest` takes `counts[{currency, amount}]` (the existing single count is the home one); the session keeps `over_short` per currency in a small append-only `till_session_counts`, and the close, the X report and the Z report answer each currency's expected, counted and difference. The **tolerance and the approval ceiling** of the close are tested on the home-value total, each foreign difference valued at the business's rate in force (`Fx`); a currency with no rate is left out of the test and named in the answer (`unvalued`), never guessed. A foreign drop, pickup, transfer and deposit line carries its currency ([store-cash-and-banking](store-cash-and-banking.md)).
  3. **House accounts: open, limit, hold (customer-svc, app).** An account belongs to a customer record (one per customer): `POST /customers/{id}/house-account {creditLimit, termsDays?, poRequired?, holderKind: BUSINESS|INDIVIDUAL}`. The **credit limit is required** (zero is allowed and means no credit yet; a missing limit is not "unlimited", because credit is the one place the platform refuses to default to generous); `termsDays` is the business's payment terms and drives what is *overdue*; `poRequired` makes a purchase order reference mandatory on each charge. An account for a private individual is refused (`409 ACCOUNT_INDIVIDUALS_NOT_ENABLED`) until the business turns on `allow_individual_accounts` (off until set), because credit to consumers is regulated in some places and the platform assumes no country. A limit above the caller's ceiling needs the approvals action `customer.account-limit` (tiered, the limit measured in home currency; no ceiling until the business sets one). A manager may hold an account (no charge while `ON_HOLD`, payments still taken) and release it; an account is closed only at a zero balance (`409 ACCOUNT_BALANCE_NOT_ZERO`). Every change is a row in the append-only `house_account_events` with who, when and why. `GET /customers/{id}/house-account` answers balance, limit, **available credit** and status to any staff at the store (the till needs it); the ledger and statement are management's.
  4. **Charging an account at the till, and refunds back to it (payment-svc, customer-svc, purchase-svc, order-svc, app).** A tender of method `ON_ACCOUNT` (a store's method switch, off until set) names the `customerId`, which payment-svc checks against the sale's customer (`409 PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_CUSTOMER_MISMATCH`, through its `OrderClient`, failing closed) and, when the account needs one, the PO reference in `reference` (`400 ACCOUNT_PO_REQUIRED`). payment-svc calls customer-svc's `POST /customers/{id}/house-account/charges` **before** recording the tender, idempotent per order (`Ids.derived(orderId, "account")`, the store-credit pattern), so a refusal or an unreachable customer-svc rejects the tender rather than inflating the sale's paid amount (`503 PAYMENT_CUSTOMER_UNAVAILABLE`); **a sale on account is never guessed at when the ledger cannot be reached**. customer-svc refuses an unknown, held or closed account (`404 ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, `409 ACCOUNT_ON_HOLD`, `409 ACCOUNT_CLOSED`) and a charge that would pass the limit (`422 ACCOUNT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, the answer naming limit, balance and asked); a manager may cover an over-limit charge by stepping up on the cashier's till (approvals action `sales.account-over-limit`, measured as the amount over the limit; the token travels on the tender and customer-svc consumes it; `403 AUTHORITY_EXCEEDED` when the approver's ceiling does not reach). The charge is a `CHARGE` row in the append-only `house_account_ledger`. purchase-svc maps `ON_ACCOUNT` to a new control account **`1110 Customer accounts`** (confirmed while building, added if missing) in `SalesPosting.controlFor`, so the tender is Dr `1110` / Cr sales clearing, and a **refund** of an on-account tender (`PaymentRefunded` method `ON_ACCOUNT`) credits the account again (a `CREDIT` ledger row, once per event id) and posts Cr `1110`. The sale is an ordinary sale with its VAT at the sale; a business buyer's invoice is the existing one (readiness row 18.9, order-svc `sales_invoices`); the till offers *Issue invoice* after an account sale.
  5. **Payments and statements (payment-svc, customer-svc, purchase-svc, notification-svc, app).** `POST /payments/account-receipts {customerId, amount, currency, method: CASH|CARD|BANK_TRANSFER|OTHER, reference?, storeId, tillSessionId?}` (`Idempotency-Key`): a cash or card receipt taken at a till belongs to that till session (cash counts in the drawer's expected cash as cash in; a card receipt follows the store's terminal rule, [card-payments](card-payments.md) slice 1), a bank transfer or other is a back-office receipt with no session; each is an append-only `account_receipts` row and announces `AccountReceiptRecorded`; customer-svc credits a `PAYMENT` ledger row once per event id, purchase-svc posts Dr the method's control account (cash in tills, card clearing or bank) / Cr `1110`. An overpayment leaves a credit balance, shown as such; returning it in money is not built (below). `GET /customers/{id}/house-account/statement?from&to` (management): the opening balance, each charge (date, store, short receipt reference, PO), payment and credit, the closing balance, what is **overdue** (a charge older than `termsDays`, first in first out against the payments; with no terms nothing is overdue) and the account's limit; `POST …/statement/send {to?}` emails it through notification-svc in the platform's own words (the address the customer holds; another address is flagged for management as receipt copies are).
  6. **Adjustments, write-offs and the overdue alert (customer-svc, purchase-svc).** `POST /customers/{id}/house-account/adjustments {amount (signed), reason}` (`Idempotency-Key`, a reason required, `400 ACCOUNT_ADJUSTMENT_REASON_REQUIRED`) writes an `ADJUSTMENT` ledger row and announces `HouseAccountAdjusted`; purchase-svc posts a write-off Dr **`6560 Bad debts`** / Cr `1110` (and an increase the reverse), redated in a closed month. Above the business's ceiling a second person agrees: approvals action `customer.account-adjustment`. Metric `house_accounts.overdue_value` on [exception-alerts](exception-alerts.md) (customer-svc, sweep only, subject the account holder, the sum overdue against the business's own threshold) tells the owner and manager which account has fallen behind; **nothing is held automatically**, a hold is a person's decision.
- **Born with its checks** (each is also an Acceptance line):
  - *Who and where:* as above; a store-held caller charges and takes payments only at their stores; opening, limits and holds business-wide.
  - *Authority:* `customer.account-limit`, `sales.account-over-limit`, `customer.account-adjustment` (new); foreign cash rides the existing `sales.large-cash-tender`; none has a default figure.
  - *Retry safety:* an `Idempotency-Key` on account opening, the charge (per order), receipts, adjustments and statement sends; a retry answers the first and posts nothing twice; a foreign-cash tender is keyed as any tender.
  - *Isolation:* another business's staff of every role, even naming our customer, account, order or store id, and a shopper, get `404` and nothing moves; a store-held cashier cannot charge or take a payment at another store; a charge never touches another business's ledger.
  - *The record:* tenders, account events, the ledger, receipts and till counts are append-only with who and when; a rate used is stored on the tender.
  - *Abuse:* `house_accounts.overdue_value` (new); charges over the limit and adjustments are approvals facts (`ApprovalDecided`); write-offs by one person are the alerts page's to count.
  - *Refusals:* stable codes below.
  - *Location-neutral:* no currency is home, foreign or accepted by the platform; no rate is a default (only the business's table); no payment term, credit limit, statement day or legal regime is assumed; minor units come from `Fx.minorUnits`; a private-individual account waits for the business's own choice.
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Room charge (a hotel property system).** It is an external party: a PMS interface as a driver behind an interface with a `SIMULATED` driver and stub-backed tests of each real driver's request shape ([docs/ACCOUNTING-CONNECTORS.md](../docs/ACCOUNTING-CONNECTORS.md)), for a business (a hotel shop) nobody has asked for. The house account is the tender it would ride on.
  - **Cheque as a tender.** A cheque is a promise, not cash: it needs a register, a clearing period and a bounced-cheque path (a payment reversed days later). None of that exists; a grocer's cheque is taken as a bank-transfer receipt once cleared. Building it would put a half-tender in the till.
  - **A private-label credit card (PLCC).** It is the card issuer's product with its own authorisation network, a driver and a contract; it belongs with card-payments' drivers when a business signs one.
  - **A spread or margin on the rate, or a cashier's override of it.** The rate is the business's table; a margin is the business setting the rate it wants. A cashier who could type a rate is the risk this design exists to remove.
  - **Foreign card, wallet or cheque; charging in another currency; giving foreign change.** Change is home currency (the brief and industry practice); a price is never charged in another currency.
  - **Returning an account's credit balance in money, and interest or late fees.** Refunds of a balance and dunning letters are a business's own policy and need numbers the platform will not choose.
  - **An automatic hold of an overdue account, and automatic monthly statements.** A hold is a person's decision (the alert says who is late); a statement schedule needs a per-business day and channel and is a later step.
  - **Statement invoice numbers.** customer-svc does not hold order-svc's invoices; the statement names the receipt reference, and each invoice is its own document.
  - **Several authorised buyers per account, and a per-buyer limit.** One account is one customer record.

## Data and flow

- **Owned by tenant-svc:** `stores.accepted_foreign_cash` (a list of ISO currency codes; the change log records it). The rates are the existing `fx_rates` (append-only, dated); `FxRates.rateOn` reads the one in force on a date.
- **Owned by payment-svc:** `payment_tenders` gains `tendered_currency`, `tendered_amount`, `rate`, `rate_effective_from`, `change_given` (all nullable; a domestic tender has none); `till_session_counts` (append-only: session, currency, counted, expected, over/short); `account_receipts` (append-only: customer, amount, currency, method, reference, store, till session, by, idempotency key unique per business); the `ON_ACCOUNT` method in `VALID_METHODS`; the client call to customer-svc.
- **Owned by customer-svc:** `house_accounts` (customer unique per business, status ACTIVE|ON_HOLD|CLOSED, `credit_limit`, currency, `terms_days`, `po_required`, `holder_kind`), `house_account_events` (append-only), `house_account_ledger` (append-only: kind CHARGE|PAYMENT|CREDIT|ADJUSTMENT, signed amount, currency, order or return, receipt reference, PO, source event or key unique per business, by, reason), `house_account_settings` (`allow_individual_accounts`). The **balance is never stored**: it is the sum of the ledger (pure `HouseAccountBalance`), so it cannot drift; **overdue and ageing are pure `Overdue`** (first in first out against payments, by terms).
- **Owned by purchase-svc:** accounts `1110 Customer accounts` and `6560 Bad debts`; `ON_ACCOUNT` in `controlFor`; posting sources for account receipts and adjustments; consumers of `AccountReceiptRecorded`, `HouseAccountAdjusted`, and the existing `PaymentCaptured` and `PaymentRefunded` (now with the method), idempotent on the event id, event-fed and so redated in a closed month.
- **Needs from other services:** rates and the home currency from tenant-svc (`FxRates`, `TenantProfiles`, cached, never joined); the store's accepted list (tenant-svc); the sale's customer from order-svc (payment-svc's `OrderClient`); the account's limit and balance from customer-svc by REST (breaker, timeout, `@Retry` only because the charge is idempotent); the approver from iam-svc's step-up consume.
- **Events published:** `PaymentCaptured` (foreign fields added at the end), `AccountReceiptRecorded` (`storeql.payment.account-receipt-recorded`), `HouseAccountAdjusted` (`storeql.customer.house-account-adjusted`); each with `eventId` last, tenant, store where there is one, customer id, amount and currency, no name.
- **Endpoints:** payment-svc `POST /payments/account-receipts`, `GET /payments/account-receipts?customerId&after&limit`; customer-svc as in slices 3 to 6 (`/customers/{id}/house-account…`, `GET /admin/house-accounts?status&overdue&after&limit` for the Accounts screen), `GET/PUT /admin/house-accounts/settings` (OWNER); tenant-svc `PATCH /admin/stores/{id}` takes `acceptedForeignCash`.
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** open, charge, receipt, adjustment, statement send, hold and release.
- **New error codes:** payment-svc `409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_CURRENCY_NOT_ACCEPTED`, `409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_RATE_MISSING`, `409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_RATE_CHANGED`, `400 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_AMOUNT_INVALID`, `400 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_CASH_ONLY`, `409 PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_CUSTOMER_MISMATCH`, `400 PAYMENT_ON_ACCOUNT_AT_TILL_ONLY`, `400 ACCOUNT_RECEIPT_INVALID`; customer-svc `404 ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, `409 ACCOUNT_EXISTS`, `400 ACCOUNT_LIMIT_INVALID`, `409 ACCOUNT_INDIVIDUALS_NOT_ENABLED`, `409 ACCOUNT_ON_HOLD`, `409 ACCOUNT_CLOSED`, `422 ACCOUNT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `400 ACCOUNT_PO_REQUIRED`, `409 ACCOUNT_BALANCE_NOT_ZERO`, `400 ACCOUNT_ADJUSTMENT_REASON_REQUIRED`; existing `403 AUTHORITY_EXCEEDED`, `409 STEPUP_SELF`, `403 STEPUP_INVALID`, `503 PAYMENT_CUSTOMER_UNAVAILABLE`, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`, `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`, `409 PAYMENT_CASH_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** the sale, its tax and the account are in the home currency; a foreign tender is recorded in its own currency with the rate and converted only through `Fx` (rounded to the target's minor units); no rate means the tender is refused, never guessed. The account is in one currency, the business's home (`409` for another). `NUMERIC(14,4)`, `BigDecimal`.
- **Ledger postings:** foreign cash: the tender posts Dr `1210` for the applied home amount as any cash tender (the notes' home value less the change); the difference on foreign cash arises only when it is banked ([store-cash-and-banking](store-cash-and-banking.md), `6540`). House account: Dr `1110` / Cr clearing at the sale; Dr cash, card clearing or bank / Cr `1110` at a receipt; Cr `1110` at a refund; Dr `6560` / Cr `1110` at a write-off.
- **Dates:** UTC instants; the rate is the one in force on the **sale's date**; *overdue* counts calendar days from the charge in the store's zone.
- **Plan limits:** none.

## Constraints

- **Golden rules:** 1 (the ledger and limit are customer-svc's, reached by REST; the debtors control is purchase-svc's by events); 3; 6 and 7 (the charge idempotent per order, receipts and adjustments per event id); 8 (tenders, the ledger, events, receipts and counts are append-only, a mistake is a credit or an adjustment row); 11; 13; 14; 15.
- **Fail closed for credit, open for the sale:** an unreadable account, limit or rate refuses *this tender* (the cashier takes another way to pay); it never refuses a sale that has another tender, and never a refund.
- **Existing tenants and flow guards:** no store accepts foreign cash and no customer has an account until the business sets them; `flow-guard-comprehensive` and `flow-guard-runtime` take cash and card tenders only and must stay green.
- **Erasure:** a customer with a non-zero house-account balance is not erased until it is settled ([privacy-requests](privacy-requests.md): a debt owed is a record the business must keep); the ledger keeps the customer id and nothing personal.
- **Backups:** plain tables, no function in a CHECK.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A foreign-cash tender converts at the business's rate on the sale's date, rounds to the home minor units, applies at most what is due, and gives change in the home currency — pure `ForeignTenderTest` (a two-decimal, a zero-decimal and a three-decimal home currency; exact change; part paid; overpaid), `ForeignCashIT.convertsAtTheBusinessRate`
- [ ] The cashier's quoted rate is checked: a stale rate is `409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_RATE_CHANGED` carrying the right one, no rate is `409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_RATE_MISSING`, a currency the store does not accept is `409 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_CURRENCY_NOT_ACCEPTED`, a non-cash method `400 PAYMENT_FOREIGN_CASH_ONLY` — `ForeignCashIT.refusals`
- [ ] An offline sale replayed after the rate changed is judged at its own date and accepted — `ForeignCashIT.offlineReplayUsesTheSaleDate`
- [ ] Foreign cash counts at its home value toward the legal cash limit and the large-cash step-up — `ForeignCashIT.countsTowardTheCashLimit`, `.largeCashStepUp`
- [ ] The receipt shows the foreign amount, the rate, the applied amount and the change; no price is charged in the foreign currency — `ForeignCashReceiptIT`, order-svc `ReceiptForeignTenderIT`
- [ ] A refund of a foreign-tendered sale is paid in the home currency — `ForeignCashIT.refundIsInHomeCurrency`
- [ ] The home drawer expects float + home cash − change on foreign tenders − refunds + pay-ins − pay-outs − drops, and each foreign drawer the notes tendered; the close counts each and answers each difference — pure `CashExpectationForeignTest`, `TillCloseForeignIT.eachCurrencyCounted`
- [ ] A foreign difference is valued at the rate in force for the tolerance and the approval ceiling; a currency with no rate is named `unvalued` and left out — `TillCloseForeignIT.valuedAtTheRateOrUnvalued`
- [ ] A store with no accepted list refuses foreign cash, and an unreadable list refuses it too; every existing tender is unchanged — `ForeignCashIT.offUntilSet`, `.unreadableListRefuses`
- [ ] An account is opened with a required limit (zero allowed), an individual's is refused until enabled, the limit above the ceiling needs the second person, and a hold blocks charges but not payments — `HouseAccountIT.openLimitHold`, `.individualsOffUntilEnabled`, `.limitApprovalKeyCustomerAccountLimit`
- [ ] A charge within the limit is recorded once per order, an over-limit charge is `422 ACCOUNT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, a manager's step-up covers it within their ceiling and beyond it is `403 AUTHORITY_EXCEEDED`, and a held or closed account is refused — `AccountChargeIT.withinTheLimit`, `.overTheLimitStepUp`, `.heldAndClosed`
- [ ] A charge with customer-svc down rejects the tender and records nothing; a retried tender does not charge twice — `AccountChargeIT.unreachableRejectsTheTender`, `.retryChargesOnce`

## Decisions

- (2026-09-30 evening, reconciliation of the second set of pages) **House accounts, store credit and stored value are three different things.** A house account is money a *customer owes the business* (an asset, `1110`); store credit and gift cards are money the *business owes* (liabilities, `2320`, `2310`) and carry the expiry and lapse rules of [stored-value-lifecycle](stored-value-lifecycle.md). A house account never expires, is never lapsed or surrendered, and takes none of that page's policies. A refund to a customer with a house account whose sale was on account goes back to the account (this page); the refund methods `ORIGINAL`, `STORE_CREDIT`, `GIFT_CARD` of [return-controls](return-controls.md) are unchanged. A merge of two customers ([customer-identity](customer-identity.md)) refuses when **both** have a house account (`409 CUSTOMER_MERGE_BOTH_HAVE_ACCOUNTS`) and otherwise moves the one account, its ledger rows repointed by a paired `MERGE_OUT`/`MERGE_IN` entry; erasure anonymises the customer link and RETAINS the ledger amounts under the accounting retention floor. The account ledger accounts are `1110 Customer accounts`, `6560 Bad debts`, `6540 Exchange differences` (see the plan's single account list).
