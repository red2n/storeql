## Problem

The cash report a manager relies on at close is wrong in ways that hide money. The session X report and the close (Z) both take every payment and refund of the whole business between the moment the till was opened and now: `sumTendersByMethod` is handed the store and never uses it, and `sumRefundsByMethod` is not given one. A second store trading, or a second till at the same store, lands in the first till's expected cash and its over/short. The report also leaves out paid-in and paid-out cash for the session (the day report counts them, the session does not), and the day report counts its day in UTC rather than the store's own, and re-running it overwrites the stored row it calls immutable. A store has no notion of *which* till: two tills at one store are told apart only by who opened them, and neither the sale, the cash report nor the receipt says which till took it. A manager can close a colleague's drawer at Z, but the sign-in session of a cashier who walked off (iam-svc `pos_sessions`) can be ended by any signed-in member of the business, cashier included, and nothing records who. A parked sale can sit for weeks and nothing links the sale finally rung up to the parked one. Deposit refunds and gift-card issue have weaker controls than the rest of the till: a cashier can hand out any container refund of a listed material and create stored value with no sale behind it.

## Outcome

- A store can have **registers** (tills) with a code and a name ("Till 2", "Kiosk"), and the session, the sale, the cash report and the receipt name the register they belong to.
- **The X report and the close count only their own till's money**, and the close shows expected cash (float + cash sales − cash refunds + pay-ins − pay-outs − drops), counted, and **over/short**, with a note when a business asks for one.
- **A manager at the store can end a colleague's stuck session** and it says who did; a cashier cannot end someone else's.
- **A parked sale expires** when the business says, is never lost silently, and the sale finally rung up says which parked sale it was.
- The offline queue is **proved end to end** (network cut, sales taken, network back, each replayed once).
- **Container refunds** can be limited to the kinds the business sold; **gift-card value** has a sale, or a manager and a reason, behind it; the **receipt copy log** is read by those who should and shows an address only to them.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the cashier (opens and works a register), the store manager (closes, supervises, reads reports, names registers), the owner (business-wide settings), the platform administrator (sweeps).
- **Channels:** POS · back-office (Registers, Cash, Parked sales) · reports.
- **Scope:** per store and per register. A caller held to stores sees and acts at those stores only (`requireStoreAccess`, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`); business-wide settings need `BusinessWide.require`.
- **Roles that can write:** registers: MANAGER and OWNER at the store (a manager held to other stores is refused); opening a session: CASHIER, MANAGER, OWNER; closing and reading the X report: MANAGER, OWNER with `till.manage` (as today); ending another's session: MANAGER, OWNER at the store; the settings: OWNER.
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same.

## Scope

- **In:** slices in build order (the first is a defect fix and ships before the rest):
  1. **A cash report that counts only its own store's money, and counts it once (payment-svc).** *Defect being fixed:* `CashManagementService.buildReport` gives the session's store to `sumTendersByMethod`, which ignores it, and gives `sumRefundsByMethod` none. Both are now filtered by tenant then store (`payment_tenders.store_id`; `refund_tenders.store_id`, which wave 1 writes), by the session's window, and refunds by the store their payment was taken at (an exchange, at the store it was made), the rule wave 1 recorded. The expected cash moves into **one pure class** (`CashExpectation`: float + cash tenders − cash refunds + pay-ins − pay-outs − drops) used by the session X report, the close and the day report, so they can no longer disagree (today the session leaves out pay-ins and pay-outs and the day report leaves out cash refunds). The day report counts its date in the **store's own zone** (`TenantProfiles.Stores.zoneOf`, the same source order-svc's windows use) instead of `atStartOfDay()` at UTC, and is **generated once**: a second request for a finished day answers the stored report (`200`, `regenerated: false`) instead of overwriting it, and a day with a session still open is `409 Z_REPORT_SESSIONS_OPEN`. Existing `z_reports` rows keep what they have. This slice alone still counts by the session's time window, so two tills open at once at one store are not yet told apart: slice 3 does that.
  2. **Registers (tenant-svc, then the projections).** A store has registers: code (short, unique per store, "2"), name, status ACTIVE or RETIRED (never deleted: sessions and sales name them). Managed by managers at the store, in the admin change log (`REGISTER_ADDED`, `REGISTER_RENAMED`, `REGISTER_RETIRED`). tenant-svc announces `RegisterChanged`; iam-svc, payment-svc and order-svc each keep a small projection (id, store, code, name, status) to validate against, never a join. **A store with no registers behaves exactly as today** (a session has no register). Where a store has registers, opening a session names one: `400 TILL_REGISTER_REQUIRED`; a retired or foreign one is `409 TILL_REGISTER_INVALID`; a register already in a live session is `409 TILL_REGISTER_IN_USE` (one live session per register, by a partial unique index). The code and name are **copied onto the session and the sale** when they happen, so a later rename never rewrites what a receipt said.
  3. **Sessions carry their money (payment-svc, order-svc, app).** The till sends its session id with a cash or card capture, a refund and a container refund; payment-svc checks the session is OPEN, the store and tenant match, and writes `till_session_id` and `register_id` on the tender or refund tender. The X report and close then sum **by session**, exactly, whatever else is open at the store (`basis: SESSION`); money with no session (online, back-office, older rows) is counted by store and window and shown on its own line as **not at a till** (`basis: WINDOW`), so it is never dropped and never mixed into a drawer. A cash refund is counted in the drawer of the session that paid it out, not the sale's. The close takes an optional **variance note** (`CloseTillRequest.note`, up to 500 characters) and the closed session keeps `over_short`, `note`, `closed_by`. A business may set a **variance tolerance** (in its home currency, off until set): when the absolute over/short is above it, a note is required (`400 TILL_VARIANCE_NOTE_REQUIRED`). Above the business's approval ceiling the close needs the approvals mechanism's action `till.close-variance` (a person other than the closer, holding `till.manage`, agrees); designed once on [approvals](approvals.md), named here. Repeated variances by one cashier are the metric `till.variance` on [exception-alerts](exception-alerts.md). `TillSessionClosed` is published (below) so reporting and alerts read it.
  4. **A supervisor's reach and the list of sessions (iam-svc, payment-svc).** `DELETE /auth/pos/sessions/{id}`: today any signed-in member of the business may end any session (`PosSessionService.end` checks the tenant and nothing else; `listActive` lists everyone's to anyone). Now the session's own owner ends it as before (`204`, idempotent); anyone else needs MANAGER or OWNER **with access to the session's store** and gives a reason, and it records `ended_by` and `end_reason` (`POS_SESSION_ENDED_BY_SUPERVISOR` in the trail, and the ended cashier's refresh tokens for that session are revoked and their push session kicked); a cashier naming another's is `403 POS_SESSION_NOT_YOURS`. `GET /auth/pos/sessions` is management only and store-scoped. (A supervisor *borrowing* a colleague's session is a different act: approvals key `till.session-take-over`, a step-up, owned by iam-svc.) payment-svc gains `GET /admin/cash/till-sessions?storeId&status=OPEN` (management, store-scoped) so a manager sees drawers left open after their cashier's sign-in session ended, and closes each with the existing close (they already can: `close` is management's, checked against the session's store). Ending a sign-in session does **not** close the drawer: cash is counted by a person.
  5. **Parked sales expire and are linked (order-svc, app).** A business may set how long a parked sale lives (`parked_sale_ttl_hours`, off until set, no number assumed); a scheduled sweep marks older open ones `EXPIRED` (kept, never deleted, `expired_at`) and lists them for the store's managers under **Needs attention**. `parked_sales.order_id` already exists ("the order created on resume") and is never written: a new `POST /pos/parked-sales/{id}/complete {orderId}` (CASHIER, MANAGER, OWNER, store access; idempotent) links the parked sale to the order rung up from it, checks the order is at the same store and was placed after the resume, and records it; `resumed_by` (wave 1) says who picked it up, `completed` says which order it became. A sale resumed and never completed within the same TTL returns to the open list flagged **abandoned resume**. The till calls `POST …/resume` (wave 1 flagged that the till still reads then deletes) then `complete`.
  6. **The offline queue proved end to end (app, order-svc).** See Acceptance: a Dart integration test drives the real `OfflineQueue` and the real HTTP client against a local server it switches off and on (including dropping a response after the server has accepted the request), and an order-svc IT replays a sale twice with the same keys. This is a test slice; no behaviour changes.
  7. **Container refunds and the kinds a business sold (order-svc).** A business setting **`container_refunds_sold_kinds_only`** (off until set): when on, a container refund line (material and volume) is accepted only if the business has sold that kind (a row in `order_deposits` for the tenant, optionally within `container_refund_lookback_days`, null = ever): `409 ORDER_CONTAINER_NOT_SOLD_HERE`. Off is the default because most deposit schemes oblige a retailer to take back any scheme container whoever sold it, so the platform assumes neither; a business under a scheme that limits it to what it sold turns it on. The existing limits stay as they are (`ORDER_CONTAINER_COUNT_TOO_MANY`, `ORDER_CONTAINER_NOT_IN_SCHEME`). How many refunds one till gives in a shift, and how much deposit money they return, are the alerts page's metrics `deposit_refunds.count` and `deposit_refunds.value` (a window set to the shift's length); nothing new is capped here.
  8. **Gift-card value has a sale behind it (order-svc, payment-svc, purchase-svc).** A card is loaded in one of two ways. **By a sale:** a `GIFT_CARD_LOAD` line (amount, and optionally an existing code to reload) on an ordinary order; the card is issued or topped up when the order's payment is captured (the `PaymentCaptured` event, once per event, on the order's own transaction of the consumer), so value can never exist without money taken; this is what a cashier uses. **By a manager, for a reason that is not a sale** (goodwill, a promotion, compensation, a migration): `POST /gift-cards` and `…/reload` with a required `reason` from that list, MANAGER or OWNER only; a cashier gets `403 GIFT_CARD_NEEDS_SALE`. Above the business's ceiling a **manager's** issue or reload needs the approvals actions `sales.gift-card-issue` / `sales.gift-card-reload` (the ceiling is a business setting on the approvals page, unlimited until set); a card loaded by a paid sale line has money behind it and needs no approval, and a cashier has no authority for a direct issue at all (`403 GIFT_CARD_NEEDS_SALE` comes first). When the sale has a customer the card is linked to them (`gift_cards.customer_id`, [stored-value-lifecycle](stored-value-lifecycle.md) uses it to warn a holder before a lapse). The books: a card sold is Dr the tender / Cr the gift-card liability; a goodwill card is Dr a goodwill or marketing expense / Cr the liability, the chart confirmed in purchase-svc while building (added if missing), as return-controls did for store-credit and gift-card refunds.
  9. **The receipt copy log (order-svc).** Access is settled by wave 1 (`ReceiptAccessIT`: staff at the sale's store make and read copies, management reads the log, another business `404`). Now: the copy log **masks the address** (`j***@example.com`) for everyone but management at the sale's store; a copy emailed to an address other than the customer's on file (or to one where the sale has no customer) is logged `ADDRESS_DIFFERS` for management; reprints per cashier are the metric `receipts.copies` on [exception-alerts](exception-alerts.md). A shopper reprinting their own online receipt stays out (a shopper-facing route is its own small feature).
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Fiscal signing per register.** The TSE device stays one per store (`tse_devices` unique per store). A regime that requires a client id per register (KassenSichV's `ClientID`) adds it inside that regime's own slice with its own tests; the register's code is on the receipt now, which is what that later step reads.
  - **Register hardware pairing** (which printer, drawer or terminal belongs to which register): `card_terminals` are per store today and keep to that; pairing is its own feature.
  - **A drawer's shared use by two cashiers at once.** One session per register; a hand-over is a close and a new open, which is what cash-control practice asks for.
  - **Auto-closing a drawer when a sign-in session ends.** Cash is counted by a person; the session is listed and a manager closes it.
  - **A maximum age on an open drawer.** The list shows how long it has been open; a limit is a business rule nobody asked for.
  - **Refusing a late offline replay on price or stock.** The sale happened and the customer paid: see Already there (a replay within the grace is recorded and flagged, never refused); this page adds no new refusal.
  - **Per-day or per-customer caps on deposit refunds.** That is exception-alerts' metric, not a hard stop that would strand a customer at the till.
  - **Shopper self-service receipt reprint.** Separate small feature.

## Data and flow

- **Owned by tenant-svc:** `registers` (id, tenant, store, code, name, status ACTIVE|RETIRED, created by/at, retired by/at; unique on tenant + store + lower(code)); admin-audit types added to `Audit`. Events: `RegisterChanged` (`storeql.tenant.register-changed`: tenant, store, register id, code, name, status).
- **Owned by payment-svc:**
  - `till_sessions` gains `register_id`, `register_code`, `register_name` (nullable), `closed_by`, `note`; a partial unique index on `(tenant_id, register_id) WHERE status = 'OPEN'`; `payment_tenders` and `refund_tenders` gain `till_session_id`, `register_id` (nullable: online and back-office rows have none); `z_reports` keeps its shape and is inserted once.
  - Local `registers` projection (validation only).
  - A `till_settings` row per business: `variance_tolerance` (nullable, home currency; **off until set**).
  - `TillSessionClosed` (`storeql.payment.till-session-closed`: session, store, register, opened by, closed by, float, expected, counted, over/short, note; consumer: reporting-svc for the cash report; the `till.variance` alert is evaluated by payment-svc itself at the close, through `AlertRaiser`, not by a consumer ([exception-alerts](exception-alerts.md)).
  - Tests use the pure `CashExpectation` (no database).
- **Owned by iam-svc:** `pos_sessions` gains `register_id`, `register_code`, `ended_by`, `end_reason` (a session started with the register the till names, same validation from its projection); audit `POS_SESSION_ENDED_BY_SUPERVISOR`.
- **Owned by order-svc:** `parked_sales` gains `status` EXPIRED, `expired_at`, `completed_order_id` use, `abandoned`; `orders` gains `register_id`, `register_code`, `till_session_id` (POS orders only); business settings `parked_sale_ttl_hours`, `container_refunds_sold_kinds_only`, `container_refund_lookback_days` (all off / null until set); `order_receipts` (the copy log; this page first called it `receipt_copies`) gains `address_differs`; a `GIFT_CARD_LOAD` line kind and `gift_card_load_lines` (order item, amount, target card or none, card issued/loaded id once paid); `gift_cards` gains `source` (SALE, GOODWILL, PROMOTION, COMPENSATION, MIGRATION) and `reason`.
- **Needs from other services:** register status from tenant-svc's event; the store's time zone from tenant-svc (`TenantProfiles.Stores.zoneOf`) for the day report and the parked-sale sweep's "store's morning"; the payment for a gift-card sale from payment-svc's `PaymentCaptured`.
- **Endpoints (new or changed):** `GET/POST /admin/stores/{storeId}/registers`, `PATCH …/{id}` (rename, retire) (tenant-svc); `POST /admin/cash/till-sessions` takes `registerId`; `GET /admin/cash/till-sessions?storeId&status`; `POST …/{id}/close` takes `note`; `GET …/{id}/x-report` and the close answer `basis`, `notAtTill`, `overShort`, `note`; `POST /admin/cash/z-report` answers the stored one; `DELETE /auth/pos/sessions/{id}` with `reason`; `POST /pos/parked-sales/{id}/complete`; `GET/PUT /admin/orders/pos-settings` (parked TTL, container rule) OWNER; `GET/PUT /admin/cash/till-settings` (tolerance) OWNER.
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** open session, close, complete parked sale, gift-card load and direct issue/reload, container refund (exists), register create.
- **New error codes:** `400 TILL_REGISTER_REQUIRED`, `409 TILL_REGISTER_INVALID`, `409 TILL_REGISTER_IN_USE`, `400 TILL_VARIANCE_NOTE_REQUIRED`, `409 Z_REPORT_SESSIONS_OPEN`, `403 POS_SESSION_NOT_YOURS`, `404 PARKED_SALE_NOT_FOUND` / `409 PARKED_SALE_NOT_OPEN` (exist), `409 PARKED_SALE_ORDER_MISMATCH`, `409 ORDER_CONTAINER_NOT_SOLD_HERE`, `403 GIFT_CARD_NEEDS_SALE`, `400 GIFT_CARD_REASON_REQUIRED`, `409 REGISTER_HAS_OPEN_SESSION` (retiring a register with a live session).

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** a session's report is in the business's home currency; tenders in another currency are shown by their own currency and translated only through `Fx` where a total is needed; the variance tolerance is in the home currency. **Ledger postings:** slices 1 to 7 none (reports); slice 8 as above. Cash over/short posting to the ledger is not made here (a business's chart decides), only reported.
- **Dates:** every instant UTC (`timestamptz`); a "day" is the **store's own local day** through its zone, never UTC and never a fixed zone; DST days are 23 or 25 hours and the window is computed from the zone's own rules. The parked-sale TTL counts hours from `parked_at`.
- **Plan limits:** none.

## Constraints

- **Golden rules:** 1 (registers reach payment, iam and order services only as an event projection); 3 (tenant from the token; register and session ids are validated against the tenant first); 6 and 7; 8 (tenders, refund tenders, `cash_drops`, `cash_movements`, `z_reports`, `order_line_adjustments`, the receipt log and the trails stay append-only; a parked sale is marked, never deleted; the tender's `till_session_id` is written once at insert); 13 (`BigDecimal` everywhere; expected cash and over/short in `NUMERIC(14,4)`); 14 (UTC stored, store zone for the day).
- **Existing data:** tenders and refund tenders before this ships have no session and are counted by store and window on the "not at a till" line; nothing is rewritten. Existing open sessions have no register and stay valid; a store adopts registers when its manager adds the first.
- **Flow guards:** `flow-guard-comprehensive` and `flow-guard-runtime` touch POS sessions and must stay green: a store with no registers behaves as before, and slice 4's stricter end refuses only what a cashier could do that nobody meant them to (the guards' cashier ends their own).
- **Location-neutral:** no currency, day boundary, weekday or hour is assumed; the store's zone decides the day and DST.

## Open questions

- [x] Are registers required? → **no; a store with none behaves as before, and once it has any a session must name one** (industry standard: adopt without forcing, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Does a tender name its session? → **yes, sent by the till and validated by payment-svc; money with none is counted by store and window on its own line** (industry standard: reconcile to the drawer, never mix, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] What variance needs a note or a second person? → **the business's own tolerance (note) and ceiling (approval `till.close-variance`), off until set** (industry standard: no policy number assumed, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Who may end a colleague's sign-in session? → **a manager or owner at the store, with a reason; a cashier only their own** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] What does a parked sale do when it expires? → **it is marked EXPIRED and kept, and the store's managers see it; never deleted** (industry standard: append-only, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Must a deposit refund be for a kind the business sold? → **a business setting, off by default, because most schemes oblige acceptance of any scheme container** (industry standard: follow the scheme, let the business narrow it, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Can a cashier create gift-card value with no sale? → **no; only a sale line, or a manager with a reason, and above the ceiling an approval** (industry standard: stored value is money, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Is a late offline replay refused? → **no, within the grace it is recorded and flagged (already so)** (industry standard: the sale has happened, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
