## Problem

A shopper who buys prepacked food online cannot pick the pack up and read it. The law in several places (in the European Union Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 art. 14, with arts. 9, 13, 30 to 35) therefore requires the mandatory particulars to be available **before the purchase is concluded**: the ingredients with allergens emphasised, the nutrition declaration, the net quantity, the name and address of the food business operator, storage and use instructions, and origin where its absence would mislead or the food is one of the listed kinds. Today the platform shows the allergens and the origin, and product safety information where GPSR binds. **Ingredients are recorded on the variant as one block of text and shown to nobody; there is no nutrition at all.** A business selling packed food online is therefore in breach without any way to comply, and nothing stops a food product going on sale without what the law needs. The one that exists, the safety-information gate, covers a different regulation and a different set of facts.

## Outcome

- A food variant carries one structured **food information declaration** in a stated language: an ingredient list in which each allergen is a marked ingredient (so it can be emphasised in the shopper's language and cannot disagree with the allergen declaration), an optional share for each, the nutrition table per 100 g or 100 ml (optionally per portion, with the reference intake where the business shows it), storage and use instructions, the operator's name and address, and where the law needs it, origin.
- The storefront product page shows it **before the shopper adds to the basket**, in the shopper's language where the business has it.
- Where the business's jurisdiction requires it (as data with a citation, never a country in code), a prepacked food product **cannot be made available online without it**: refused when it is published, never silently taken off sale when it is live. It is the same gate the unit-price rule uses; there is one gate, and it tells the manager everything that is missing at once.
- Every change to a declaration is kept; on any date the business can say exactly what a shopper was shown (for a complaint or an inspector).
- A supplier's data is recorded as what the business received and is confirmed by a person of the business, who owns it from then on.
- Food sold loose at a counter gets a **counter ticket** built from the same data.

## Already there

- Allergen declaration per variant with CONTAINS / MAY_CONTAIN, and `allergen_status` keeping "declared none" apart from "nobody declared" (`V13__food_safety_and_restrictions.sql` for the allergens and the declarations, `V1__init.sql` for the status, `FoodSafetyIT`); the storefront shows it, and says "ask in store" when unknown (`allergen_summary.dart`). This page **uses** it; it does not replace it.
- `ingredients` free text on the variant (`V1__init.sql`, `ComplianceRepository`, `variant_compliance_dialog.dart`). Kept: it is the fallback the storefront shows while no structured list exists, and an existing business's data is never rewritten.
- Origin and its detail line, net content and unit, per variant (`V1__init.sql`); the unit price is pricing-svc's.
- Product safety information for online offers under GPSR, with the gate `requireListable` and the `503` when rules cannot be read (V14, `ProductSafetyIT`). The operator's name and address on this page are **not** the manufacturer of that record: the food business operator under whose name the food is marketed can be another person (art. 8), so they are separate fields with a "same as manufacturer" button in the admin form.
- `Jurisdictions.inForce(tenant, ruleCode, date)` and `inForceIn(tenant, country, ruleCode, date)` (common-service), with `legal_obligations` rows in tenant-svc carrying a citation.
- Not there: any nutrient, any structured ingredient, any language on food text, any history of a declaration (the allergen upsert keeps only `declared_by` / `declared_at` of the last change), any gate for food, any ticket.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **catalogue manager** (writes and confirms), the **owner** (answers for it), the **shopper** (reads it before buying), the **food-safety lead** (history, gaps), the **deli or counter staff** (ticket), an inspector or complainant (the as-of read).
- **Channels:** back-office (Admin > Products); ONLINE storefront (product page); counter tickets from the admin app; POS: none.
- **Scope:** per variant (a pack), per language. The catalogue belongs to the whole business, not a store: writes follow the roles that write product data today (OWNER, MANAGER through the shared admin filter); reads of the history and gaps need management; the shopper's read is public through the storefront host, tenant from the host as for allergens.
- **Roles that can write:** OWNER, MANAGER (and whoever writes variants today). **Confirming** a declaration (slice 2) is the same roles; the person who confirms is recorded and is the person who answers for it. No new permission.
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same; a sandbox business's pages are never indexed (storefront-trust).

## Scope

- **In**, in build order (each slice ships and is tested on its own):
  1. **The one publish gate, and the food rule inside it (product-svc, tenant-svc).**
     - **One gate, `ListingGate`, that every listing rule feeds.** Today `requireListable` checks safety information; [unit-pricing-and-listing-rules](unit-pricing-and-listing-rules.md) slice 3 adds the unit price "in one place with `requireListable`". This page names the one place: a pure `ListingRequirements` (each rule is a small check returning what is missing, `rule` + `item`) run by `ListingGate.require(tenant, product, variants)` from every path that makes a product available online (create, edit, import, launch, un-delist, and the variant edit that changes the inputs). **Whichever of the two pages is built first creates the gate and moves the existing safety check into it; the other adds a check and changes nothing else.** Answer shape: if one rule fails, that rule's own code stays (`PRODUCT_SAFETY_INFORMATION_REQUIRED`, `PRODUCT_UNIT_PRICE_INFORMATION_REQUIRED`, `PRODUCT_FOOD_INFORMATION_REQUIRED`, all `400`, so existing tests and clients are unchanged); if several fail, `400 PRODUCT_LISTING_INFORMATION_REQUIRED` with the details grouped by rule and item. If `Jurisdictions` cannot be read: `503` as today, nothing assumed, nothing stored. Bulk import refuses the row, not the file, with `sellableOnline false` as the escape.
     - **The rule as data.** tenant-svc gains `legal_obligations` rows with the code `FOOD_INFORMATION_ONLINE` (`REGIME` for the European Union: Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 art. 14 read with arts. 9, 30 to 35; a row per other place that has its own rule, each with its own effective dates and citation, added by whoever verifies the law, never assumed here). A business not bound is never asked. Which nutrients a place requires, the layout (per 100 g / per serving) and which kinds of food need their origin stated are also data, in product-svc reference tables keyed by the rule and regime with the citation on each row (the same precedent as `allergens` and `age_restriction_rules`): `food_information_requirements (regime, kind NUTRIENT | ORIGIN_CATEGORY, code, basis, mandatory, citation)`. The European Union rows are seeded from Annex XV of the regulation (energy in kJ and kcal, fat, of which saturates, carbohydrate, of which sugars, protein, salt) and its art. 26 categories; another place's rows are a data change, not a code change.
     - **What the food check asks:** for each variant the offer includes that is food (`allergen_status` not `NOT_APPLICABLE`) and **prepacked** (the default: a food variant is prepacked unless its declaration says `LOOSE`, so a food product nobody described is caught, not passed): a confirmed declaration in each language the business's storefront is offered in (read through `TenantProfiles`; the house language where only one is held), holding ingredients (the structured list, or the legacy text while the business has not moved to it), the nutrition table with every nutrient the rule marks mandatory (or a recorded exemption, slice 2), `net_content` and its unit, operator name and address, and, where the chosen origin category is one the rule lists, an origin. Allergens must be `DECLARED`. Storage and use instructions are checked only where the rule's row says so (`kind STORAGE_INSTRUCTIONS`), never assumed.
     - **What is already live is never delisted silently.** A product already online keeps selling; `GET /admin/food-information/gaps` (management, cursor) is the worklist and the Products screen shows a "Food information missing" badge; the next edit of that product must satisfy the gate. Same rule as unit-pricing slice 4.
  2. **The declaration, structured, with its history (product-svc).** New tables (below) and `POST /admin/food-information/variants/{variantId}` which records a **new version** (append-only; never an update) for a language, `PUT`-like in effect: the body is the whole declaration. Ingredients: an ordered list, each with its name, an optional percentage (where the law needs a quantity), an optional `allergenCode` from the fourteen (which is what makes it emphasised), and an optional parent (a compound ingredient's parts). A saved list whose allergen ingredients disagree with the variant's declaration is refused (`409 FOOD_INFO_ALLERGEN_MISMATCH`, naming the codes: an ingredient marked `MILK` needs `MILK` CONTAINS; a CONTAINS allergen with no ingredient marked is allowed, because a processing aid is a legitimate reason, but is shown in the "Contains" line the storefront already draws). Nutrition: per version a basis (per 100 g, per 100 ml, or the regime's own basis), each nutrient with an amount, its unit, and a "less than" flag (`<0.5 g`); an optional portion (size, unit, the household measure in words, portions per pack) with per-portion amounts **computed on read** from the per-100 table unless the business enters its own; `showReferenceIntake` where the business chooses to show percentages, computed on read by pure `ReferenceIntakes` from data rows (`reference_intakes`: regime, nutrient, amount, unit, citation; the European Union's from Annex XIII), never stored. A nutrition **exemption** (art. 16 and Annex V for unprocessed foods and the like) is recorded as `nutritionExempt` with a reason from a small data list and satisfies the gate for the table only, on the record. Fields: `operatorName`, `operatorAddress`, `storageInstructions`, `useInstructions`, `originCategory` (optional, from the rule's list), `presentation` (`PREPACKED` default, `LOOSE`). A version is `DRAFT` until `POST …/{version}/confirm` (management; an idempotent replay answers the first); **only a CONFIRMED version counts for the gate and is shown**, and a new version replaces the shown one only when confirmed, so a half-edited declaration never reaches a shopper. The date of durability shown is the product's `date_kind` (use by / best before) and the batch's own date is not a product fact, so the page says which kind the pack carries, not a date. An allergen change made through the existing allergen route (`PUT …/variants/{id}/allergens`) also writes a new version's allergen snapshot on the same transaction, so a version always carries the allergen declaration that stood with it. `GET /admin/food-information/variants/{variantId}/history?asOf=&after=&limit=` (management) lists versions with who and when, or the one in force at an instant (`asOf`, UTC): what was shown on a given day.
  3. **Supplier-provided data, recorded as such (product-svc).** A version carries `source` (`MANUAL`, `SUPPLIER`, `IMPORT`), an optional `sourceSupplierId` (purchase-svc's supplier, referenced, never joined; a stale id is kept as written), `sourceReference` (the supplier's specification number or document name) and `sourceDate`. Import (CSV) creates DRAFT versions with source `IMPORT`, one per row, refusing a row (not the file) that is malformed; nothing imported is shown until a person confirms it. The platform does not fetch or receive supplier specifications (see Out, on purpose). Whoever confirms is the food business operator's person of record: a supplier's mistake is still the business's.
  4. **The storefront shows it before the shopper buys (product-svc read, app).** `GET /catalog/variants/{variantId}/food-information?language=` (public, storefront tenant from the host) returns the confirmed version in the requested language, else the business's house language, else any confirmed one with its `language` named (never a machine translation): ingredients with the allergen ingredients flagged for emphasis, the nutrition table (per 100, per portion, reference intake when chosen), net quantity (from the variant), operator, storage and use, the date kind, origin. `404` is not used for "none": it answers `{recorded: false}` and the page then says "Full product information is not available for this product yet" and, for a food variant, does not offer the add button where the gate would have refused it (a live legacy product keeps the button, with the legacy ingredient text shown and the same notice). Emphasis is semantic (`emphasis: true` on an ingredient span); the app renders bold, so it works for any language and for a screen reader (the word is announced as strong). Shown above the add-to-basket control on a phone and beside it on a wide screen, not behind a tab or a link.
  5. **The counter ticket (product-svc, app).** For food sold loose, or prepacked for direct sale on the premises where the business's rule needs the same ticket, a ticket is a small view over the same data: `GET /admin/food-information/variants/{variantId}/ticket?language=` returns the product name, the ingredient list with allergens emphasised (or the allergen line only for a `LOOSE` variant where the rule needs no more), the allergen declaration, storage and use, the operator, the durability kind, the net quantity, and the origin where held. The **price and price per unit are not product-svc's**: the app already reads them from pricing-svc's label endpoint for the store and puts them on the ticket, so no price is joined here. It refuses to produce a ticket for a variant whose allergens are `UNDECLARED` (`409 FOOD_TICKET_ALLERGENS_UNDECLARED`): a ticket that says nothing looks like a ticket that says "none". A print writes `food_ticket_prints` (append-only: variant, version id, language, store, actor, copies, at) on the `POST …/ticket/print` call (`Idempotency-Key`; a retry answers the first), so the business can show what was on the counter. Any staff at the store may print (the store must be the caller's: `requireStoreAccess`); the admin app renders it for a printer through the platform's print sheet; label-printer devices and scale integration are out.
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **The supplier specification workflow (Oracle: "Product record and specification workflow with supplier approval"; recipe and raw materials; produce specifications; surveillance results).** It is a supplier-facing portal: suppliers would sign in to StoreQL, submit and version specifications, and the retailer approve them. The platform has no supplier identity, no external-party login, and Oracle's own grade for a supplier portal is one of the fourteen "needs a partner before code matters". What a retailer needs of it today is served: it records what a supplier gave it (slice 3), confirms it, and can show what it held on any day. A portal would sit on top of the same `food_information` versions by adding a writer; nothing here blocks it.
  - **Claims and icons** (vegan, organic, "low fat", health claims): each is regulated by its own scheme with evidence rules (nutrition and health claims regulation in the European Union). Free-text claims are not a field here; a business can hold them as an item attribute today, and the platform will not lend them the authority of a declaration.
  - **Deriving allergens, nutrition or a front-of-pack score from a recipe.** The business states them; the platform checks consistency (the ingredient marks against the allergen declaration) and never invents a figure. A score system (Nutri-Score and the like) is a marketing label with its own licence.
  - **Automatic translation** of the declaration. A machine's translation of an allergen or an ingredient is a safety risk; the business enters each language, and the shopper sees the language they read named.
  - **A country's nutrient list in code.** Every list is a data row with a citation; a place whose rule is not in `food_information_requirements` has no food gate (and no guess is made).
  - **Products sold only at the till.** The gate applies to online offers, as safety information does; the counter ticket serves the counter.
  - **Label printers, scales and shelf-edge labels.** Devices.
  - **A snapshot of what the shopper saw on each order.** The version history answers "what was shown on this day"; order-svc keeps no copy of food text. (If a complaint procedure needs the exact rendering, that is an order-time snapshot, a later decision with its own page.)

## Data and flow

- **Owned by product-svc** (all with `tenant_id UUID NOT NULL` first in the composite index, append-only where stated):
  - `food_information_versions` (append-only): id, tenant_id, variant_id, language (BCP 47), version (number per variant and language), state (`DRAFT` / `CONFIRMED`; the one permitted change after insert is a **confirmation row** in `food_information_confirmations`, not an update, so the table stays append-only), presentation, operator_name, operator_address, storage_instructions, use_instructions, origin_category, nutrition_exempt, nutrition_exempt_reason, nutrition_basis, portion_size NUMERIC(12,4), portion_unit, portion_description, portions_per_pack NUMERIC(12,4), show_reference_intake, source, source_supplier_id, source_reference, source_date, created_by, created_at.
  - `food_information_confirmations` (append-only): version id, confirmed_by, confirmed_at. The version in force for a language is the highest confirmed version.
  - `food_information_ingredients`: version id, position, parent_position, name, percentage NUMERIC(7,4), allergen_code (from `allergens`), part of the immutable version.
  - `food_information_nutrients`: version id, nutrient_code, amount NUMERIC(14,4), unit, less_than boolean, per_portion_amount NUMERIC(14,4) nullable.
  - `food_information_allergens`: version id, allergen_code, presence: the snapshot of the variant's allergen declaration at the version.
  - `food_ticket_prints` (append-only), see slice 5.
  - Reference data, no tenant: `food_information_requirements`, `reference_intakes`, `nutrients` (code, unit, name in English as fallback; the app localises the name by code), `nutrition_exemption_reasons`, each row with regime and citation.
  - No change to `product_variants.ingredients` (legacy text stays). Composite indexes `(tenant_id, variant_id, language, version)` and `(tenant_id, variant_id)` for the gate.
- **Owned by tenant-svc:** the `FOOD_INFORMATION_ONLINE` rows in `legal_obligations`, and the business's storefront languages (read; verify the field at build, the house language is the floor).
- **Needs from other services:** `Jurisdictions` and `TenantProfiles` (common-service, from tenant-svc); pricing-svc's label endpoint read by the app for the ticket; nothing else. No joins.
- **Events published:** `FoodInformationConfirmed` (`storeql.product.food-information-confirmed`: tenantId, variantId, productId, language, version, confirmedBy, `eventId` last). Consumers: none required today. It exists because storefront caches and the crawler feed ([storefront-trust](storefront-trust.md) slice 5, where `hasNutrition`-style structured data may later be added) want the change, and because notification-svc's recall text may quote the ingredients; consumers dedupe on `eventId`. The variant's allergen change keeps its existing announcement.
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** `POST …/food-information/variants/{id}` (a retry answers the first version), `POST …/confirm`, the CSV import, `POST …/ticket/print`.
- **New error codes:** `400 PRODUCT_FOOD_INFORMATION_REQUIRED` (missing items in details), `400 PRODUCT_LISTING_INFORMATION_REQUIRED` (several rules), `503` when rules unreadable (existing code), `409 FOOD_INFO_ALLERGEN_MISMATCH`, `400 FOOD_INFO_INVALID` (unknown nutrient or unit, a negative amount, a percentage above 100, a language not BCP 47, a parent that does not exist), `409 FOOD_INFO_ALREADY_CONFIRMED`, `404 FOOD_INFO_NOT_FOUND`, `409 FOOD_TICKET_ALLERGENS_UNDECLARED`, `400 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED`.

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** none in the declaration; the ticket's price is pricing-svc's, in the business's currency.
- **Ledger postings:** none.
- **Dates:** versions and confirmations UTC; `asOf` is an instant; the durability is a kind (use by / best before) on the product, not a date here.
- **Plan limits:** none. Compliance is not a paid feature.

## Constraints

- Golden rules 1 (supplier ids referenced, never joined), 3 (tenant from the JWT on every admin route; the public read takes the storefront tenant from the host; `tenant_id` first), 6 (the event with the write), 8 (versions, confirmations and prints are append-only: a correction is a new version), 9 and 10 (thin resources, DTOs), 15 (a malformed declaration is `400`).
- **Safety first:** an empty ingredient or allergen list is never read as "free from". The existing UNDECLARED discipline extends: a food variant with no declaration shows "not available", never blank.
- **Location-neutral:** no country, regulator, nutrient list, language or measurement basis is in code. The rule row (with its citation), the requirement rows and the reference intakes are data; a business in a place with no row is never gated; a business trading in two places is bound by each place's row where the law binds it (`inForceIn` per store country, the way the unit-price gate reads it).
- Existing tenants: no behaviour changes until a rule binds a business **and** it publishes; nothing already online is delisted; `variants.ingredients` keeps working.
- Callers a new refusal breaks: k6 suites and ITs that publish a food product (`allergen_status` not `NOT_APPLICABLE`) in a bound business without food information; the build begins by grepping `FoodSafetyIT`, `k6/` and the seed data and giving them a declaration.
- Gate ordering with the unit-price page: both must be built against the one `ListingGate`; a test proves that a product failing both is one `400 PRODUCT_LISTING_INFORMATION_REQUIRED` naming both.

## Open questions

- [x] Is the nutrition declaration per variant or per product? → **Per variant (a pack): amounts differ by pack and recipe, and the existing allergens, origin and net content are per variant** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] How is an allergen emphasised in the ingredient list? → **A marked ingredient (`allergenCode`), rendered strong by the app; the mark must agree with the allergen declaration** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Which nutrients and layout are mandatory? → **Whatever the business's jurisdiction row says, as data with a citation; the European Union's from Annex XV, seeded; none assumed elsewhere** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Is a draft shown? → **No: only a confirmed version is shown or counts; a new version replaces the shown one only when confirmed** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Refuse or delist when a rule starts to bind? → **Refuse at publish; never delist what is live; a worklist and a badge** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Where does the gate live for the food rule and the unit-price rule? → **One `ListingGate` in product-svc; several failures are one `400 PRODUCT_LISTING_INFORMATION_REQUIRED`; a single failure keeps its own code** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Is a counter ticket a separate feature? → **No: a view over the same declaration plus the price the app already reads; slice 5** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Does the platform take specifications from suppliers? → **No; the business records what it received and confirms it; a supplier portal is out (needs an external identity)** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)
- [x] Approval by a second person for a declaration? → **No: it moves no money, stock or access; the confirmation names who answers for it. The abuse case (removing an allergen to cover a mistake) is an alert, not an approval** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30)

## Acceptance

Who, authority, retry, isolation, record, abuse, refusals and location are lines here, each with its test.

- [ ] **The gate, one place.** A bound business's publish of a prepacked food variant with no confirmed declaration is refused `400 PRODUCT_FOOD_INFORMATION_REQUIRED` naming the missing items; complete, it passes — `FoodInformationListingIT.publishNeedsTheDeclarationWhereLawBinds`
- [ ] A product missing both safety information and a unit price is one `400 PRODUCT_LISTING_INFORMATION_REQUIRED` naming both; one failure keeps its own code — `ListingGateTest.*` (pure), `ListingGateIT.severalRulesAreOneRefusal`
- [ ] Rules unreadable: `503`, nothing assumed, nothing stored — `FoodInformationListingIT.unreadableRulesAreRefusedNotGuessed`
- [ ] A business the law does not bind, and a `LOOSE` or non-food variant, are never asked — `FoodInformationListingIT.anUnboundBusinessIsNeverAsked`
- [ ] A food variant nobody described counts as prepacked and is caught — `FoodInformationListingIT.anUndescribedFoodIsPrepacked`
- [ ] Two businesses in different countries get different answers for the same product data; a rule with its own nutrient list is applied as data — `FoodInformationListingIT.lawFollowsTheBusiness`, `FoodRequirementsTest.*`
- [ ] Bulk import refuses the row, not the file, with `sellableOnline false` as the escape — `FoodInformationListingIT.importRefusesTheRowNotTheFile`
- [ ] Live products are never delisted when a rule starts to bind; they appear on `GET /admin/food-information/gaps`, and their next edit must satisfy the gate — `FoodInformationListingIT.liveOffersAreNeverDelisted`
- [ ] **The declaration.** A version stores ingredients (order, parts, percentages, allergen marks), nutrition per 100 and per portion, and the other particulars; a second save is version 2 and version 1 is unchanged (append-only) — `FoodInformationIT.aChangeIsANewVersion`, `FoodInformationSchemaTest.appendOnly`
- [ ] An ingredient marked with an allergen the variant does not declare is refused `409 FOOD_INFO_ALLERGEN_MISMATCH`; invalid amounts, units or parents `400 FOOD_INFO_INVALID` — `FoodInformationIT.allergenMarksMustAgree`, `.invalidInputIsRefused`
- [ ] Per-portion amounts and reference intakes are computed, never stored; rounding is stated and pure — `ReferenceIntakesTest.*`, `NutritionPortionTest.*`
- [ ] A draft is neither shown nor counted; confirming makes it the shown version once; confirming twice `409 FOOD_INFO_ALREADY_CONFIRMED`; a replay with the same key answers the first — `FoodInformationIT.onlyConfirmedIsShown`, `.confirmIsIdempotent`
- [ ] An allergen change through the existing route adds a version snapshot in the same transaction — `FoodInformationIT.anAllergenChangeIsOnTheRecord`
- [ ] The history answers what was shown on a given day (`asOf`) and lists who and when — `FoodInformationIT.asOfAnswersWhatWasShown`
- [ ] **Supplier data.** `source`, supplier and reference are kept; an import makes drafts only; a stale supplier id is kept as written — `FoodInformationIT.supplierDataIsRecordedNotTrusted`
- [ ] **The storefront.** The public read returns the confirmed version in the shopper's language, else the house language, else another named; `recorded: false` is an answer, not a `404`; an allergen ingredient carries `emphasis` — `FoodInformationCatalogIT.languageFallback`, `.emphasisIsSemantic`, `.notRecordedIsSaid`
- [ ] The product page shows it above the add button, bold allergens (with a semantic label), the notice when absent, no add button where the gate would refuse — widget tests `product_food_information_test.dart`, `product_detail_food_gate_test.dart`
- [ ] **Isolation.** Another business's OWNER, MANAGER and STOREKEEPER, naming our variant id, get 404 on every admin route and nothing is written; a shopper cannot call an admin route (403); the public read of another business's variant through our storefront host is `{recorded: false}` — `FoodInformationTenantIsolationIT.*`
- [ ] **Roles.** A CASHIER cannot write or confirm (403); a STOREKEEPER cannot confirm; reading history needs management — `FoodInformationAuthorizationIT.*`
- [ ] **The record.** Every version and confirmation names who and when and cannot be edited or deleted through any route; the history is readable by management only — `FoodInformationIT.theRecordIsAppendOnly`
- [ ] **Counter ticket.** A ticket for a `LOOSE` variant carries name, emphasised allergens, storage, operator and net quantity; the app adds the price and unit price it reads from pricing-svc; a variant with undeclared allergens gets `409 FOOD_TICKET_ALLERGENS_UNDECLARED`; a print is recorded once per key with version, store and actor; a store-held caller cannot print for another store (`403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`) — `FoodTicketIT.*`, widget `food_ticket_screen_test.dart`
