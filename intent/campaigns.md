## Problem

A business can send one marketing message to one customer whose consent it has checked, by hand, through the send route. It cannot say "tell everyone who has not been in for two months about this offer next Tuesday", cannot ask for the same message every month, cannot see what became of it, and cannot hold itself to a limit on how often one person hears from it. The coupon engine works but nothing carries a code to anybody. A business doing this today exports customers to another tool, taking the consent evidence out of the platform where the law expects it to stay attached.

## Outcome

- **A campaign is a message aimed at a segment, with a date or a rhythm.** The business writes it in its own words and languages, starting from a catalogue message, adds an offer if it wants, sends a test to itself, and starts it.
- **Nothing is sent that the consent rules would refuse.** Every recipient is checked at the moment of sending by the same `allowance` gate as a single send: the `MARKETING` purpose, the channel's own consent, a pending double opt-in, a lapsed re-ask, a withdrawn channel. A campaign never widens who may be contacted, and a person's answer changing between the audience being worked out and the send is honoured.
- **The business's own limits hold:** how many marketing messages one person may get in a period, and the hours in which messages may go, across all its campaigns.
- **Every message carries its way out**, which is attributed to the campaign, so a rising opt-out rate is seen.
- **The report says what was sent to whom and what happened**, honestly: sent, skipped and why, failed, delivered where the transport tells us, unsubscribed; what cannot be measured is shown as not measured, never as zero.
- **A campaign can be paused or stopped at any moment**, and a stop is honoured before the next send.
- **A large send can need a second person** where the business says so.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **marketing manager** (a MANAGER) and **owner** (build, test, start, stop, read reports), a **second approver** where the business turns approval on, the **shopper** (receives, unsubscribes).
- **Channels:** EMAIL and SMS only. **Marketing never goes by push, in-app or a call or post here**: the platform has no consent model on push, and `PHONE`/`POST` have no sender ([consent-evidence](consent-evidence.md): "a recorded tick"). The offer itself is also shown in the app by [personalised-offers](personalised-offers.md); that is a display, not a message.
- **Scope:** per business. A campaign's audience is a segment of the same business. A caller held to stores may build and run campaigns only over segments confined to their stores; a business-wide segment needs a caller held to none.
- **Roles that can write:** OWNER and MANAGER. STOREKEEPER and CASHIER: read nothing, write nothing. The shopper: only their own unsubscribe and preferences, as built.
- **Sandbox tenant:** runs the whole flow (audience, checks, runs, report) but **every send is suppressed and logged `SUPPRESSED`**, so a business can rehearse a campaign with no message leaving.

## Already there

- The single marketing send with its gate: `SendResource` refuses a marketing message with no customer (`MARKETING_CONSENT_MISSING`), any channel but EMAIL or SMS (`MARKETING_CHANNEL_UNSUPPORTED`), a person `allowance` refuses, appends the unsubscribe footer itself, and asks the plan's text quota. Tests: `MarketingConsentIT`, `MarketingUnsubscribeIT` and notification-svc's send tests (wave 1 leaves them as they are).
- The unsubscribe link is a per-send token, redeemed with no sign-in (`POST /marketing/unsubscribe`, customer-svc), a second click is not an error, and it appends a consent-log row with source `UNSUBSCRIBE_LINK`.
- The template engine and its catalogue: forms EMAIL, SMS, PUSH, ALERT; Mustache subset; per language; the business may reword a catalogue message (`PUT /admin/notifications/templates/{type}`), with the English fallback.
- Promotions with `couponCode`, `maxRedemptions`, `maxPerCustomer`, and the redemption ledger.
- Not there: any campaign, schedule, recurring send, audience, frequency cap, test send, report or attribution of an unsubscribe to a message.

## Scope

- **Who may do it, and where (every slice):** as above; every campaign route checks the business, then the store rule of its segment (`requireStoreAccess` for a confined segment; `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY` otherwise).
- **In (slices in build order):**
  1. **The one marketing gate (notification-svc, customer-svc).** Before any campaign exists, move the marketing branch of `SendResource` into one service, `MarketingSender`, and make it the **only** code that may send a message of category `MARKETING` (the single send route now calls it too). It asks customer-svc's `allowance` per person per channel, fails **closed** when the answer cannot be had, mints the unsubscribe token, appends the footer, checks the text quota, and writes the log row. An ArchUnit rule fails the build if `Notifier.notifyOnce` is reachable for a `MARKETING` type from anywhere else. `allowance` gains an optional `campaignId` so the token minted for the send is attributed (`marketing_unsubscribe_tokens.campaign_id`, additive, nullable), and the unsubscribe redeem publishes `MarketingUnsubscribed` (`storeql.customer.marketing-unsubscribed`: `eventId`, `tenantId`, `customerId`, `channel`, `campaignId?`, `occurredAt`) so the campaign's report can count it. No campaign feature works without this slice; it changes no behaviour for the existing single send.
  2. **The message (notification-svc, app).** Catalogue types marked `category: MARKETING` are added, each with platform starting words in every language the catalogue covers and the English fallback: `MARKETING_OFFER`, `MARKETING_NEWS`, `MARKETING_WINBACK`. They are the **starting point**: the business rewords them for its whole business through the existing template routes, and a campaign may carry **its own words per language and form** (`campaign_messages`) that win for that campaign. The same limits apply (plain text, no HTML, the form's length, the subset), plus a check that the text contains no unsubscribe wording of its own that could contradict the footer. Available values: business name, the person's first name (only what the customer record holds; missing = the greeting without it), the coupon `couponCode`, `offerLabel`, `offerEnds` (from the promotion, slice 6), the store's name where the segment names one store. **The unsubscribe line is never in the author's hands**: it is appended by `MarketingSender`. Language per person: their preferred language, else the business's, else the platform's English fallback.
  3. **Campaign, audience and schedule (notification-svc, app).** A campaign: name, `segment_id`, channels in preference order with a mode (`FIRST_ALLOWED`: one message per person on the first channel they allow and have a contact for; `ALL_ALLOWED`: on each allowed channel), the message (slice 2), an optional `promotion_id`, `one_per_household` (off; [customer-segments](customer-segments.md) slice 6), and a schedule: `ONCE` at an instant, or `RECURRING` every N days, weeks or months at a **local time of day in the business's home zone**, from a start, until an end date or a run count (a recurring campaign with neither is allowed and shown as "no end"). States: DRAFT, PENDING_APPROVAL, SCHEDULED, RUNNING, PAUSED, STOPPED, COMPLETED, FAILED. Only a DRAFT or PAUSED campaign can be edited; **any change to the audience, message, promotion or schedule of a SCHEDULED campaign returns it to DRAFT** and voids an approval. Endpoints: create, read, list (cursor), edit, `POST /admin/campaigns/{id}/schedule` (validates everything: segment active, message complete in the business's language, promotion active over the window; `409 CAMPAIGN_INCOMPLETE` naming what is missing), `pause`, `resume`, `stop`, `archive` (only a STOPPED, COMPLETED or FAILED one).
  4. **The run and the send loop (notification-svc).** A sweep (technical interval) claims each due occurrence with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` (safe with several replicas) and creates one `campaign_runs` row, then:
     1. asks customer-svc to refresh the segment (`POST /internal/segments/{id}/refresh`, trigger CAMPAIGN); if it cannot, the run uses the last membership and **records its age**; a campaign's `max_audience_age_hours` (a business choice, null = accept any) fails the run `CAMPAIGN_AUDIENCE_STALE` before any send if the last computation is older;
     2. re-checks the promotion is still active (`CAMPAIGN_PROMOTION_NOT_ACTIVE`, run FAILED, nothing sent);
     3. reads members by cursor (`GET /internal/segments/{id}/members`, ids and language) and, **per person and per channel, in this order**, decides with pure `CampaignEligibility`: sandbox (suppressed) → **`allowance`** through `MarketingSender` (any refusal is the skip reason, e.g. `NO_CONSENT`, `CONFIRMATION_PENDING`, `CONSENT_LAPSED`, `PURPOSE_WITHDRAWN`) → contact on record (`NO_CONTACT`) → the **frequency cap** (`FREQUENCY_CAP`: the person's marketing sends across **all** campaigns in the window, from notification-svc's own log) → **send window** (`OUTSIDE_SEND_HOURS`: the message is deferred to the next allowed moment; the run stays RUNNING until then) → household duplicate (`HOUSEHOLD_DUPLICATE`) → plan quota (`PLAN_LIMIT`: further sends of that channel are skipped and the run ends `COMPLETED` with the reason counted, never silently short);
     4. sends through `MarketingSender` with the derived key `Ids.derived(runId, personChannel)`, so a resumed or replayed run sends each person once (`campaign_sends` is unique on run, person and channel and `notify_once` is unique on `(event_id, type)`); a crash mid-run resumes and completes the remainder without a double send;
     5. before **each** send, reads the campaign's status; a `pause` or `stop` takes effect before the next person, with at most the one send already handed to the transport still going. The report says when the stop was asked and how many sends followed it (expected: none).
     A run's rate is bounded by a technical config so a large audience does not swamp the SMTP relay or the SMS provider.
  5. **Outcomes (notification-svc, customer-svc, app).**
     - **Per send** (`campaign_sends`, then append-only `campaign_send_events`): `SENT` (accepted by the transport), `FAILED` (with the transport's reason), or a skip with its reason; later `DELIVERED` or `BOUNCED` **only where the channel reports it**: texts through the SMS provider's delivery receipt, received on one new public route `POST /notifications/receipts/{provider}` verified by the provider's signature over the raw body (a driver behind `SmsProvider`, `SIMULATED` twin, stub-backed test of the exact signature and request shape as the driver kit requires); email through an SMTP relay reports no delivery, so email is "accepted by the relay" and its bounces are **not measured** unless a provider driver is added later. `UNSUBSCRIBED` from `MarketingUnsubscribed` with the campaign id.
     - **Opens and clicks are not collected** (no tracking pixel, no tracked links): they are unreliable behind mail-privacy features, and where consent is required for the tracking itself they would need a second consent this platform does not model. A driver that reports them can add them behind a per-business switch (off) with the same event table; until then the report says "not measured".
     - **Report** `GET /admin/campaigns/{id}/report?runId=`: per run and total, the audience size and its computed-at, reachable per channel at the moment, sent, skipped **by reason**, failed by reason, delivered and bounced (or "not measured"), unsubscribed, the number and rate, the stop time if any; and, when a promotion is carried, the redemptions of it since the first send (count and value, asked of pricing-svc, presented as *what was redeemed*, never as *caused by this campaign*: no attribution claim is made). `GET /admin/campaigns/{id}/sends?status=&after=&limit=` lists sends (cursor) with the customer's name; a **sensitive read** with a reason, like a segment's members. The shopper's Notifications list ([notification-centre](notification-centre.md)) shows the message they were sent, and staff's "did you send it" view shows it with the campaign's name.
  6. **The offer it carries (notification-svc, pricing-svc).** `promotion_id` on the campaign; the message values `couponCode`, `offerLabel`, `offerEnds` come from pricing-svc through a read (`GET /admin/promotions/{id}`, the leaf staff read) at run start, cached for the run. If the promotion is limited to an audience ([personalised-offers](personalised-offers.md)) and the campaign's segment is not the same segment, the schedule call answers with a **warning** (`OFFER_AUDIENCE_DIFFERS`, not a refusal): people outside the offer's audience would be sent a code that will not work. The campaign never creates or edits the promotion; the "create an offer for this campaign" shortcut on the screen calls pricing-svc's own route (and passes its own value ceilings and approvals).
  7. **Settings, limits and the test send (notification-svc, tenant-svc, app).**
     - `campaign_settings` (one per business, OWNER or business-wide MANAGER): `max_messages_per_person` **and** `per_days` (both null until both set: a cap of "3 in 7 days" is the business's own; one without the other is `400 CAMPAIGN_SETTINGS_INVALID`), `send_from_local` and `send_to_local` (the hours, in the business's home zone; null = any time), `default_channel_order`, `max_audience_age_hours` default. **Where the register carries a `MARKETING_SEND_HOURS` obligation** for a country the business trades in (a citation; none is seeded on this page's authority, a row is added when a country's rule is confirmed) **it applies even if the business set none** and the business is told on the settings screen: the stricter of the two holds.
     - **Test send:** `POST /admin/campaigns/{id}/test {channel}` (`Idempotency-Key`; OWNER, MANAGER): renders the campaign's message with sample values and sends it **only to the caller's own login email** (or, for SMS, to the caller's own phone if the staff record has a verified one). Never to a customer, so it needs no consent; it is logged `CAMPAIGN_TEST`, prefixed "[TEST]", counts nothing in any report or cap, and is rate-limited per person (technical bound, `429 CAMPAIGN_TEST_LIMITED`). A sandbox test is suppressed like any send.
     - **Approval:** the action key **`marketing.campaign-send`** (new; see Authority): when the business has a rule for it and the campaign's **reachable audience** at scheduling exceeds the rule's ceiling, `schedule` returns a pending approval (`202`, campaign `PENDING_APPROVAL`) and a second person with authority approves it in their own session; nothing runs until then. The approval covers the **version** scheduled (any edit voids it). No rule = no approval, as for other new keys, unchanged behaviour.
     - **Plan:** `campaigns.sends.per-month` (email and text campaign sends counted together; texts also count under the existing `sms.per-month`), enforced here through `Quotas`, fails open when the plan cannot be read.
  8. **Screens (app).**
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Push, in-app messages, WhatsApp, calls and post as campaign channels.** No consent model or sender exists for them; adding one is a different feature with its own legal question.
  - **Open and click tracking, and send-time or subject-line optimisation.** See slice 5: unreliable, privacy-costly, and the latter needs measurement the platform does not take.
  - **Purchasing or importing an outside list, or sending to someone with no customer record.** A campaign's audience is a segment of customers the business already holds, each with their own consent. There is no address-list upload.
  - **A/B testing.** A later slice needs a holdout and a measure; the report as designed does not attribute results to a message.
  - **Attribution of sales to a campaign.** Only the redemption of a carried code is shown, labelled as such.
  - **Serialised per-recipient codes.** The offer's audience already ties the code to the segment ([personalised-offers](personalised-offers.md)); serial codes are for mailing outside a customer base, which is out.
  - **A drag-and-drop email designer and HTML emails.** The template engine is plain text by design so nothing a business writes can carry markup that could hide the footer; images and layout are a separate, larger feature.
  - **Its own consent, or a way round it.** Not a setting: there is none.

## Data and flow

**Why notification-svc owns campaigns.** A campaign is a *send job*: a schedule, a loop, transport providers, per-person delivery records, plan metering, and the message templates. All of these are notification-svc's already (the log, `Notifier`, `SmsChannel`, `Quotas`, the catalogue). Consent, membership and the customer record are customer-svc's and are asked per person through the existing `allowance`, so consent never leaves its owner. Putting campaigns in customer-svc would have moved transports, templates and metering there or made it call notification-svc for every one of thousands of sends; putting them in pricing-svc would attach messages to a service that owns prices. Frequency caps come free: notification-svc already has every marketing send in its own log.

- **Owned by notification-svc:**
  - `campaigns`: id, `tenant_id`, `name`, `segment_id`, `channels` (ordered list), `mode`, `promotion_id` (nullable), `one_per_household`, `max_audience_age_hours`, `schedule_kind`, `starts_at`, `every_n`, `every_unit`, `send_time_local`, `ends_at`, `max_runs`, `status`, `version`, `approval_id` (nullable), `created_by`, `started_by`, timestamps. Index `(tenant_id, status, next_run_at)`. `campaign_versions` (append-only: the whole definition each time it is scheduled).
  - `campaign_messages`: `campaign_id`, `language`, `form`, `subject`, `body`.
  - `campaign_runs`: id, `tenant_id`, `campaign_id`, `version`, `due_at`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, `status` (RUNNING, COMPLETED, FAILED, STOPPED, PAUSED), `failure_reason`, `audience_size`, `audience_computed_at`, `reachable_email`, `reachable_sms`, counters (sent, skipped by reason, failed, delivered, bounced, unsubscribed, as columns or a small JSON so **erasing a person's sends never changes the run's totals**), `stop_requested_at`, `sends_after_stop`, `started_by`.
  - `campaign_sends`: id, `tenant_id`, `run_id`, `customer_id`, `channel`, `outcome` (SENT, FAILED, SKIPPED), `reason`, `notification_id` (the log row), `at`; unique `(run_id, customer_id, channel)`; index `(tenant_id, customer_id, at)` for the frequency cap. `campaign_send_events` (append-only): send, `kind` (DELIVERED, BOUNCED, UNSUBSCRIBED), `at`, `detail` (no personal value).
  - `campaign_settings`, `campaign_tests` (append-only: who, when, channel; no recipient value beyond "self").
  - **SubjectDataSpec:** `campaign_sends.customer_id` ERASE on `CustomerErased`; `campaign_send_events` follow their send; `campaign_runs` totals stay (they hold no person). `campaigns` hold no customer data.
- **Owned by customer-svc (additive):** `marketing_unsubscribe_tokens.campaign_id`; `allowance`'s optional `campaignId`; `MarketingUnsubscribed`; `POST /internal/segments/{id}/refresh`. **Owned by tenant-svc:** the `MARKETING_SEND_HOURS` obligation code (data, `limit_value`/`limit_unit` columns from privacy-requests slice 1 or 0.4 (a), with a citation per row) and the plan keys.
- **Endpoints:** `/admin/campaigns/**` (OWNER, MANAGER by the path gate) as in the slices; `POST /notifications/receipts/{provider}` (public through the gateway, verified by signature, in the shared `OpenRoutes` table); `GET/PUT /admin/campaigns/settings`.
- **Needs from other services:** customer-svc (`allowance` per person, members by cursor, refresh, `MarketingUnsubscribed`), pricing-svc (the promotion read and redemption summary), tenant-svc (`TenantProfiles`: zone and language; `Jurisdictions`). Each a REST or event call with timeout, retry and breaker; **an unreadable answer sends nothing** for that person (fail closed for consent) and marks the run's skipped reason `CONSENT_UNREADABLE`.
- **Events published:** `CampaignRunCompleted` and `CampaignStopped` (`storeql.notification.campaign-run-completed`, `…campaign-stopped`: `eventId`, `tenantId`, `campaignId`, `runId`, counts; no person) for reporting-svc and the webhook catalogue. `ExceptionAlertRaised` for the metrics below.
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** create, schedule, pause, resume, stop, test send. The sends are keyed by the derived key above.
- **New error codes:** `404 CAMPAIGN_NOT_FOUND`, `409 CAMPAIGN_INCOMPLETE` (with `details` naming what), `409 CAMPAIGN_NOT_EDITABLE`, `409 CAMPAIGN_STATE_INVALID`, `409 CAMPAIGN_AUDIENCE_STALE`, `409 CAMPAIGN_PROMOTION_NOT_ACTIVE`, `400 CAMPAIGN_MESSAGE_INVALID` (over the form's length, markup, its own unsubscribe wording, an unknown value), `400 CAMPAIGN_SETTINGS_INVALID`, `429 CAMPAIGN_TEST_LIMITED`, `409 CAMPAIGN_SEGMENT_NOT_CONFINED` (a store-held caller naming a wider segment), `202` with `approvalId` for a pending approval, `403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`, `409 PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED`, `400 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED`, `401` for a receipt with a bad signature.

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** none held. An offer's value is pricing-svc's, in the business's home currency; the redemption summary shows that.
- **Ledger postings:** none. Texts are metered to the business through the existing usage meter (21.10).
- **Dates:** occurrences due at the business's local time of day converted to UTC by `TenantProfiles` zone (a DST change moves the UTC instant, not the local time); every stored instant UTC; the report and screens show the business's zone.
- **Plan limits:** `campaigns.sends.per-month` (new key, notification-svc, fails open); the existing `sms.per-month`.

## Constraints

- Golden rules 1, 3 (the tenant from the JWT for staff; from the run's own row for the sweep and the receipt), 6 and 7 (each send once by key; consumers dedupe on `eventId`), 8 (`campaign_send_events`, `campaign_versions`, `campaign_tests` append-only), 9, 10, 11, 15, and CLAUDE.md's marketing rule: **no send without `allowance`, from the one code path that can send it.**
- **Consent is checked at send, not at planning.** The audience and reach are previews; the truth is the per-person check in the loop. A withdrawal one second before the send is honoured.
- **Privacy:** no message text in an event or an alert; the log copy is as for any message; a sends read is a sensitive read; erasure removes the person's sends and leaves totals.
- **Location-neutral:** no country's send-hours, opt-in or opt-out law in code; the register carries any with a citation; times in the business's zone; wording per language.
- **Abuse:** a run's rate is bounded; a stop is honoured before the next send; a second person can be required over a ceiling; the test send goes only to the caller.
- **Cost:** the loop reads members by cursor and checks per person in bounded concurrency (a technical bound); `allowance` calls are per person because the truth is per person, with a batch form of the same function available to customer-svc's reach preview only.
- Existing behaviour unchanged: the single marketing send, the footer and the quota work exactly as before.

## Decisions

- (2026-09-30 evening, reconciliation of the second set of pages) **Audiences are segments** ([customer-segments](customer-segments.md), the one owner): a campaign never evaluates rules itself. Every campaign send goes through the one `MarketingSender` gate and `allowance`; approvals key `marketing.campaign-send` (area `marketing`, added to the catalogue); the sends read is the sensitive-read key `campaign-sends`.

- **One gate, one code path** (2026-09-30, industry standard): every marketing message goes through `MarketingSender`, enforced by an architecture test, so a campaign cannot be a way round consent.
- **Consent at send, not at planning** (2026-09-30): audience and reach are previews; the per-person `allowance` call is the truth, and an unreadable answer sends nothing.
- **Honest measurement** (2026-09-30): what the channel cannot tell us is "not measured"; opens and clicks are not collected; no sale is attributed to a campaign.
- **notification-svc owns campaigns** (2026-09-30): the send job lives with the transports, templates, metering and the send log; customer-svc keeps consent and membership.
