## Problem

**Working time.** The roster already says something about rest and breaks, but it says it the same way to every business on earth: `Workforce.concerns` flags an eleven-hour daily rest and a break after six hours, quoting the EU directive, for a business in any country, and it reads the roster's day in UTC rather than in the store's own zone. It says nothing about a maximum length or week, or about young workers, and it never refuses anything: where the law makes a roster unlawful, a manager can still publish it. A manager can also correct a time entry to more hours with a reason and nobody else looks: `adjust` needs OWNER or MANAGER, checks no store, does not stop a manager correcting their own hours, and publishes the higher `LabourRecorded` at once.

**Notices and tasks.** An urgent notice can be left unread with no consequence and no deadline. A task missed again and again at the same store is only a count on a summary.

**Zones.** A zone's status is free text that nothing listens to: a zone marked out of service still receives new stock by putaway rules and is still walked by pick waves.

**Slot fee.** A delivery or collection slot is always free. Some businesses charge for a tight or premium slot; `intent/delivery-and-collection-slots.md` left it out on purpose because the tax treatment of such a charge differs by country and it reaches refunds, the ledger, the tax return and every report.

## Outcome

- **A roster is judged by the law that binds the store**, read from data with a citation, in the store's own zone, and a business can only make its own rules tighter. The manager is **warned while planning**, and **publishing is refused only where the data says the roster is unlawful**.
- **A correction that raises someone's paid hours needs a second person**, and nobody corrects their own hours.
- **An urgent notice can carry an acknowledge-by time**, the sender is told who missed it, and a business can make a notice block sign-in until it is read. A task missed repeatedly at a store reaches its manager through the alerts page.
- **A zone's status steers stock**: putaway skips a zone that is out of service, waves show what is blocked by one, and a retired zone takes nothing new.
- **A business may switch on a fee for a slot**, in its own currency with its own tax treatment, charged once, refunded when the business fails the customer, and visible everywhere money is.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the store manager (rosters, corrects hours, publishes notices), the owner (business rules, settings, the fee), the staff member (acknowledges, is rostered), the shopper (pays a slot fee where one is on), the warehouse keeper (zones).
- **Channels:** back-office (Workforce, Notices, Zones, Fulfilment windows) · POS sign-in · storefront checkout.
- **Scope:** per store (rosters, zones, windows, notices to a store) and business-wide (rules, fee switch). A manager held to stores acts only at those stores (`requireStoreAccess`); business-wide settings need `BusinessWide.require`. A shift or entry is judged by its own store (wave 1 left publish, cancel and adjust by id without a store check; this page adds it).
- **Roles that can write:** rosters and corrections: OWNER, MANAGER at the store; rules and settings: OWNER; young-worker flag: whoever holds `staff.manage`; the fee and its tax treatment: OWNER; zones: management at the store.
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same.

## Scope

- **In:** slices in build order:
  1. **Working-time rules as jurisdiction data (tenant-svc).** A new reference table `working_time_rules` alongside `legal_obligations`, with the same discipline: platform reference data, no `tenant_id`, changed only by a migration with its citation, scope REGIME or COUNTRY (and an optional region for laws that differ inside a country), effective from/to dates, and per rule: a code (`MAX_SHIFT`, `MAX_WEEK`, `MIN_DAILY_REST`, `BREAK_AFTER`, `MIN_BREAK`, `WEEK_REFERENCE`), a number and unit, `severity` (`ADVISORY`, `UNLAWFUL`), `applies_to` (`ALL` or `YOUNG_WORKER`), the citation and a summary. **What ships first is only what the code already applies:** the eleven-hour rest and the break after six hours now sit under the `EU` regime, citing the directive their comments name, as `ADVISORY` (the code's own reason: member states implement it with derogations and agreements, so it stays flagged, not refused). **Every other country's or regime's pack is added by a migration by someone who supplies the citation**; this page states no other country's numbers and the platform assumes none: a business in a place with no pack sees only its own rules and the always-true concerns (overlapping shifts). The store's rules are found through its country (tenant-svc's own store data) and `Jurisdictions`-style regime membership with dates; the roster's day boundaries and "week" use the **store's zone** (`TenantProfiles.Stores.zoneOf`), never UTC, and the week's start comes from the rule (`WEEK_REFERENCE`) rather than a weekday assumed. `Workforce.concerns` becomes a pure `WorkingTime.evaluate(shifts, rules, zone)` and the `Concern` answer gains `severity` and `source` (`LAW` with the citation, `BUSINESS`).
  2. **A business's own, tighter rules (tenant-svc).** The owner may set the same rule codes for the business or for one store, and each carries `action`: `WARN` (default) or `BLOCK`. A business rule **looser than the law's** for its country is refused when saved (`400 WORKFORCE_RULE_LOOSER_THAN_LAW`, naming the citation); where there is no law rule, any value the owner sets is theirs. Off until set: a business with no rules of its own sees only the law's.
  3. **Warn at planning, refuse at publish only where unlawful (tenant-svc).** Creating and reading a shift returns its concerns as today (a roster listing carries them). **Publishing** (`POST /admin/workforce/shifts/{id}/publish`) evaluates the person's shifts across **all stores of the business** (rest between a shift at one store and the next at another counts) together with the new one: any `UNLAWFUL` law breach, and any business rule whose action is `BLOCK`, refuses with `409 WORKFORCE_RULE_BREACH` naming each rule, its source and its citation; anything `ADVISORY` publishes with the warnings recorded on the shift (`concerns_at_publish`) so the roster and a later dispute show what was known. There is **no manager override of an UNLAWFUL rule**; the way through is to change the roster. (Where a law allows a worker's signed opt-out, that is a per-country fact that needs its own reviewed data and consent record; it is left out, below.)
  4. **Young workers (tenant-svc).** A person's young-worker status is a date, not a birth date: `young_worker_until` on the person's workforce profile, set by whoever holds `staff.manage` (and audited in the admin change log), so the platform keeps the least personal data that answers the question. Rules with `applies_to = YOUNG_WORKER` apply to that person's shifts until that date, evaluated in the store's zone. Where the store's country has no such pack the date is kept and does nothing. Never inferred from anything else.
  5. **What was actually worked (tenant-svc).** The same evaluation runs over **time entries** when an entry closes or is corrected: a break not taken where one was due, a rest shorter than the rule between two entries, hours above a maximum. Each breach is recorded (`working_time_breaches`, append-only, with the rule and citation) and announced as `WorkingTimeBreachDetected` (`storeql.tenant.working-time-breach-detected`); the alerts page turns "the same store or manager, N times in a period" into the metric `working_time.breaches` (named here, designed there). Nothing is refused at the clock: a person clocking out is never blocked.
  6. **A correction that raises hours needs a second person (tenant-svc, approvals).** Three rules, in this order of firmness. **(a)** Nobody corrects, or clocks in by hand, their own hours: `403 WORKFORCE_SELF_ADJUST_REFUSED` (fixed, no setting: it is the fraud control the catalogue names). **(b)** `adjust` and the manual clock-in judge the entry's own store: `requireStoreAccess` (`403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`), closing the gap wave 1 recorded. **(c)** A correction that would **raise** the person's paid minutes (paid minutes = the entry's length less unpaid breaks) is not applied at once: it becomes a **pending correction** (`time_entry_corrections`: entry, proposed in/out, reason, requested by, status PENDING / APPROVED / REFUSED, decided by/at) and is the approvals mechanism's action **`staff.time-correction-up`**, decided by a person other than the requester holding `staff.manage` at the store. Only on approval does the superseding entry get written (`time_entries` stay append-only, supersede as now) and `LabourRecorded` publish, so labour cost never moves on an unapproved figure. A correction that keeps or lowers paid minutes applies at once as today. Whether (c) is on for a business is the approvals page's policy switch, **off until the business turns it on**; (a) and (b) are on for everyone.
  7. **Urgent notices with an acknowledge-by time (tenant-svc, iam-svc, notification-svc, app).** A notice with `requires_ack` may carry `ack_by` (a time in the store's zone, on or before its expiry). When it passes, tenant-svc records who did not acknowledge (the notice's existing reach, now per person), publishes `BroadcastAckOverdue` once, and notification-svc tells the sender and the managers of the stores concerned, in-app, with the count and a link to the list of names. A sender may also set **`gate: ON_SIGN_IN`** (URGENT only): tenant-svc publishes the notice's gating state (`BroadcastPublished` / `BroadcastWithdrawn` carrying `gate` and the audience), iam-svc keeps a small projection of pending gating notices per person and refuses to open a POS session while one is unacknowledged: `409 BROADCAST_ACK_REQUIRED`, listing the notice ids; the app shows them on sign-in and the session opens the moment they are acknowledged. A gate ends when the notice is acknowledged, withdrawn or expired. It never blocks a sale already in progress, only starting a session, and a manager (with access to the store) can **stand a person down from one notice** (recorded, with a reason) so an unreachable notice never strands a till. Default is no gate: only a sender who chooses one blocks anyone.
  8. **A task missed repeatedly (alerts).** Named here, designed on [exception-alerts](exception-alerts.md): the metric `tasks.missed_consecutive` (per store, per period, from the existing `store_tasks` day summary and `TaskMissed` where it exists), whose alert notifies the store's managers. This page adds no mechanism.
  9. **What listens to a zone's status (tenant-svc, inventory-svc).** The vocabulary is fixed and checked: `ACTIVE`, `OUT_OF_SERVICE`, `RETIRED` (a CHECK, with existing free-text values migrated to `ACTIVE` when they are not one of these three, and the value changes audited in the admin change log). tenant-svc publishes **`ZoneStatusChanged`** (`storeql.tenant.zone-status-changed`: `eventId`, `tenantId`, `storeId`, `zoneId`, `oldStatus`, `newStatus`, `storageClass` (the zone's current class key or absent, added by [receiving-controls](receiving-controls.md) slice 4), `occurredAt`; sent on a change of the status **or** of the storage class, a class change having the two statuses equal) through the outbox; inventory-svc keeps one `zone_status` projection (status and storage class) and: **putaway** (`PutawayRepository.directTx`) never sets a non-ACTIVE zone: a rule that points at one is skipped and a `putaway_tasks` row is raised for a person, and the rule shows as needing attention; **waves** (`Waves.plan`) leave a non-ACTIVE zone out of the walk and list the order lines whose only stock is in one as **blocked: zone out of service** instead of picking them (a manager may include them); **transfers and receipts** into a `RETIRED` zone are refused (`409 INVENTORY_ZONE_RETIRED`); stock **already in** a zone that goes out of service is not changed and not made unavailable by this (that is a stock decision, done with the batch's existing `material_status` such as QUARANTINE), but it appears on `GET /admin/inventory/zones/attention` with quantities so a person can move it. Making a zone `RETIRED` while stock is in it is allowed and lists it there. Order-svc and POS do not read zones and are unchanged.
  10. **A fee for a slot a business may switch on (order-svc, pricing-svc, payment-svc, purchase-svc, app).** Off unless the owner switches it on (`slot_fees_enabled`, default false); **with it off, every window is free exactly as today** and the reasoning in `intent/delivery-and-collection-slots.md` stands: nothing in a business that does not switch it on changes. With it on, a window may carry a `fee` (NUMERIC(18,4), the business's home currency, null or zero = free). The fee is chosen at checkout with the slot, shown in the basket total before payment, **stored on the order at placement** (`orders.slot_fee`, `slot_fee_vat`, and a snapshot of the treatment; a later change to the window never changes an order), and paid with the order like any other part of its total, so payment, refund and the reports carry it with no second path. **Tax is data, not an assumption:** the owner chooses one treatment per business, from pricing-svc's own categories: `FOLLOWS_GOODS` (apportioned across the basket's tax rates in proportion to their value, the treatment that applies where an ancillary charge takes the goods' tax), a named **category** of the business's own tax categories (a fixed rate or exempt), or `NO_TAX_LINE`; the platform ships no default and refuses to switch the fee on until one is chosen (`409 SLOT_FEE_TAX_TREATMENT_REQUIRED`). Optional `free_above` (a basket value in the home currency, null = never free). **Refunds:** the fee is returned in full when the *business* cancels or fails the slot (cancelled by staff, a missed delivery window recorded as failed) and when the shopper cancels before the window's cutoff; it is **not** returned when goods are returned after delivery; the rule is the business's to tighten through a setting `slot_fee_refund_on_shopper_cancel_after_cutoff` (default false). The fee is a **sale of a service**, posted Dr the tender / Cr delivery income (and the tax through the same posting as the goods' tax), through the existing sales event to purchase-svc; a fee in a **split checkout** is a group-level charge shared across the parts by the existing pure `OrderSplit`, the last part taking the rounding. A foreign-currency display shows it with the basket, never charging in the other currency (the display rule). Consumers see one order with one extra line: `OrderPlaced` gains `slotFee` and `slotFeeVat` (additive), so reporting, the tax return and purchase-svc pick it up.
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Worker opt-outs and collective-agreement derogations.** Where a law lets a worker sign out of a maximum, or an agreement replaces a rule, each is per-country reviewed data with a consent record and is its own item; until then such rules are `ADVISORY`, never `UNLAWFUL`, in the data.
  - **Payroll, overtime pay rates and holiday accrual.** This page judges rosters and hours, it does not pay them (pay rates and commission exist as their own tables; a rule that raises pay is not a working-time rule).
  - **Country packs beyond what the code already applies.** The design is the table and the discipline; a pack is added by a migration with its citation, by a person who has the legal source. Listing numbers here would be inventing them.
  - **Refusing a clock-in or clock-out.** A person is never blocked from recording the hours they worked; breaches are recorded and reported.
  - **Blocking a sale for an unread notice.** A gate refuses only starting a session, and can be stood down.
  - **Slot fees per postcode, per driver route, or dynamic pricing by demand.** A fee is per window; demand pricing is its own feature.
  - **A delivery charge that is not tied to a slot** (a per-order or per-distance fee). The design here is the slot's; a general delivery charge reuses the tax-treatment and refund rules but is a different price and is left to its own page.
  - **Changing zone status on stock.** Zone status never changes a batch, its quantity or its availability by itself.
  - **Retroactive judgement of already published rosters and closed entries.** New rules apply from when they ship; history is not re-flagged.

## Data and flow

- **Owned by tenant-svc:**
  - `working_time_rules` (reference data, migration only, citation required): scope kind and scope, region, code, value, unit, severity, applies_to, effective from/to, citation, summary. A test fails a migration that inserts a row with no citation.
  - `business_working_time_rules` (tenant, optional store, code, value, unit, action WARN|BLOCK, set by/at); `working_time_breaches` (append-only: entry or shift, rule, source, at); `staff_workforce_profiles` (tenant, user, `young_worker_until`, set by/at); `time_entry_corrections` (pending requests, decided once); `work_shifts.concerns_at_publish` (text/JSON snapshot); `store_broadcasts` gains `ack_by`, `gate` (NONE|ON_SIGN_IN) and `broadcast_stand_downs` (append-only); `zones.status` CHECK.
  - Admin-audit types: `YOUNG_WORKER_SET`, `WORKING_TIME_RULE_SET`, `ZONE_STATUS_CHANGED`, `BROADCAST_STOOD_DOWN`.
- **Owned by iam-svc:** `pending_gating_notices` (projection: tenant, user or store audience, notice id, until) fed by `BroadcastPublished` / `BroadcastWithdrawn` / `BroadcastAcknowledged`.
- **Owned by inventory-svc:** `zone_status` (projection); `putaway_rules` and `waves` behaviour as above; `GET /admin/inventory/zones/attention`.
- **Owned by order-svc:** `fulfilment_windows.fee` (nullable NUMERIC(18,4)), a per-business `slot_fee_settings` row (enabled, tax treatment, category, free_above, shopper-cancel rule, updated by/at), `orders.slot_fee`, `orders.slot_fee_vat`, `orders.slot_fee_treatment` (snapshot), `order_fee_refunds` (append-only, links the fee's refund to the payment refund).
- **Needs from other services:** the store's country, region and zone from tenant-svc's own store data (order-svc and inventory-svc through `TenantProfiles`); pricing-svc's tax categories and the apportioning rate lookup for the fee (REST, existing resolve style); payment-svc records the refund of the fee as part of the order's refund (the order's total carries it; `PaymentRefunded` is unchanged); purchase-svc posts the fee from `OrderPlaced`; reporting reads `slotFee` from the same event.
- **Events published (outbox, `eventId`, idempotent consumers):** `WorkingTimeBreachDetected`, `BroadcastAckOverdue` (`storeql.tenant.broadcast-ack-overdue`), `BroadcastPublished` / `BroadcastWithdrawn` gain `gate`, `ackBy`, audience (additive), `ZoneStatusChanged`, `TimeEntryCorrectionDecided` (tenant-svc; the requester is told), `OrderPlaced` gains `slotFee`, `slotFeeVat` (additive); order-svc's refund of a fee rides the existing cancellation and refund events.
- **Endpoints (new or changed):** `GET/PUT /admin/workforce/rules` (OWNER; `GET` for management: shows the law's rules with citations and the business's own), `GET /admin/workforce/shifts` (concerns carry severity and source), `POST …/publish` (`409 WORKFORCE_RULE_BREACH`), `PUT /admin/workforce/staff/{userId}/young-worker {until}` (`staff.manage`), `GET /admin/workforce/breaches` (management, store-scoped), `POST …/time-entries/{id}/adjust` (`403 WORKFORCE_SELF_ADJUST_REFUSED`; `202 {status: PENDING_APPROVAL, correctionId}` when it raises paid minutes and the business has approvals on), `GET /admin/workforce/corrections` and `POST …/{id}/decide` (via the approvals mechanism); `POST /admin/workforce/broadcasts` gains `ackBy`, `gate`; `POST …/broadcasts/{id}/stand-down {userId, reason}` (management at the store); `GET/PUT /admin/orders/slot-fees` (OWNER) and the windows' `fee`; inventory `GET /admin/inventory/zones/attention`; `PATCH …/zones/{id}/status` validates the vocabulary (`400 ZONE_STATUS_INVALID`).
- **Retryable writes (Idempotency-Key):** publish, adjust, decide, stand-down, the slot-fee settings, and checkout (exists, carries the fee).
- **New error codes:** `409 WORKFORCE_RULE_BREACH`, `400 WORKFORCE_RULE_LOOSER_THAN_LAW`, `403 WORKFORCE_SELF_ADJUST_REFUSED`, `409 WORKFORCE_CORRECTION_NOT_PENDING`, `409 BROADCAST_ACK_REQUIRED` (iam-svc), `400 BROADCAST_ACK_BY_INVALID`, `400 ZONE_STATUS_INVALID`, `409 INVENTORY_ZONE_RETIRED`, `409 SLOT_FEE_TAX_TREATMENT_REQUIRED`, `400 SLOT_FEE_INVALID`.
- **Settings, all off until set:** the business's own working-time rules and their action; the approvals switch for `staff.time-correction-up`; `slot_fees_enabled`, its tax treatment, `free_above`, the shopper-cancel rule; a notice's `gate` (per notice, default none).

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** the fee is in the business's home currency, per window; a basket in another currency shows it through `Fx` as display only and never charges in it. **Ledger postings:** the fee is Dr the tender / Cr delivery income, with its tax through the order's tax posting, dated at the sale; a refund reverses both; no postings for the working-time and zone parts.
- **Dates:** every instant UTC in storage; **every day, week and window is the store's local one** through its zone (a shift crossing midnight or a DST change is judged by elapsed time and by the local day it belongs to, as `Windows` already does for slots); `ack_by` is entered and shown in the store's zone and stored as an instant; law rules' effective dates are dates in the rule's own regime.
- **Plan limits:** none. The fee, the rules and the notices are not metered.

## Constraints

- **Golden rules:** 1 (inventory-svc, iam-svc and order-svc learn what they need from events and `TenantProfiles`, never joins); 3 (the tenant from the token; a shift, entry, zone or window is judged first by its tenant then its store); 6 and 7; 8 (`time_entries` supersede, corrections and stand-downs and refunds of fees are appended, `working_time_breaches` and the trails are append-only); 13 (`BigDecimal`, `NUMERIC(18,4)`); 14 (UTC stored, store zone shown).
- **Location-neutral:** the rule data is the only place a law's number lives, always with its citation; a country with no pack gets none, never a default; the roster's day is the store's; the fee's tax treatment is chosen by the business from its own categories; no currency is assumed.
- **Existing behaviour that changes:** a business in the EU regime keeps exactly today's flags (now cited from data and read in the store's zone, which can change a shift's "day" near midnight); a business **outside** the EU regime **stops being flagged** for an eleven-hour rest and a six-hour break it was never bound to (a behaviour change, stated: it was a defect against the location-neutral rule). `WorkforceIT.theRoster` (which asserts `DAILY_REST_SHORT`) moves to an EU-store fixture.
- **Existing tenants:** no business rules, no gates, no approvals, no fee until an owner sets them.
- **Flow guards:** unaffected unless a flow publishes a roster; checkout with the fee off is unchanged.
- **Personal data:** a young-worker date is the only new personal fact and is deleted with the person's profile and by tenant erasure (derived from the schema by the export/erase catalogue).

## Acceptance

- [ ] An EU-regime store's roster flags a short rest and a missing break from data with the directive's citation and reads the day in the store's zone; a store with no pack gets no legal flag; overlapping shifts flag everywhere — pure `WorkingTimeTest` (a UTC+13, a UTC−8 and a DST store; a shift over midnight), tenant-svc `WorkforceIT.theRoster` (EU fixture), `.noPackNoLegalFlag`
- [ ] Every row in `working_time_rules` has a citation and an effective date; a migration without one fails the build — `WorkingTimeRulesDataTest`
- [ ] A business rule looser than the law's is refused; a tighter or an unrelated one is saved; off until set — `WorkingTimeRulesIT.tightenOnly`
- [ ] Publishing across two stores of the business counts the rest between them; an `UNLAWFUL` law breach and a business `BLOCK` rule refuse `409 WORKFORCE_RULE_BREACH` with citations; an `ADVISORY` one publishes with its warnings kept — `WorkforceRulesIT.publishAcrossStores`, `.unlawfulRefused`, `.advisoryPublishesWithWarnings`
- [ ] A young worker's rules apply until the date and not after; the flag needs `staff.manage`; another business cannot read or set it — `YoungWorkerIT` (tenant-isolation line: rival owner and manager)
- [ ] A closed or corrected entry with a missed break or a short rest records a breach and announces it once per event; a clock-out is never refused — `WorkingTimeBreachIT`
- [ ] Nobody corrects or hand-clocks their own hours (`403 WORKFORCE_SELF_ADJUST_REFUSED`); `adjust` at another store is `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`; another business's entry is `404` — `TimeCorrectionIT.selfAndStore`
- [ ] With the approvals switch on, a correction that raises paid minutes waits, publishes no `LabourRecorded` until a different `staff.manage` holder approves, and then supersedes the entry; one that lowers or keeps minutes applies at once; the requester cannot approve — `TimeCorrectionIT.raisesWaitForASecondPerson`, `.lowerApplyAtOnce` (with the approvals mechanism)

## Screens

- **Back-office → Workforce → Rules:** the law's rules for the store's country with their citations (read-only), the business's own below them with **Warn** / **Block**, and a plain reason when a value is refused as looser than the law.
- **Back-office → Workforce → Roster:** concerns per shift in words with their source ("the law: <citation>" or "your rule"); **Publish** shows a refusal as a list, each with its rule; a young-worker mark on the person; times in the store's zone.
- **Back-office → Workforce → Hours:** **Correct** shows "waiting for a second person" when it raises hours; a **Corrections** tab for the approver; a person's own hours have no **Correct**.
- **Back-office → Notices:** the composer gets **Acknowledge by** and **Block sign-in until read**; the reach list shows who has not, with **Stand down** for a manager.
- **POS → Sign-in:** an unread gating notice shows first and the till opens when it is acknowledged.
- **Back-office → Stores → Zones:** the three statuses in words, and a note when a zone has stock or rules that need attention; **Inventory → Zones needing attention**.
- **Back-office → Fulfilment windows:** a **Fee** column when slot fees are on; **Settings → Slot fees** (switch, tax treatment picker from the business's categories, free above, refund rule) with the switch disabled until a treatment is chosen.
- **Storefront checkout:** the slot picker shows each window's fee, the basket shows the fee line and its tax, and the confirmation and receipt carry it.
- Words not codes (`status_labels.dart`), money through `AppFormat.money`, dates through `AppFormat`, adaptive per UI-GUIDE §7.2.
