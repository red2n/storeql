## Problem

StoreQL knows what a till holds and stops there. A cashier opens a session with a float they say they counted; a manager records "cash drops" that go nowhere; a session closes with a count. The cash that leaves the drawer at close, the float a manager hands out in the morning, the store safe, the bag sent to the bank, and the bank's credit are all outside the system, so the very moment money is most easily lost (between the till and the bank) is the one nobody can reconcile. Oracle's Xstore treats it as one chain in which every movement has two sides and a person: safe to till, till to safe, safe to a sealed bag, bag to the bank, bank confirms. There is also no recorded start to the day (the register is the unit, not the store) and no way to trade round the clock with a floats carried over. The ledger's `Cash in tills` account rises with every cash sale and nothing ever takes it down except a refund, so the books say a store is holding all the cash it ever took.

## Outcome

- **A store is opened and closed for trading as a recorded step**, by a named person, and a store that wants it can refuse to open a till before its day is open.
- **Cash is never nowhere.** Every note is in a till, in the safe, in a sealed bag or at the bank, and every move between two of these is one record with two sides, the amount, the person and, where the business asks, a witness.
- **A float is issued from the safe** and returned to it (or carried over in the till for the next shift), and a till's takings are put into the safe at close.
- **The safe is counted**, the count is compared with what the movements say it should hold, and a difference is explained by a person and, above the business's ceiling, agreed by a second.
- **A bank deposit is made up, sealed in a bag with a reference, banked, and confirmed against what the bank credited**, and the difference is shown, explained and posted.
- **The books follow the cash:** cash leaving the store for the bank comes off `Cash in tills`, sits in `Cash in transit` until the bank confirms, and only then is bank.
- **A store that trades 24 hours** chooses where its day rolls over and can carry a float across it.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **store manager** (opens and closes the day, issues floats, counts the safe, makes up and banks deposits), the **cashier** (opens their own till from the safe or a carried float), the **witness** (a second colleague present), the **finance user** (confirms deposits), the **owner** (settings, ceilings).
- **Channels:** back-office and POS (Cash screen) · Finance (Deposits).
- **Scope:** per store. A store with no safe behaves exactly as today. A manager held to stores acts only at theirs (`requireStoreAccess`, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`); the business's settings and a store's cut-off are business-wide or need the store's own manager as stated below.
- **Roles that can write:** the safe, its counts, pickups, transfers, deposits and the store day: OWNER, MANAGER holding `till.manage` (existing) at the store; a cashier only opens and closes their own till (which moves cash through the safe, below); confirming a deposit is OWNER or MANAGER at the store (no new permission) and, when the business asks, a person other than the sealer. A store's day cut-off: the store's manager or a business-wide manager. Settings: OWNER or a business-wide manager.
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same.

## Scope

- **In:** slices in build order.
  1. **The store day, its cut-off and 24/7 trading (tenant-svc, common-service, payment-svc).** tenant-svc's store gains `day_cutoff` (a local time, null until set: the day is then the store's calendar day) in the admin change log (`STORE_DAY_CUTOFF_SET`); `TenantProfiles.Stores.dayCutoff` reads it; [sales-audit](sales-audit.md)'s pure `BusinessDay.of(instant, zone, cutoff)` now honours it (an instant before the cut-off belongs to the previous date; a DST day is 23 or 25 hours). payment-svc gains `POST /admin/cash/store-days/open {storeId}` (records who and when; opening a day that is already open answers it, `200`), `POST …/{id}/close` and `GET /admin/cash/store-days/current?storeId`. Closing is refused while a session of the day is open (`409 STORE_DAY_TILLS_OPEN`, naming them) and, where the business sets `safe_count_at_close`, until the safe has been counted since the last movement (`409 STORE_DAY_SAFE_NOT_COUNTED`). A day nobody opened by hand still exists ([sales-audit](sales-audit.md) creates it on first activity) and closes by itself when its date has ended and no session of it is open, marked implicit. Business setting `store_day_required` (off until set): a session cannot open before its store's day is open (`409 STORE_DAY_NOT_OPEN`); a sale on a session already open is never refused. A store trading past its cut-off keeps the old day open until its last till closes, and the new day opens beside it (the old one is listed as a late close for the manager, never closed for them). `StoreDayOpened` and `StoreDayClosed` are announced.
  2. **The safe and the two-sided movement (payment-svc, iam-svc, purchase-svc).** A store has at most one active **safe** (`POST /admin/cash/safes {storeId, name, openingCount}`; the opening count is the first count). Every cash move is one **safe movement**, append-only: a `kind`, a from side and a to side (each a safe, a till session, a bank deposit or *outside* the store), currency, amount, who did it, an optional witness, a reason where the kind needs one, and an `Idempotency-Key`. The kinds: `FLOAT_ISSUE` (safe → session), `FLOAT_RETURN` (session → safe), `PICKUP` (session → safe, mid-shift), `TAKINGS_DEPOSIT` (session → safe, at close), `TILL_TRANSFER` (session → session, no safe), `CASH_IN` (outside → safe: a bank change order or a cash-in-transit delivery), `CASH_OUT` (safe → outside: a payment out of the safe, with a reason), `ADJUSTMENT` (a count difference, below), `DEPOSIT_SEALED` and `DEPOSIT_UNSEALED` (slice 3). **At a store with a safe**, opening a till session issues its float from the safe in the same transaction (`floatSource: SAFE`, refused `409 SAFE_INSUFFICIENT` when the safe holds less), or continues the float the same register's last session left (`CARRIED`, `409 FLOAT_NOT_CARRIED` when it did not) or, for a manager only, takes it from outside with a reason (`OUTSIDE`); and closing a till puts what it holds where the manager says: the takings to the safe, and the float either back (`floatDisposition: RETURN`) or left in the drawer for the next shift (`CARRY`), so a closed till's counted cash is fully accounted for by movements. A **cash drop** at a store with a safe writes its `PICKUP` movement on the same transaction; the drop stays the till's side of it. The session's expected cash ([till-sessions-and-registers](till-sessions-and-registers.md), `CashExpectation`) gains transfers in and out. Where the business sets `cash_movements_need_witness` (off until set), every movement except a float issue names a witness proved by the approvals step-up (`witnessToken`, action `till.cash-witness`, a person other than the mover, `409 STEPUP_SELF`, `403 STEPUP_INVALID`; missing is `400 CASH_WITNESS_REQUIRED`).
  3. **Counting the safe (payment-svc, purchase-svc).** `POST /admin/cash/safes/{id}/counts {kind: OPENING|CLOSE|SPOT, lines[{currency, amount}], witnessToken?}` is a **blind count**: the request carries no expected figure; the answer then shows what the movements said it should hold and the difference per currency. Counts are append-only. A difference is explained and put right by `POST …/counts/{id}/adjust {reason}`, which writes an `ADJUSTMENT` movement bringing the safe to the count; above the business's ceiling on the amount adjusted it needs a second person (approvals action `till.safe-adjustment`, new, below). The adjustment is announced (`SafeAdjusted`) and purchase-svc posts it (Dr `6530 Cash over and short` / Cr `1210 Cash in tills` for a shortfall, the reverse for an excess). A difference beyond the till tolerance raises the sales-audit finding `SAFE_COUNT_DIFFERS` on the day it was counted.
  4. **The bank deposit (payment-svc, purchase-svc).** A deposit is one bag for one store, made up from the safe by currency: `POST /admin/cash/deposits {storeId, lines[{currency, amount}]}` (`MADE_UP`, nothing moved yet); `POST …/{id}/seal {bagReference}` moves the money out of the safe (`DEPOSIT_SEALED`), the bag reference being unique for the business (`409 DEPOSIT_BAG_REFERENCE_USED`); `POST …/{id}/bank {carrier, slipReference}` records it handed to the bank or courier (`BANKED`); `POST …/{id}/confirm {lines[{currency, confirmedAmount}], reference?}` records what the bank credited (`CONFIRMED`), computes the difference per line, and a difference must then be explained (`POST …/{id}/resolve {reason}`, above the ceiling a second person, the same action `till.safe-adjustment`). A sealed bag that was never banked can be unsealed with a reason (money returns to the safe); nothing after `BANKED`. Where the business sets `separate_deposit_confirmer`, the person who confirms is not the one who sealed (`403 DEPOSIT_CONFIRMER_IS_SEALER`). `BankDepositBanked` and `BankDepositConfirmed` are announced. purchase-svc posts: at banked, Dr `1215 Cash in transit to bank` / Cr `1210 Cash in tills` at the deposit's home value (a foreign currency line at the business's rate on the day, through `Fx`); at confirmed, Dr `1200 Bank` for what was credited / Cr `1215` for what was expected, the home-currency difference to `6530` and a foreign-currency line's difference to `6540 Exchange differences` (all three accounts confirmed in purchase-svc while building, added if missing). A difference beyond the tolerance raises the sales-audit finding `DEPOSIT_DIFFERS`.
  5. **Spot counts of a till, the pickup prompt, alerts and screens (payment-svc, app).** `POST /admin/cash/till-sessions/{id}/counts` (management, blind, append-only, does not close or change the till) compares a mid-shift count with expected and, beyond the tolerance, raises the finding `SPOT_COUNT_DIFFERS`. A business setting `pickup_prompt_above` (a home-currency amount, off until set) makes the cashier's own session answer `pickupDue: true` when its expected cash passes it (never the amount, never the figure to a cashier) so the till asks for a manager to make a pickup. Alert metrics and screens below.
- **Born with its checks** (each is also an Acceptance line):
  - *Who and where:* `till.manage` at the store for every safe, count, transfer and deposit; a cashier only their own float and close; a store-held caller only theirs.
  - *Authority:* `till.safe-adjustment` (a second person above the business's ceiling on any adjustment or accepted difference) and `till.cash-witness` (the second person present, where the business asks); both added to the approvals catalogue; neither has a default figure.
  - *Retry safety:* an `Idempotency-Key` on every movement, count, deposit step and store-day step; a retry answers the first and moves nothing twice.
  - *Isolation:* another business's staff of every role, naming our safe, deposit, session or store id, get `404` and nothing moves; a manager held to another store `403`; a shopper `403`.
  - *The record:* every movement has a from, a to, a person and a time and is never edited; counts, deposit steps and day events are append-only and appear on the audit trail.
  - *Abuse:* `safe_adjustments.value` and `deposits.difference_value` on the alerts page; a person cannot witness their own movement.
  - *Refusals:* stable codes below.
  - *Location-neutral:* the day, its cut-off and every time are the store's zone; every currency and amount is the business's; no note or coin denomination, no bank, no carrier, no bag format is assumed.
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **Matching a deposit to a bank statement feed.** Confirmation is a person entering what the bank credited (from a slip or their online banking). A feed needs a bank connector (a driver behind an interface with a `SIMULATED` driver, as [docs/ACCOUNTING-CONNECTORS.md](../docs/ACCOUNTING-CONNECTORS.md) describes) and the credentials that come with it; it is its own feature and reads the `bank_deposits` this page writes.
  - **Cash-in-transit companies' own portals and smart safes.** A carrier is text on the deposit; a smart safe's device feed is a hardware integration.
  - **Note and coin denominations.** A count is an amount per currency; denominations are a business habit, and any currency has its own.
  - **A separate acknowledgement step for each float** (cashier signs for it). The person opening the till is the recipient; a witness covers the second person where the business wants one.
  - **Networked cash drawers shared between registers** (Oracle ch. 54): one session per register stays ([till-sessions-and-registers](till-sessions-and-registers.md)); a hand-over is a close and an open.
  - **Promotions refresh at store open.** A pricing matter with no cash in it.
  - **Cash over and short posting at the till close.** It is posted at the day's release ([sales-audit](sales-audit.md)); this page posts only the safe's and the deposit's differences, which nothing else covers.

- **Owned by payment-svc:** `store_days` gains `opened_by`, `opened_at`, `closed_by`, `implicit`, `late` and keeps `store_day_events` (shared with [sales-audit](sales-audit.md)); `safes` (store, name, status ACTIVE|RETIRED; one active per store); `safe_movements` (append-only, as above; unique `(tenant, idempotency_key)`); `safe_counts` and `safe_count_lines` (append-only: kind, currency, counted, expected, difference, by, witness, at); `bank_deposits` (status, bag reference unique per tenant, made-up, sealed, banked and confirmed by and at, carrier, slip reference, bank reference) with append-only `bank_deposit_events` and `bank_deposit_lines` (currency, expected, confirmed, difference; `confirmed` and `difference` written once at confirm); `till_counts` (append-only); `till_sessions` gains `float_source`, `float_disposition`, `carried_amount`; `cash_drops` gains `safe_movement_id`; `cash_settings` per business (`store_day_required`, `safe_count_at_close`, `cash_movements_need_witness`, `separate_deposit_confirmer`, `pickup_prompt_above`; all off or null until set). - **Owned by purchase-svc:** the new accounts (`1215`, `6530`, `6540`) and posting sources `SAFE_ADJUSTMENT`, `BANK_DEPOSIT_BANKED`, `BANK_DEPOSIT_CONFIRMED`; consumers of `SafeAdjusted`, `BankDepositBanked`, `BankDepositConfirmed`, idempotent on the event id, event-fed and so redated (never refused) in a closed month.
- **Needs from other services:** the store's zone and cut-off (tenant-svc, cached); the witness proof and the approver (iam-svc step-up, `POST /auth/step-up/consume`, breaker and timeout, unreachable means refused); rates through `FxRates` and `Fx` for a foreign line's home value (no rate: the line is posted at the amount the bank credited and the deposit's expected home value is reported as unknown, never guessed).
- **Events published:** `StoreDayOpened`, `StoreDayClosed`, `SafeAdjusted`, `BankDepositBanked`, `BankDepositConfirmed` (topics `storeql.payment.<kebab>`, each with `eventId` last and store, business date, currency, amounts as fields; no names). reporting-svc may read them; nothing else consumes the day events but [sales-audit](sales-audit.md).
- **Endpoints (payment-svc, `/admin/cash/…`):** store days (open, close, current, list); `POST/GET /admin/cash/safes`, `…/{id}` (balance and movements, cursor-paged), `POST …/{id}/movements` (`PICKUP`, `TILL_TRANSFER`, `CASH_IN`, `CASH_OUT`), `…/counts`, `…/counts/{id}/adjust`; deposits (make up, seal, bank, confirm, resolve, unseal, list, one); till spot counts; `GET/PUT /admin/cash/settings` (OWNER or business-wide manager). tenant-svc: `PATCH /admin/stores/{id}` takes `dayCutoff`. `POST /admin/cash/till-sessions` takes `floatSource`; the close takes `floatDisposition`.

- **New error codes:** `409 STORE_DAY_NOT_OPEN`, `409 STORE_DAY_TILLS_OPEN`, `409 STORE_DAY_SAFE_NOT_COUNTED`, `404 STORE_DAY_NOT_FOUND`, `404 SAFE_NOT_FOUND`, `409 SAFE_EXISTS`, `409 SAFE_INSUFFICIENT`, `400 CASH_WITNESS_REQUIRED`, `400 CASH_REASON_REQUIRED`, `409 FLOAT_NOT_CARRIED`, `400 FLOAT_SOURCE_INVALID`, `400 DEPOSIT_LINES_INVALID`, `409 DEPOSIT_BAG_REFERENCE_USED`, `409 DEPOSIT_NOT_IN_STATE` (a step out of order or after banked), `409 DEPOSIT_DIFFERENCE_OPEN`, `403 DEPOSIT_CONFIRMER_IS_SEALER`, `404 DEPOSIT_NOT_FOUND`, `400 STORE_DAY_CUTOFF_INVALID` (tenant-svc); existing `409 STEPUP_SELF`, `403 STEPUP_INVALID`, `403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`, `403 AUTHORITY_EXCEEDED` and approvals' `202`.

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** each safe line, count and deposit line is in its own currency; nothing is netted across currencies. Home value only where the ledger needs it, through `Fx` at the business's own rate; `NUMERIC(14,4)`, `BigDecimal`. See [till-tenders-foreign-cash-and-accounts](till-tenders-foreign-cash-and-accounts.md) for how foreign cash gets to a drawer.
- **Ledger postings:** slice 3 (Dr/Cr `6530`, `1210`) and slice 4 (Dr `1215` / Cr `1210` at banked; Dr `1200` / Cr `1215` with the difference at confirmed), as above. Cash sales still post Dr `1210` as today; nothing already posted changes.
- **Dates:** UTC instants; the store's local day and cut-off for a business date; a bag sealed before the cut-off and banked after keeps its own dates.
- **Plan limits:** none.

## Constraints

- **Golden rules:** 1 (the cut-off reaches payment-svc and purchase-svc by cached read, and the ledger by events); 3; 6 and 7 (announcements through the outbox, postings idempotent on the event id); 8 (movements, counts, deposit steps and events are append-only; a mistake is a reversing movement, an unseal or an adjustment, never an edit); 11; 13; 14.
- **Existing stores:** a store with no safe and no cut-off behaves exactly as today: floats are typed, drops go nowhere, closes are counted, no day step is required. The first safe changes only that store, and only from its creation forward. `store_day_required` off means no session is ever refused for a day.
- **Never strand a sale:** a settings read or the step-up service being unreachable can refuse a *movement* (fails closed, as approvals do for money) but never a sale, a tender or a refund.
- **Conservation:** for a closed session at a store with a safe, counted cash equals the takings deposited plus the float returned or carried, and the safe's balance equals the sum of its movements; both are proved by tests, so cash cannot be created or lost by a bug.
- **Flow guards:** `flow-guard-comprehensive` and `flow-guard-runtime` open and close tills at stores with no safe; they must stay green unchanged.
- **Backups:** plain tables, no function in a CHECK.

## Screens

- **POS → Open till:** a float source (from the safe, carried, outside for a manager) and the amount; the till never shows the safe's balance to a cashier. The cashier's till shows a *Call a manager for a pickup* banner when `pickupDue`.
- **Admin shell → Stores → Cash (per store):** the day (Open / Close, late close warning), the safe balance per currency with recent movements, **Pick up**, **Transfer**, **Cash in**, **Cash out**, **Count the safe** (blind: enter amounts, then see the difference and **Explain**), and a *waiting for approval* state above the ceiling.
- **Admin shell → Finance → Deposits:** a list by store and status; a deposit's steps as a stepper (Made up, Sealed with a bag reference, Banked with carrier and slip, Confirmed with what the bank credited), the difference and its explanation.
- **Admin shell → Settings → Cash:** the day-required, safe-count-at-close, witness, separate-confirmer and pickup settings; **Stores → a store:** the day's cut-off.
