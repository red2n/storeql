## Problem

A grocer's margin is made in the supplier's price list and the supplier's deals, and the platform holds neither. The price on an order line is whatever the buyer typed, or whatever was paid last time. So:

- **There is no agreed cost.** Nothing says "this supplier sells us this product at this price from this date". A price rise the supplier announced for the first of next month is in someone's inbox, and orders raised in between are priced by memory.
- **A cost change has no date.** The only way to change what the next order costs is to type another number, which leaves no record of what the price was, who changed it or when it began.
- **Deals live in spreadsheets.** An allowance taken off the invoice, a rebate earned on volume and claimed at the quarter's end, a fixed sum for putting a product in the leaflet: none reaches the order, the cost of the stock or the books. Money earned and not yet received is invisible, so margin is understated and a claim nobody made is money lost.
- **A commitment is untracked.** A business that agrees to buy a quantity or value over a year at a price has no view of how much it has drawn, so it either falls short of the commitment or buys past it at the wrong price.

## Outcome

- **The buyer keeps an agreed cost per product and supplier, with dates.** A change is scheduled ahead; it never edits a cost that orders were raised under. The cost in force on any day is one answer.
- **A new order line is priced from it,** and shows the buyer the net cost: the agreed cost, less any allowance taken off the invoice, with each part named. A draft order that a later cost now overtakes says so.
- **A contract records a committed quantity or value over a period at a price,** and every order raised under it draws it down. The buyer sees committed, ordered, received, remaining and where the period is heading.
- **Deals are recorded as the supplier agreed them:** an off-invoice allowance (cheaper on the invoice), a bill-back or rebate earned on volume and claimed afterwards, a fixed payment for a promotion.
- **What is earned and not yet received is on the books.** An accrual is posted as the goods arrive, the stock's cost falls by what still sits on the shelf, and the rest is a credit to cost of sales. The claim goes to the supplier, the settlement is recorded, and a shortfall is written off by a person with the authority.
- **Nothing is guessed.** No supplier, deal or cost exists until a person records it; a business without any reads exactly as today.

## Who and where

- **Personas** ([PRD §2](../PRD.md)): the **buyer** (owner, manager, storekeeper) ordering, the **buyer in management** who agrees costs and deals, the **finance user** who claims and settles, the **owner** who approves what needs a second person.
- **Channels:** back-office. Supplier costs and deals are **recorded by the buyer** from what the supplier sent, the way RFQ quotes and acknowledgements are; a supplier portal is not part of this.
- **Scope:** per business (a supplier is per business). A cost, contract or deal may name stores it applies to; none named means every store. A cost list is business-wide.
- **Roles that can write:**
  - Costs, contracts, deals, claims and settlements: OWNER and MANAGER (`PLATFORM_ADMIN` as elsewhere) holding the new permission `purchasing.costs`; a manager narrowed out of it is refused `403 PERMISSION_DENIED` naming it, as `finance.journal` is.
  - **Store-held callers:** a manager held to stores writes only a contract or deal whose named stores are all theirs (`403 STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`); a cost list and a deal that names no store are business-wide, which needs a caller held to none (`403 BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY`). They read what touches their stores.
  - STOREKEEPER and above read costs, contracts and the net cost while ordering; a cashier cannot (`403`).
- **Sandbox tenant:** behaves the same; nothing leaves the platform.

## Scope

- **In** (slices, in build order; each is built and tested alone):
  1. **The agreed cost, dated (purchase-svc).** Append-only `supplier_costs`: supplier, variant, unit cost (an amount and its currency, the supplier's), the unit it is quoted per (the order line's own unit; a pack conversion is out), `effective_from` (a date in the business's zone), an optional `effective_to`, a `source` (KEYED, RFQ, CONTRACT), and the person and instant. **A change is a new row** dated from its day; the row it overtakes is closed at that day in a further row, never edited. A future row can be **withdrawn** by another row (kind WITHDRAWN) until its day arrives; after that it is history. Pure `SupplierCosts.effective(asOf)` answers the one cost in force (the latest `effective_from` on or before the day, not withdrawn, not ended). **Ordering:** an order line added with no price is priced from the cost in force on the order's expected delivery day, else on the day it is raised; the line **stamps** `cost_id` and `cost_source` (COST_LIST, CONTRACT, DEAL, MANUAL). A typed price is still allowed (MANUAL, as today) and is recorded as such, and a manual price above the cost in force by more than the business's own tolerance is a warning (below), never a refusal. The proposal engine reads the cost in force before the last paid price, which stays its fallback. **The order price never moves after the line is written:** a DRAFT order whose line is now overtaken by a later cost carries `warnings: ["COST_CHANGED"]` and the buyer re-prices by hand (the existing line amend); an order that has left DRAFT is never touched, because the price it was approved and matched at is the price it was raised under. An RFQ award may offer `saveQuotedPricesAsCost` with a start date, which writes KEYED-like rows of source RFQ; it is off unless asked. A raise beyond the business's tolerance is the approvals action `purchasing.cost-change`. `SupplierCostChanged` is announced (see events).
  2. **Off-invoice allowance (purchase-svc).** `supplier_deals` and `supplier_deal_lines` (variants; none listed means every product of the supplier), kind `OFF_INVOICE`, a period (`starts_on`, `ends_on`, both required), a per-unit amount or a percentage of the cost, optionally the stores. Status DRAFT, ACTIVE, ENDED, CANCELLED; a deal is activated by a person and **once active its terms are never edited: a change ends it and starts another** (append-only lines). An order line priced on a day inside an active deal stamps `list_price`, `allowance_per_unit`, `deal_id`, and its `unit_price` is the **net** (list less allowance, rounded to the currency's minor units, never below zero): that is what the supplier will invoice, so the three-way match compares like with like and no variance appears. The line and the order screen show the breakdown. Nothing is posted: the invoice is simply cheaper, and the stock is booked at the net price as today.
  3. **Bill-back and rebate earned (purchase-svc, inventory-svc).** Kind `BILL_BACK`: the supplier invoices at list, and pays an amount later. Terms: per-unit amount or percentage of cost, **optionally tiered on volume** (`deal_tiers`: from a quantity or value in the period, a rate), the period, whether a reached tier applies to the whole period's volume (`retroactive`, the usual reading) or only above the threshold. **Earned as goods arrive:** on each goods receipt purchase-svc's own receipt line yields an append-only `deal_earnings` row (deal, receipt line, variant, quantity, amount, currency, the tier applied). For a tiered deal, when a tier is crossed the earnings so far are re-stated by a **catch-up row** (positive or negative, naming the tier), never an edit; pure `DealAccrual.earn` and `DealAccrual.catchUp` decide, both unit-tested. **Cost and books, by accounting practice** (a rebate is a reduction of the cost of what was bought, so it follows the units: what is still on the shelf is stock at a lower cost, what has been sold is cost of sales that was too high): purchase-svc posts the whole earning as **Dr Supplier Rebates Receivable / Cr Purchase Rebates**, and announces `SupplierDealEarned`; **inventory-svc, which owns cost,** lowers the unit cost of the receipt's batches still on hand by the per-unit earning through `revalueReceiptOnce` (the AVERAGE pool moves by the on-hand share, as landed cost's does) and answers `SupplierDealCapitalised` naming, per line, how much it moved into stock; purchase-svc then re-posts that part **Dr Purchase Rebates / Cr Stock**, so the rebate stays in cost of sales only for what was sold. A reversal of the receipt or its earning is the mirror. The earning is dated the receipt's day and takes the redate rule of a closed month.
  4. **Fixed payment for a promotion (purchase-svc).** Kind `FIXED`: a sum for a period, tied to nothing that is bought, optionally naming a pricing-svc promotion by id only. It is **earned over the period, one day at a time,** by a sweep that writes one `deal_earnings` row per deal and day (unique on the pair, so a rerun writes nothing twice), posted **Dr Supplier Rebates Receivable / Cr Supplier Promotional Funding** (income, never inventory cost, because it pays for a service and does not reduce the price of goods). Ending the deal early stops the days; earnings already made stand.
  5. **Claim and settle (purchase-svc).** `deal_claims`: a period and supplier, the earnings gathered (each earning is in one claim at most, held by a unique pair like consignment settlements), the amount claimed and its currency, status DRAFT, SENT, PART_SETTLED, SETTLED, DISPUTED, WRITTEN_OFF. A claim is **recorded as sent by the buyer** (how, when, the reference). A settlement is recorded (`deal_settlements`, append-only): by **supplier credit note** (a document number; posts Dr Trade Creditors / Cr Supplier Rebates Receivable, which lowers what the business owes the supplier without any payment-run feature), by **money received** (Dr Bank / Cr Receivable), and the difference to the claim is a **shortfall**. A shortfall is written off, or disputed and left open, by a person; a write-off is **Dr Purchase Rebates (or Promotional Funding) / Cr Supplier Rebates Receivable** and needs the approvals action `purchasing.deal-writeoff` above the business's ceiling. An accrual with no claim after the deal ended is listed as *earned, not yet claimed* so it is chased, never lost.
  6. **Contracts and drawdown (purchase-svc).** `supplier_contracts` and lines: supplier, period, a commitment in QUANTITY (per variant) or VALUE (one figure in a currency), an agreed price per variant (which becomes a cost row of source CONTRACT for the period, so slice 1's resolution finds it), status DRAFT, ACTIVE, ENDED, CANCELLED. An order line priced from a contract stamps `contract_id`; **drawdown is append-only `contract_draws`** (a draw when the order is submitted, a reversing draw when it is cancelled or rejected, a received figure as receipts arrive), so ordered, received, remaining and **projected shortfall at the period's end** (a straight run-rate over the days gone, shown as a projection and never a promise) are read, not stored. An order that would take the contract past its commitment carries the warning `CONTRACT_EXCEEDED`; an order that draws it is never refused for that. A contract is signed off under the approvals action `purchasing.contract` (the committed value, home currency).
  7. **Net cost and margin (purchase-svc read, reporting-svc).** `GET /admin/purchasing/net-cost?variantId&supplierId&asOf`: list cost, allowance, earned-not-yet-claimed rebates, landed charges already applied, and the resulting **net cost**, each labelled. reporting-svc reads `SupplierCostChanged` and `SupplierDealCapitalised` for a *margin outlook* (products whose scheduled cost rise falls below the current price less current cost); pricing-svc holds no cost and is not told (a repricing rule is its own page).
  8. **The screens** (below) and the notification: the buyer is reminded when a scheduled cost is a week away or a deal ends with earnings unclaimed (notification-svc `SUPPLIER_DEAL_UNCLAIMED`, the lead time the business's own setting, off until set).
- **Out, on purpose:**
  - **A supplier portal or supplier-written prices.** A supplier writes nothing; the buyer records what the supplier sent, and the record says who.
  - **Editing a cost or a deal that orders were raised under.** Both are append-only; a change is a new dated row.
  - **Repricing orders that are already submitted.** The price approved and matched is the price the order stands at.
  - **Enforcing a commitment.** No penalty, take-or-pay charge or refusal to order over it; the platform shows the drawdown and warns.
  - **Deals that change what the shopper pays,** and **promotions.** That is pricing-svc; a FIXED deal may name a promotion by id only.
  - **Pack and case conversions of a quoted cost, and price breaks by order size.** A cost is per the order line's unit; break-quantity pricing is a tier only on a BILL_BACK deal.
  - **Standard-cost accounting and its variances.** Stock is booked at the order's net price; the variance between what was invoiced and what was ordered is [purchase-price-variance](purchase-price-variance.md).
  - **A supplier's own tax point or currency rules.** Costs are in the supplier's currency and translated at the business's rate through `FxRates`, failing closed as any spend figure does.
  - **Automatic offset of a claim in a payment run.** A credit note lowers what is owed through the ledger already; netting inside a run belongs with the payment-run ceilings on [approvals](approvals.md).

## Data and flow

- **Owned by** purchase-svc: `supplier_costs` (append-only), `supplier_deals`, `supplier_deal_lines`, `supplier_deal_tiers` (lines and tiers append-only once the deal is ACTIVE), `deal_earnings` (append-only), `deal_claims`, `deal_claim_earnings` (the pair, unique on the earning), `deal_settlements` (append-only), `supplier_contracts`, `supplier_contract_lines`, `contract_draws` (append-only); `purchase_order_lines` gain `cost_id`, `cost_source`, `list_price`, `allowance_per_unit`, `deal_id`, `contract_id`. Every table has `tenant_id` first in its index and a `CHECK` that ids are v7. A **chart-of-accounts seed** adds *Supplier Rebates Receivable* (asset), *Purchase Rebates* (a credit to cost of sales) and *Supplier Promotional Funding* (income), codes chosen with the wave-2 seed so none collides.
- **Owned by** inventory-svc: nothing new except a handler; the existing `batch_cost_adjustments` records each change with `source_type = SUPPLIER_DEAL`.
- **Needs from other services:** the business's home currency and rates (`TenantProfiles`, `FxRates`); stores' existence and zones (`TenantProfiles.stores`, never joined); inventory-svc's answer to the capitalisation (an event, below); pricing-svc's promotion id is stored and never read.
- **Events published** (through the outbox; consumers idempotent on `eventId`):
  - `SupplierCostChanged` (`storeql.purchase.supplier-cost-changed`): `eventId`, `tenantId`, `supplierId`, `variantId`, `unitCost`, `currency`, `effectiveFrom`, `previousCost`, `occurredAt`. Consumers: reporting-svc (margin outlook); notification-svc (the buyer's reminder).
  - `SupplierDealEarned` (`storeql.purchase.supplier-deal-earned`): `eventId`, `tenantId`, `dealId`, `earningId`, `kind`, `lines: [{grId, storeId, variantId, qty, perUnit, amount}]`, `currency`, `reversal` (boolean), `occurredAt`. Consumer: inventory-svc, which lowers (or on a reversal raises) the unit cost of the receipt's batches on hand and answers with the next event. A `FIXED` deal's earning carries no lines and is not sent (nothing of it reaches stock).
  - `SupplierDealCapitalised` (inventory-svc, `storeql.inventory.supplier-deal-capitalised`): `eventId`, `tenantId`, `earningId`, `lines: [{variantId, capitalisedAmount, soldAmount}]`, `occurredAt`. Consumer: purchase-svc posts the capitalised part **Dr Purchase Rebates / Cr Stock**, once per event.
  - A receipt inventory has not booked yet answers 503 and is redelivered, exactly as `LandedCostHandler` does.
- **Endpoints** (purchase-svc; all `Idempotency-Key` on writes; cursor pagination): `GET/POST /supplier-costs` and `POST /supplier-costs/{id}/withdraw`, `GET /supplier-costs/in-force?variantId&supplierId&asOf`; `GET/POST /supplier-deals`, `POST /supplier-deals/{id}/activate|end|cancel`, `GET /supplier-deals/{id}/earnings`; `GET/POST /deal-claims`, `POST /deal-claims/{id}/sent|settle|dispute|write-off`; `GET/POST /supplier-contracts`, `POST .../activate|end|cancel`, `GET /supplier-contracts/{id}/drawdown`; `GET /admin/purchasing/net-cost`; `GET /admin/purchasing/deals/unclaimed`.
- **Retryable writes** (Idempotency-Key): every POST above; a retry answers with the first.
- **New error codes:** `PURCHASE_COST_INVALID` 400 (not positive, no currency, a date in a form the store cannot read); `PURCHASE_COST_BACKDATED` 409 (a change dated before the day it is recorded, unless the person holds `purchasing.costs` and says why: it is kept as `backdated` and never touches an order already raised); `PURCHASE_COST_NOT_FOUND` 404; `PURCHASE_COST_ALREADY_EFFECTIVE` 409 (withdrawing a cost whose day has come); `PURCHASE_COST_NEEDS_APPROVAL` 403 (`purchasing.cost-change`, with the shared authority `details`); `PURCHASE_DEAL_INVALID` 400; `PURCHASE_DEAL_PERIOD_INVALID` 400 (ends before it starts); `PURCHASE_DEAL_OVERLAP` 409 (two active deals of one kind on one supplier-variant-store for a day: **the deeper allowance does not silently win; a person ends one**); `PURCHASE_DEAL_NOT_DRAFT` 409 (editing terms of an active deal); `PURCHASE_DEAL_NOT_FOUND` 404; `PURCHASE_DEAL_TIERS_INVALID` 400 (tiers not ascending); `PURCHASE_CLAIM_NOTHING_EARNED` 409; `PURCHASE_CLAIM_EARNING_CLAIMED` 409; `PURCHASE_CLAIM_STATE` 409; `PURCHASE_CLAIM_WRITEOFF_NEEDS_APPROVAL` 403 (`purchasing.deal-writeoff`); `PURCHASE_CLAIM_CURRENCY_MISMATCH` 400; `PURCHASE_CONTRACT_INVALID` 400; `PURCHASE_CONTRACT_NOT_ACTIVE` 409; `PURCHASE_CONTRACT_NEEDS_APPROVAL` 403 (`purchasing.contract`); `PURCHASE_CONTRACT_SUPPLIER_MISMATCH` 400 (a line naming another supplier's contract); `PURCHASE_FX_RATE_MISSING` 422 (a foreign-currency ceiling with no rate fails closed, as elsewhere).

## Money, time and limits

- **Currency:** a cost, a contract and an order are in the supplier's currency; a deal is in the supplier's currency too, so its earning matches what the supplier will pay. Translation to the business's home currency (ceilings, reports, postings of a foreign-currency order) is through `FxRates` and `Fx`, rounded to the target's minor units; no rate means the report shows the figure untranslated and the ceiling **fails closed**. No currency is assumed.
- **Ledger postings** (each dated the document's day, a closed month redating as [accounting-periods](accounting-periods.md) says, none refused because they come from events):
  - OFF_INVOICE: none of its own (the invoice is at net).
  - BILL_BACK and any volume rebate earned: Dr Supplier Rebates Receivable / Cr Purchase Rebates; then, on `SupplierDealCapitalised`, Dr Purchase Rebates / Cr Stock for what is on the shelf.
  - FIXED: Dr Supplier Rebates Receivable / Cr Supplier Promotional Funding, day by day.
  - Settlement by credit note: Dr Trade Creditors / Cr Supplier Rebates Receivable; by money: Dr Bank / Cr Supplier Rebates Receivable; write-off: Dr Purchase Rebates or Promotional Funding / Cr Supplier Rebates Receivable.
  - **VAT:** a rebate or allowance credit note carries its own VAT as the supplier states it; the receipt of the credit note is booked through the existing supplier-credit-note path (input VAT reduced). An earning is booked **net**; the platform derives no tax on it.
- **Dates:** a cost's, a deal's and a contract's days are dates in the business's own zone (`TenantProfiles`); recorded instants are UTC. A month is never assumed.
- **Settings** (owner: purchase-svc, unset = off): `purchasing.cost-change.tolerance-percent` (the rise over the cost in force that a manual price or a scheduled raise may reach before a warning, and before `purchasing.cost-change` is asked), `purchasing.deal.unclaimed-notice-days`. The platform names no number.
- **Plan limits:** none.

## Constraints

- **Existing tenants:** no cost, deal or contract exists, so an order line priced by hand is `MANUAL` and exactly as before, the proposal engine's last-paid fallback is untouched, and no posting appears (`SupplierDealsCompatibilityIT`).
- **Golden rule 1:** inventory-svc's cost is changed only by its own handler, on an event; purchase-svc never reads or writes a batch. The event round trip is the rule 6 seam.
- **Golden rule 8:** costs, earnings, draws, settlements and the claim-earning pairs are append-only; a correction is a new row. Only a claim's and a deal's `status` move, each with an event row in the history.
- **A consumer is never refused by a closed month:** earnings and capitalisation redate.
- **Tenant from the JWT;** a supplier, a variant, a store or a promotion id in a request is checked to belong to the business (`404` otherwise, never a foreign row).
- **The platform never derives a rebate rate,** a tier or a tolerance: each is the business's own number.

## Open questions

- [x] Does a cost change ever alter an order already raised? → **No: an order's price is stamped when the line is written; a later cost warns a draft and never touches a submitted order** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
- [x] What does an off-invoice allowance do to the order? → **It lowers the line's price to the net the supplier will invoice, with list and allowance kept beside it, so the match has nothing to flag** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
- [x] What does a rebate do to inventory cost? → **It lowers the cost of what is still on hand and is a credit to cost of sales for what was sold; inventory-svc decides the split and purchase-svc posts it** (industry standard, IAS 2 on consideration from a vendor, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
- [x] Is a fixed promotional payment inventory cost? → **No: it is income earned over the promotion, because it pays for a service and does not price the goods** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
- [x] When is a volume rebate earned, and does a reached tier reach back? → **As the goods arrive, at the tier reached so far; a crossing writes a catch-up row; the deal says whether the tier is retroactive** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
- [x] What if two deals cover the same product on the same day? → **The platform refuses the overlap and a person ends one; it never picks the better one silently** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
- [x] Is a commitment enforced? → **No: it is measured and warned; the business decides** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
- [x] Who may agree costs, deals and contracts? → **Management with `purchasing.costs`; a second person above the business's own tolerance or committed value, through the approvals page** (industry standard, under the user's standing instruction of 2026-09-30).
